NOTIFICATION 
No. A/Re-appeals/19/70 
May 10, 2019 
 
Undergraduate and Postgraduate students of IIT Kanpur terminated from the 
Institute due to inadequate academic performance or other academic reasons can 
submit an appeal or a re-appeal for reinstatement in their respective programmes 
subject to following conditions: 

Appeals: 

1) A terminated student can appeal for reinstatement in his/her academic 
programme just after the termination. Such appeals, if submitted within 
the stipulated time frame, may be admitted for consideration in the same 
semester for reinstatement. 

2) If the appeal is submitted beyond the stipulated time frame, it is admitted 
for consideration in the subsequent semester. 

Re-appeals: 

1) If an appeal or a subsequent re-appeal has been turned down by the 
Senate, the concerned student may re-appeal after a minimum period of 
one semester from the date of the turning down of the earlier appeal/re- 
appeal. Students are advised to cite in the re-appeal the activities, including 
academic activities, pursued by them after the termination and/or the 
previous appeal. 

2) It may be noted that: 

a) Re-appeals are received at the DOAA office throughout the year. 

b) Re-appeals are admitted for further consideration once at the end of 
every regular semester. 

c) Re-appeals received by mid-June are admitted for consideration for 
the semester beginning in July and the re-appeals received by mid- 
November are admitted for consideration for the semester beginning 
in January.
