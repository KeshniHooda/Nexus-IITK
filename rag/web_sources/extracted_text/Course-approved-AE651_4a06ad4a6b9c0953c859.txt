Course Title: System Identification Techniques for Aerial Vehicles 
Course Number: 
Credits: 3-0-0-0 [9] 
Pre-requisite: AE647A or equivalent 
Who can take the course: Masters and Ph.D. Students 
Proposer & Instructor: Dr. Subrahmanyam Saderla 
Interested Second Instructor: Dr. Raghavendra P Kukillaya 
6 Sl 
Course Objective: The objective of the course is to familiarize students with existing 
system identification techniques applied to flight vehicles. The focus will be on the 
applicability of various estimation techniques in different flight conditions and the 
quality of sensor data. This course will be beneficial for comprehensive analysis of flight 
vehicle's performance, stability, and control.
