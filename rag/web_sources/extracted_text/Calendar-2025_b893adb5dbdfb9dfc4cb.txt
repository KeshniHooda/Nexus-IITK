Notes: 
1) If the requisite number of lectures cannot be scheduled, the instructors may, in consultation 
with the students in their respective courses, decide on a schedule of extra classes. The requisite 
number of lectures in a full semester course is 39 lectures of 50 minutes each for 3- lectures a 
week schedule. 
 
2) There will be no extra classes on Gazetted Holidays. 
 
3) No class, quiz, presentation or any other academic activity will be scheduled on February 20, 
April 24-25, 2025, June 15, 2025, July 12-13, 2025, September 15, 2025, and November 15-16, 
2025. 
 
4) Saturdays and Sundays falling within the examination period will be used for scheduling 
examinations. Under extraordinary circumstances, examinations of some of the courses may be 
scheduled on the days preceding/following the mid semester/end semester examinations. 
 
5) All make-up examinations permitted by the SUGC/SPGC will be conducted by the concerned 
instructors in -charge as per the schedule detailed in the Academic Calendar.
