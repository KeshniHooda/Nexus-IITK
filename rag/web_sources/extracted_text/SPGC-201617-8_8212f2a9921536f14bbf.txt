Item 29: To consider the request of Mr. Abhishek Singh to be allowed to register in the M.Tech. 
programme in the Dept. of Electrical Engineering and to resubmit his M.Tech. thesis. Mr. 
Abhishek Singh (13104006) was a student in the Dept. of Electrical Engineering, who 
received his M.Tech. degree in 2016. His M.Tech. degree was, however, revoked by the 
Board of Governors, IIT Kanpur on grounds of plagiarism. The case was considered in the 
seventh SPGC meeting held March 24, 2017, and the SPGC had asked for further inputs 
from the department on the case. 
 
 The SPGC considered the request of Mr. Abishek Singh to be allowed to resubmit his thesis. 
After detailed discussion, it was decided that since Mr. Singh’s status is unclear, the matter be 
referred to the Senate for guidance on the following issue: Is the applicant required to be 
readmitted to the M.Tech. programme in Electrical Engineering in order to resubmit his thesis, or 
should he be allowed to register in the M.Tech. programme as a special case even though he is 
not a student of the Institute?

Item 32: Any other item with the permission of the Chair. 
 
(a) 
 The SPGC discussed the case of Mr. Pawan Kumar (15101037) M.Tech. student, Dept. of 
Aerospace Engineering, who had registered for two online courses (from outside IIT Kanpur) 
without prior information of the thesis supervisor. After discussion, it was concluded that 
registering for online courses outside the Institute does not require a prior permission of the 
SPGC though the thesis supervisor should have been consulted beforehand. However, SPGC 
categorically said that such an engagement could not be used as an excuse for underperformance 
in the programme. 
(b) 
 The SPGC considered the request of Convener, DPGC, Dept. of Industrial & Management 
Engineering, to extend the duration of the Oral Comprehensive Board beyond eight weeks in the 
case of Mr. Amit Kumar Trivedi (15114261) Ph.D. student, Dept. of Industrial & Management 
Engineering. After discussion, the committee extended the validity of Comprehensive Board for 
Mr. Amit Kumar Trivedi (15114261) by two weeks (i.e. up to 10/05/2017). 
(c) 
 The SPGC considered the request of Mr. Kunwar Pritiraj Rajput (15104269), Ph.D. student, 
Dept. of Electrical Engineering, for extension (beyond four semesters) of the deadline for 
appearing in the comprehensive examination up to June 30, 2017. The SPGC granted extension

Rajput (15104269).
