C. Pre-requisites: Not Applicable. 
D. Short summary of the Courses: The aim of this course is to introduce the students from 
the departments of SPASE, AE and EE to the fundamentals of the science and engineering 
topics related to satellite-based communication & navigation. The students will learn 
about the various parts of an artificial satellite, suitable orbits, communication links, 
modulation, coding and multiple access techniques and various applications. In the 
navigation part the course will cover the basic principles of navigation, example of existing 
global and regional systems, the three segments of GNSS, viz., Space segment, Control 
segment and Ground segment, navigation signal structures, measurements, sources of 
error and applications. The course is targeted for all engineering and science disciplines.
