Notes: 
1) If the requisite number of lectures cannot be scheduled, the instructors may, in consultation with the 
students in their respective courses, decide on a schedule of extra classes. The requisite number of 
lectures in a full semester course is 38-40 lectures of 50 minutes each for 3- lectures a week schedule. 
 
2) There will be no extra classes on Gazetted Holidays. 
 
3) No class, quiz, presentation or any other academic activity will be scheduled on February 21, April 25- 
26, 2026, June 13-14, 2026, July 11-12, 2026, September 12, 2026, and November 14-15, 2026. 
 
4) Saturdays and Sundays falling within the examination period will be used for scheduling examinations. 
Under extraordinary circumstances, examinations of some of the courses may be scheduled on the days 
preceding/following the mid semester/end semester examinations. 
 
5) All make-up examinations permitted by the SUGC/SPGC will be conducted by the concerned 
instructors in -charge as per the schedule detailed in the Academic Calendar. 
 
* DMC (Doctoral Monitoring Committee) Report should be submitted on the Pingala portal for all 
eligible PhD students. 
 
**April 11, 2026 and October 03 (586th Senate) October 10, 2026 are compensatory Fridays for the Gymkhana 
Holidays. 
**In the 2026-27-1 semester, during the week in which the Gymkhana Holiday is granted (Friday of that week 
will be a holiday), Wednesday timetable will be followed on the Thursday of that week.
