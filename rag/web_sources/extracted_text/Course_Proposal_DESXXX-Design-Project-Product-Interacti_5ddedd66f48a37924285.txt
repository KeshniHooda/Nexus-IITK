6. Course Description: 
 
A) Objectives: 
• To develop understanding of how humans interact with products, and to identify scope for 
improving those interactions. 
• To analyse different interaction approaches towards achieving similar product functions, thereby 
developing more effective, efficient and satisfactory methods for the same. 
• To learn standard terminology, tools and techniques for interaction analysis and interaction 
design.

D) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet 
 
Products have evolved by leaps and bounds in the last few decades, owing to technological innovations 
and lifestyle changes. This in turn has put the spotlight on interactions between humans and the 
products they use. The focus of this course is to develop capabilities to analyse and redesign the 
nuances of human-product interactions, so as to make tasks easier, more effective and more enjoyable 
for the target users. In this course, students work in groups to research and redesign a tangible product 
from a usability perspective. The product could be a home/personal product (washing machine, sewing 
machine, party speaker, DSLR, video camera, etc.), community/commercial product (treadmill, coffee 
machine, projector, photocopy machine, vending machine, DJ console, etc.), medical product 
(sonography machine, body composition analyser, ventilator, etc.) industrial product (paper cutting 
machine, book binding machine, laser cutting machine, etc.) or vehicle control panel (bus, excavator, 
road paving machine, etc.).
