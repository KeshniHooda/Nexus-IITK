Enhance your business credentials by earning dual MBA degrees from the Indian Institute of 
Technology Kanpur (IITK) and the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee Lubar College of Business 
(UWM). 
This partnership program provides you with the opportunity to expand your horizons by coupling 
your MBA from IITK with a STEM-designated MBA program from UWM’s Lubar College of 
Business, a top business school accredited by AACSB International. Up to 4 courses of your 
IITK coursework are eligible to transfer to UWM!

ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
Grade point average of 2.75 and above (on a 4.0 scale). Many international 3-year 
bachelor’s degrees are accepted as equivalent to U.S. 4-year bachelor’s degree.
English Language Scores (one of the following required): 
• Minimum TOEFL score: Traditional: 550, IBT: 79
• Minimum IELTS score: 6.5
• Duolingo English test score: 120
Test Scores (may be waived under certain circumstances)
• GRE: 305 (verbal + quantitative scores), Quantitative score of 149, Verbal score of 142 
for international students, or
• GMAT: 500 total, Quantitative score of 36, Verbal score of 17 for international students
HOW TO APPLY
Step 1 – Prequalification
Inform IITK that you are interested in this dual degree program. IITK will share relevant 
documents with UWM for evaluation.
Step 2 – UWM Formal Application
If prequalified, IITK students must complete UWM’s online application, upload all required 
documents, and pay the UWM application fee. (Deadlines: July 15 for Fall Semester or 
December 1 for Spring Semester)
TUITION, FEES, SCHOLARSHIPS
IITK students will be charged UWM tuition and fees at the standard non-resident per credit 
hour rate, found at: catalog.uwm.edu/admission-costs/tuition-fees/ (See Business 
Masters Fee Schedule).
The Lubar College of Business offers a $6,500 scholarship paid over two semesters. To 
be eligible for the scholarship, the student may not be claimed by any recruitment agents, 
must pay the non-resident tuition rate, and remain in good academic standing.
