Notes: 
1) HOD's are requested to ensure that department offices are functional for 
registration purpose on May 20, 2017 (Saturday) and July 29, 2017 (Saturday). 
2) Lectures will be held on following Saturday's with the time table of the day 
mentioned against them: 
21-Jan-2017 (Sat) - Schedule of Friday shall be followed. 
1-Apr-2017 (Sat) - Schedule of Tuesday shall be followed. 
1-July-2017 (Sat) Schedule of Monday shall be followed. 
7-Oct-2017 (Sat) - Schedule of Thursday shall be followed. 
3) If the requisite number of lectures cannot be scheduled (requisite number being 
40 lectures of 50 minutes each for a 3-lecture a week full semester course), the 
instructors may, in consultation with the students in their respective courses, 
may decide on to schedule lectures 4th Feb, 8th April, 5 Aug or 28th October, 
2017. 
4) Extra classes should not to be scheduled on Gazetted Holidays. 
5) No class, quiz, presentation, or any other academic activity can be scheduled 
on April 22, 23; July 15, 16 and November 16, 17, 2017. 
6) Saturdays and Sundays falling within the examination period will be used for 
scheduling examinations. If the need arises, examination of some courses may 
also be scheduled on days preceding the mid-sem/end-sem exams. 
7) Lectures for modular courses offered in first half of semesters/summer term will 
commence on January 5, May 23 and July 31, 2017 whereas, lectures for 
modular courses offered in second half of semesters/summer term will 
commence on March 6, June 19, and October 3, 2017. 
8) Final examinations of modular courses offered in first half of semesters/summer 
term will be conducted during Feb 27 — Mar 4, June 16 — 17 and September 18 
— 23, 2017 whereas, final examinations of modular courses offered in second 
half of semesters/summer term will be conducted during April 24 — May 3, July 
17 — 18 and November 18 — 27, 2017. 
9) Instructor in charge of courses to ensure conduct of make-up exam on the 
assigned dates for students permitted by the SUGC/SPGC
