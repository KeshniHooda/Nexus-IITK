OFFICE ORDER 
 
Subject: Submission of GATE / CSIR / UGC-NET / INSPIRE / NBHM Documents at the Time of 
Admission. 

All students-including foreign nationals, part-time, sponsored, and self-sponsored candidates-who 
possess a valid GATE / CSIR / UGC-NET / INSPIRE / NBHM scorecard must submit the same during the 
admission process. Based on the submitted scorecard, the concerned Departmental / Centre 
Admission Committee will take an appropriate decision regarding eligibility for admission in the 
respective branch and the benefits applicable to the student, including fee concessions or 
scholarships, if any. 
 
Once the admission is offered and the semester has commenced, no additional documents or 
certificates of this nature will be accepted for claiming any further financial benefits, including fee 
concessions or scholarships. 
 
This order is issued with the approval of the competent authority.
