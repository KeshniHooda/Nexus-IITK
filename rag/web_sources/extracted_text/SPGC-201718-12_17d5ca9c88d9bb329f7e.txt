(c) 
To consider the request of DPGC, Convener, Dept. of Computer Science & Engg. for change in the minimum 
course requirement for M Tech (CSE) degree. The item was deferred from earlier meeting of the SPGC. 
 
The SPGC considered the request of the DPGC, Convener, Computer Science & Engineering, for change of 
minimum course work credits from 54 to 63 and minimum thesis work credits from 72 to 81 for M Tech 
programme with the stipulation that all the course credits be from CSE, unless approved otherwise by the DPGC on 
a case to case basis. After due deliberations, the committee recommends acceptance of the revised template for M 
Tech programme in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering. 
(d) 
 To consider the proposal of DPGC, Convener, Dept. of Economic Sciences, for candidates having qualified 
GATE/UGC-JRF/UGC-NET examination being eligible for PhD programme with Institute Assistantship. 
 
After due deliberations, the SPGC recommends that NET (Lecturer ship) qualified candidates be considered 
eligible for admission to Ph.D. programme with Institute Assistantship in the department of Economic Sciences.

After due deliberations, the members were of the opinion that for theses with no Category III external reports, the 
on line participation of the external examiner in the oral defense should be routinely permitted. 
(d) To discuss the way forward on several thesis grading anomalies in Dept. of Electrical Engineering. 
The SPGC discussed the issue of thesis grading anomalies in the Dept. of Electrical Engineering. After due 
deliberations, the committee recommended the conversion of all X grades to S grades. The SPGC has also 
expressed it displeasure on awarding of ‘fail’ grades to students without any valid academic reasons and asked to 
Chairperson to send an appropriate advisory note to DPGC, Convener, Dept. of Electrical Engineering.
