ISSUE OF DUPLICATE GRADE SHEET FOR STUDENTS GRADUATED FROM IIT K 

A. DUPLICATE GRADE SHEET: 
 
1. An alumnus requesting for duplicate grade sheet must provide the following details: 

a) Name of the Student 
b) Date of birth 
b) Roll No. 
c) Year of Graduation 
d) Programme of study 
e) Department of study 
 
2. For speedy processing, he/she may provide a Xerox or scan copy of his/her 
original grade-sheet. 

B. POSTAL CHARGES: 
 
If the alumnus or his/ her representative is unable to collect the duplicate grade sheet 
in person from the DOAA office, Institute can post the same at his/her requested 
address on payment of Rs. 300/- (if to be posted within India) else, Rs. 2,500/- (if to 
be posted outside India) 

 
C. PAYMENT OF CHARGES: 
 
Payment, in total, may be made through Bank Draft drawn in favor of 
“IITK Transcript Account” payable at “KANPUR”

E. PROCESSING: 

Upon receiving the bank draft/ net banking transaction receipt, postal address(s) (at 
which the document(s) is to be posted), and other relevant papers, if any, the request 
will be forwarded to the concerned unit for further processing. 
 
May kindly note that it takes approximately 15 working days from the day of receiving the 
above documents to process an application (this excludes postal time).
