7. Course Description: 
 
A) Objectives: 
 This course develops mathematical methods and computational algorithms for learning control policies 
subject to safety requirements. It connects constrained Markov decision processes with robust 
reachability, control barrier functions, and optimization-based safety filters. Students will learn to 
formulate safety specifications, distinguish expected constraint satisfaction from trajectory safety, and 
incorporate model uncertainty into controller design. The course also introduces conformal prediction and 
conformal risk control for statistical calibration of uncertainty and risk. Analytical exercises and 
computational case studies will develop students’ ability to evaluate the assumptions, guarantees, and 
performance limitations of safe learning methods.

C) Pre-requisites, if any 
1. Modern control theory, including state-space models and stability analysis. 
2. Knowledge in Probability and Statistics along with MATLAB simulation experience is required. 
3. Familiarity with basic constrained optimization is expected; preparatory material will be provided for 
students without this background. 

 D) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet: This course develops mathematical and 
computational methods for learning safe control policies under uncertainty, integrating constrained decision-
making, reachability, control barrier functions, safety filters, and conformal methods for uncertainty and risk 
calibration.
