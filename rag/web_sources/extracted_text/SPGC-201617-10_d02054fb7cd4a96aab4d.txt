Item 7: To consider the feedback from the departments on guidelines for the award of M.Tech./MS degree 
to those students who take a direct admission into the Ph.D. programme after B.Tech./BS/M.Sc as 
per the decision of the Senate (491st/2016-17/2nd). 
 
 The members were of the opinion that the guidelines needed to be looked at in detail before being 
finalized. The matter was deferred until the next SPGC meeting. 
Item 8: To consider the templates, eligibility criteria and admission procedure for Ph.D., MS (2-year) and 
MS (3-year) for the newly approved IDP in Cognitive Science (CgS IDP). 
 
 The SPGC considered the templates, eligibility criteria and admission procedure of Cognitive Science 
(CgS IDP). After discussion, the committee approved the Templates, Eligibility Criteria & Admission 
Procedure for Ph.D. and MS (By Research) programmes of the new IDP. The decision is to be reported to 
the Senate. 
Item 9: To consider the request of Convener, DPGC, Dept. of Materials Science & Engineering, for 
admitting two candidates into the merit list for the M.Tech. programme after the completion of the 
selection process. 
 
 The SPGC approved the inclusion of the two candidates in the merit list for M.Tech. admissions in the 
dept. of Materials Science & Engineering for the semester 2017-18-I. The names of these candidates had 
earlier been left out from the merit list. 
Item 10: To consider the proforma for registering for courses under S/X mode as per the suggestions 
emerging from the SPGC meeting held on 17/05/2017. 
 
 The SPGC approved the format as per Appendix - A.
