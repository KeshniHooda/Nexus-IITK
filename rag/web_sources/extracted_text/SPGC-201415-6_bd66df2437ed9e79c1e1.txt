Item 14: To discuss the proposal of ‘Early PhD admission for non-CFTI’s UG and all M.Tech. students’

 The SPGC discussed the proposal of ‘Early PhD admission for non-CFTI’s UG and all M.Tech.

clause and Depts./IDPs will be free to decide whether they want to take the admission under this 
clause in particular semester or not. 
Item 15: To discuss the departmental/IDPs feedback on ‘Mentoring/Monitoring of Doctoral Students’ 
and ‘exit option’ of PhD programmes. 
 
 The SPGC discussed the departmental/IDPs feedback on ‘Mentoring/Monitoring of Doctoral 
Students’ and ‘exit option’ of PhD programmes. After detailed discussion and considering the views 
of the various Departments/IDPs, committee recommends following: 
Mentoring/Monitoring 
 (a) 
Committee will have two members besides supervisor. 
 (b) 
Departments/IDPs may decide whether the committee has to be formed before/after the 
comprehensive exam. The Dept./IDP will have the option to induct one external member from 
outside the Dept./IDP. 
 (c) 
It will meet once in a semester. It will monitor student's performance in courses, 
comprehensive exam and progress in thesis. 
(d) 
It will replace peer committee and will also serve the purpose of recommending extension of 
programme/financial support. 
 
Exit Option 
 
 A student can apply for exit from Ph.D. after he/she fails in both attempts of comprehensive exam. 
and even after passing comprehensive exam. Student can get an alternate degree MTech./M.S. 
provided that he/she completes all requirements of the said degree as per existing clause 7.8 of the PG 
manual. Ph.D. thesis credits will be converted to master's thesis credits. The student can be given 
second Master’s degree even if he/she already has one. 
 
 The committee also wants to place on record that the department of Physics does not agree to an exit 
option from PhD programme, who directly admitted to PhD programme.
