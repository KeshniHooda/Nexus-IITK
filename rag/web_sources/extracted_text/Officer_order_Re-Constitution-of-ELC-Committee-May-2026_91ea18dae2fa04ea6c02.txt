Office Order
Sub: Re-Constitution of English Language Cell (ELC) Committee.
The UGARC in its report recommended that every BT/BS student should take an English course (as
part of the SCHEME basket) in their first year. The course level will be based on their performance in
the English Diagnostic Test (EDT). In compliance to the recommendations of UGARC 2020 as
approved by the Senate, the English Language Cell (ELC) was constituted. The ELC was housed in the
office of Dean of Academic Affairs. Accordingly, a steering committee was constituted vide Office
Order No. DIR/IITK/2022/OO/36 dated 05-May-2022.
The term of the aforementioned committee expired on 08-May-2025 and therefore consequent upon the
approval of the Director, the term of the ELC steering committee has been further extended upto 25-
May-2028 with the following revised composition:
1. Dean of Academic Affairs / Associate Dean Academic Affairs
Convener
2. Prof. Lakshmana Rao Pinninti, Coordinator
3. Prof. N P Sudharshana, Co - Coordinator
4. Prof. G Neelakantan, HSS

shall remain the same.
