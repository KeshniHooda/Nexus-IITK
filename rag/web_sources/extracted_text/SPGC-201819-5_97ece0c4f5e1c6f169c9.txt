Annexure-A 
 
REDUCING THE THESIS EXTERNAL REVIEW PROCESS COMPLETION TIME 
Background 
The reviewed data clearly shows that: 
 
More than 35% theses take above 4 months for external review completion. 
 
The average response time of foreign referees is 20+ days less than the Indian referee. 
 
If only two reviews were required, the average external review completion time will be more than a month lower 
(79 days for 2 reviews vs 116 days for 3 reviews) 
SPGC View 
The SPGC is of the firm view that when journals have moved from 3-6 months manuscript review period ~15 years 
ago, to 3-6 weeks today, it is not healthy for IITK to have a thesis external review average lag time of ~4 months. 
Recommendation 
With a view to significantly reducing the lag time, the SPGC suggests that: 
 
An automated online thesis review portal be developed on priority with automated reminders to the reviewers. In 
the interim, a stop-gap automated arrangement should be put in place. 
 
Revise the external thesis review process so that in case the first two received reports are positive (not category 
III), the referee whose report is awaited is given 2 weeks to submit the review (assuming s/he is past the deadline). 
If the referee still does not respond, the thesis processing need not be further delayed and the thesis defence may 
proceed armed with the two positive reviews. 
The detailed revised step-by-step thesis external review process, after receiving feedback from various departments, is 
as below: 
1. The three referees are approached, as usual. The distinction of abroad vs within India should be done away with. 
The approached referees should be well reputed in the research area of the thesis. 
2. At the time of approaching the referees, they are gently intimated that they may be requested to serve on the PhD 
thesis oral board with additional remuneration as per norms and that by consenting to review the thesis, they are 
also expressing their willingness to serve on the oral board. Referees from abroad will also be intimated that their 
participation in the oral board examination, should they be requested, shall be over video conferencing only. 
3. In case the first two referee reports are positive (i.e. not category III) and both reports are from abroad, then the 
higher priority referee (as per Chairman, Senate’s original prioritization) is requested to join the thesis oral 
examination board. In case one of the reports is from within India, the Indian examiner is requested to serve on the 
oral board. 
4. Concurrently, the remaining third referee is given a final deadline to submit the review. This deadline shall be two 
weeks from the receipt of the second report or the originally conveyed deadline, whichever is later. The polite

[Page 6]
6 
reminder shall clearly specify that the given deadline is final and in case we do not hear from him/her, we shall 
assume that s/he is not in a position to review the thesis and proceed further with the processing of the thesis. 
5. One week before the deadline, a final reminder is sent to the referee whose review is awaited. 
6. During the correspondence with the referee, in case the s/he seeks reasonable additional time to send the review 
(say extra two weeks), the deadline is extended as requested. 
7. In case referee does not submit review by the final deadline (including extension if any), a very polite message is 
sent to him/her saying that while we appreciate the time and energy that may have been spent in reviewing the 
thesis, in the interest of time, it shall not be possible for us to wait further for his/her review. We hope s/he would 
be willing to oblige us with a timely review the next time. 
8. Armed with two positive (non category III) referee reports and respective student responses and revised thesis, the 
process for thesis oral defence is initiated by the thesis supervisor. 
9. In case the third review is received sufficiently before the thesis oral defence, all efforts shall be made to address 
the concerns raised. This includes postponing the defence in case the delayed third report is Category III. 
 
10. Honorarium shall be paid to all delayed third reports.
