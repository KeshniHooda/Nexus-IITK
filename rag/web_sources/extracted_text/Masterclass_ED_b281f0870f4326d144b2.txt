Valedictory & Closing Remarks
The Centre for Energy Regulation (CER) at IIT Kanpur is engaged in capacity building and regulatory research in the power and energy sector. As electricity markets evolve and procurement becomes increasingly exposed to price volatility and market uncertainty, effective procurement planning and risk management assume growing importance. The Masterclass on Electricity Derivatives aims to develop a comprehensive understanding of derivative instruments and their applications in electricity procurement and risk management. It covers power market operations, derivative instruments and hedging strategies, Indian and international market developments, and the regulatory framework governing these instruments. The programme also examines applications in power portfolio management and procurement strategies for generators, traders, and commercial and industrial (C&I) consumers, including captive and open-access consumers, with a focus on opportunities and challenges in India's evolving power market.
❖ Understand the economic and financial foundations of electricity derivatives and their role in power markets.
❖ Explore power procurement arrangements, market operations, and electricity price formation.
❖ Identify price, volume, and basis risks and understand the principles of hedging.
❖ Examine Indian and international experience in electricity derivatives markets.
❖ Understand regulatory frameworks, market infrastructure, and product-design considerations.
❖ Explore derivatives applications in portfolio management and procurement by C&I, captive, and open-access consumers.
