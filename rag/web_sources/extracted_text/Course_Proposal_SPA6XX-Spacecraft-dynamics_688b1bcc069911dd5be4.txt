Proposal for a new course
Course title: Spacecraft dynamics
Course no.: SPA 637
Proposer: Ishan Sharma, Sharvari Nadkarni-Ghosh, Arun Misra
Department: Space, Planetary & Astronomical Sciences and Engineering (SPASE)
Credits: 9 (3-0-0-0)
Pre-requisite: Basic course in mechanics and adequate exposure to calculus.
Course description and contents:
This course discusses the orbital motion, trajectory analysis and maneuvers of a spacecraft. The
lecture distribution is given below in terms of 41 lectures of 50 minutes each.
1. The Two-Body Problem, Orbital Elements and Orbit Determination – 4 lectures
2. Orbit Maneuvers, Plane Change and Transfer between Orbits – 5 lectures
3. Interplanetary Trajectory Analysis and Gravity Assist – 8 lectures
4. Orbit Perturbations: Lagrange and Gauss Perturbation Equations and Applications – 9 lec-
tures
5. The Three-Body Problem, Lagrangian Points, Lyapunov and Halo Orbits – 8 lectures
6. Spacecraft Relative Motion, Rendezvous and Formation Flying – 7 lectures
Textbooks:
1. Curtis, H. D. 2019 Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students, 4th ed. Elsevier (Butterworth
– Heinemann).
2. Kaplan M. H. 2000. Modern Spacecraft Dynamics & Control, Dover.
3. Prussing, J. E., and Conway, B. A. 2012 Orbital Mechanics, 2nd ed. Oxford University Press.
4. Schaub, H., and Junkins, J. L. Analytical Mechanics of Space Systems, 4th ed. AIAA.
5. Roy, A. E. 2005. Orbital Motion, 4th ed. CRC Press.
References:
1. Chobotov, V. A. Orbital Mechanics, 3rd ed. AIAA.
2. Gurzadyan, G. A. 1996. Space Dynamics. London: Taylor & Francis.
3. Vallado, D. A. 2019. Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications, 4th ed. Space Tech-
nology Library.
