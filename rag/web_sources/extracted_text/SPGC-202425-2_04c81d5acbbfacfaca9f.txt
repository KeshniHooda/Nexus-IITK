8 
No Termination Policy at the end of first semester due to 
academic deficiency 
In view of the approved APEC guidelines in new PGARC, 
after due deliberation, SPGC members opined that the 
decision on the termination of PG students, at the end of 
their first semester, should be on a case-by-case basis, 
similar to what is being followed for other PG students. 
Therefore, SPGC members opined that "No Termination 
Policy" of PG students after 151 semester should be 
revisited. The same is to be placed for consideration of 
Senate.
