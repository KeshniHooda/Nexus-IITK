Particulars of the proposed Core Time Table (1st and 2nd Semester): 
 
1. The size of the first-year incoming batch in 2021-22 is ~1200. 
2. The students have been divided into two groups which will further be divided into 
four batches for 1st and 2nd semester courses (Batch A, B, C and D). 
3. These groups/batches shall consist of the students of the following Departments:
