A. Objectives 
The course introduces optimization and simulation techniques that are used in planning and 
management of water resources systems. The course will include application of these 
techniques to problems such as reservoir design and operations, river basin management, water 
allocation to multiple sectors, decision making under uncertainty and multi-criteria decision 
making.
