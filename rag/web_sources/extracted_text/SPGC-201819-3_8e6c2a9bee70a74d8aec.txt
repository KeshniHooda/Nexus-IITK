Item 4 Requiring SPGC Recommendations for Consideration of Chairperson Senate / Senate 
(a) 
To consider the cases of PG students who have failed to meet the stipulated academic requirements in the 
2018-19-I semester. 
 
The SPGC considered the cases of academically deficient students in the 2018-19-I semester. Annexure A 
lists borderline M Tech/MBA/MS(Research)/PhD students recommended to continue in their respective 
programmes. These students are to be issued letters of warning by their respective HoDs. Annexure B lists 
PhD students who have obtained at least one X in this semester, and have accumulated 2-7 Xs so far in their 
thesis work. The students are to be issued letters of warning by their respective HoDs. Annexure C contains 
the list of M Tech/MBA/MS(Research)/PhD students recommended by the SPGC for termination in their 
respective programmes as per extant rules. 
(b) 
To consider requests for academic extension for 2018-19-II from PG students exceeding maximum academic 
programme duration. 
 
The SPGC considered the requests of 122 PhD students for academic extension for 2018-19-II. The SPGC 
was alarmed at the high number of students exceeding the maximum programme duration. Cases requesting 
extension for the fifth or more than fifth time were individually discussed. Overall the following 
recommendations are made for consideration of the Senate (enclosed as Annexed-C). 
 
 The academic programme of all students positively recommended by their DPGC should be extended, with 
exceptions noted below: 
1. The academic programme of Mr Rajesh Bhatt (Y7204064, EE) is recommended for eleventh extension 
with a condition that registration be allowed only if he delivers the open seminar before January 02, 
2019 (registration date). Also a warning letter be issued to him by the HoD explicitly stating that this is 
likely to be the last extension. 
2. The academic programme of Mr. Assiz M P (Y8101061, AE, external) is recommended for termination 
since he has failed to progress satisfactorily even after the issue of warning letter in the previous 
extension. 
 
3. The academic programmes of Mr Sandeep Kumar (Y910266, CHE, part time) CHE, Mr Anshul 
Khandelwal (10101061, AE) and Mr Avinash I (10107065, CHM) are recommended for sixth extension. 
Along with the academic extension, a letter of warning should also be issued by the respective HoDs.

(e) 
To discuss the request of Mr Deepak Patidar (M Tech, AE, 16101009) seeking permission to modify his 
archived M Tech thesis. Mr Patidar was conferred the degree in the Jun 2018 Convocation. 

The SPGC expressed its concern on the use of experimental data from another research group in the same 
department by Mr Patidar in his thesis, without any explicit permission for the same and also not giving due 
credit to it in his archived thesis. The SPGC also noted that a large fraction of figures in Chapter 4 of the 
thesis use the aforementioned experimental data. Given that the experimental data is not his work, whether 
the modified thesis then meets the standard expected of an IIT Kanpur M Tech thesis is a question that will 
have to be properly addressed. Overall, the SPGC is of the firm view that the lapse in Mr Patidar’s thesis 
needs to be corrected as soon as possible. 
 
In view of the above, the SPGC recommends the following way forward: 
• 
Permission, in writing, be sought from Ms Puja and her thesis supervisors, Profs Sanjay Kumar and 
Kamal Poddar, for use of the relevant experimental data in Mr Patidar’s M Tech thesis. 
• 
 Armed with the written permission, the thesis be modified appropriately. 
• 
The modified thesis be evaluated by a board to certify its suitability as an M Tech thesis from IIT 
Kanpur. Preferably, the same board as the thesis oral board (Profs PS Ghoshdastidar, Kamal Poddar and 
TK Sengupta) be appointed for the purpose. sPost certification by the appointed board, the modified 
thesis be archived. 

 
In addition to the above, Head of Department, AE, should issue an advisory to Mr Patidar with a copy to his 
thesis advisor (Prof Tapan Sengupta) that any future publications from the thesis may use the 
aforementioned experimental data only with explicit permission from Ms Puja and her supervisors with due 
credit in terms of citations and acknowledgement.
