OFFICE ORDER 
 
Subject: Award for Graduate Research Impact and Merit (AGRIM) selection committee. 
 
The following committee is hereby constituted for the evaluation of applications received under 
the Award for Graduate Research Impact and Merit (AGRIM) and to oversee the selection process 
with effect from 24 September 2026: 
 
1. Dean, Academic Affairs (DOAA) – Convener 
2. Associate Dean, Academic Affairs (ADAA) – Member 
3. Chairperson, Senate Postgraduate Committee (SPGC) – Member 
4. Chairperson, Senate Undergraduate Committee (SUGC) – Member 
5. President Gymkhana or its Nominee – Member 
 
The Committee shall be entrusted with the responsibility of evaluating the AGRIM applications, 
selecting the eligible AGRIM students, and overseeing the implementation of the prescribed 
AGRIM guidelines. 
 
The Committee shall also undertake such other activities as may be necessary for the effective 
implementation of the AGRIM Scheme in accordance with the approved guidelines. 
 
This has approval of the competent authority.
