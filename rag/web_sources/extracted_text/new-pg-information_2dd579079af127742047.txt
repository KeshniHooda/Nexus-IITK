Reaching IITK
By Air The most popular air route to IIT Kanpur is through Lucknow Airport (IATA: LKO | ICAO: VILK). Lucknow is connected by direct flights to and from major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Bengaluru Note: Kanpur airport (Chakeri) is now open to civilian purpose. The Airport is located around 21 Km from IITK and cabs are available to and fro.

By Road Kanpur is connected to NH-2 that runs between Amritsar and Kolkata and connecting cities like Delhi, Agra, Allahabad, Patna. Kanpur is also connected to Lucknow on NH25 and is about 91 km from Lucknow. Cabs are available from Lucknow to reach IIT Kanpur.
By Road
Kanpur is connected to NH-2 that runs between Amritsar and Kolkata and connecting cities like Delhi, Agra, Allahabad, Patna. Kanpur is also connected to Lucknow on NH25 and is about 91 km from Lucknow. Cabs are available from Lucknow to reach IIT Kanpur.
By Train Kanpur is well connected by railway network with nearly every major city. Numerous overnight trains are also present from major cities like New Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore. IIT Kanpur is about 17 km from the Kanpur Central Railway Station. Direct buses to campus front gate are there from Jhakarkati. Tempos and Autos are also available which take about Rs. 200 - Rs. 250 from Station to IITK campus. One can also find shared auto services (commonly known as Vikram) for as low as 40 Rupees from Kanpur Railway Station, outside Platform 9 to campus front gate. Metro services are also available from Kanpur Central Metro Station to IIT Kanpur Metro Station from 6am to 10pm daily.

Hall Allocation
The Hall Allocation for 2026-27 is as following. Boys: Hall 13 Girls: Hall 4 The reporting help desk numbers will be updated here a night before the reporting.
It is mandatory for all registered students of IIT Kanpur to reside in the halls of residence assigned to them. The halls assigned to the first year boys students is Hall 13 . Hall 4 is the hall of residence assigned to the first year girl students.
The rooms and roommates in the halls are also assigned randomly to the students by the institute keeping in mind various factors. It is not possible to entertain a request for room/roommate change. It is also compulsory for a student to register in his/her Hall of Residence's mess.

Congratulations on your admission to IIT Kanpur! We are delighted to welcome you to the institute and look forward to having you as a part of the IIT Kanpur community. Please carefully go through the following important instructions regarding your reporting and stay at the institute. Mail to pg_cmhw@iitk.ac.in for further queries.
Announcement 📌 Directions to Hall 14 and Hall 4 has been updated in the Reporting Dates section. 📌 In the case of "Self-declration of Mental Health Status" and "Student Wellbeing Services - Consent form", please use Roll No instead of Application Number. 📌After you receive Pingala login details on 17th July (tentative), please fill PI (Personal Information) details accurately and upload all documents as mentioned in PI form. 📌In addition to the submission of scanned copy in Pingala, please bring hard copy of all forms with you. PG students of the Y26 batch are required to report in-person to the institute on 23rd July 2026 between 7:00 AM and 7:30 PM (Reporting Day).
On the reporting day, boys are required to report at Hall 14 and girls are required to report at Hall 4 .
Accommodation in the Halls of Residence (Hostels) will be allotted to students upon reporting.
Parents or guardians are welcome to accompany students. However, please note that the institute does not provide or arrange accommodation for parents/guardians. Parents are suggested to arrange for their own accommodation. The list of hotels at Kanpur can be found here. [Hotels] They are welcome to spend time with their wards in the halls from 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM . Parents and guardians are requested to leave the halls by 7:30 PM and they must leave the campus by 10:00 PM . Cars, including Ola and Uber, can enter the campus only with a valid registration number and license.
If a PG student does not wish to avail the hostel facility of IIT Kanpur, they may apply to become a day scholar . More details are available in the "Hall Allocation" tab.
Hostel rooms will be equipped with only basic furniture, including cots, chairs, tables, and cupboards. However, mattresses, pillows, blankets, and other materials will not be provided. Stalls will be present near the halls to buy these items.
The campus has two shopping complexes and food courts, and all halls have their own stationery, photocopy, and other related shops. Students can purchase materials from these shops if they wish to travel light. These shops provide items at reasonable prices. Toilets are equipped with geysers, and several washing machines are available for use in each hall.
IIT Kanpur has various canteens and mess facilities . Students can access these facilities from 23rd July 2026 onwards . IIT Kanpur also has a Health Centre and Pharmacy , both of which operate 24/7 .
After in-person reporting, students are required to stay in their allotted halls or on campus and are not allowed to leave the campus . They must spend the nights only in their allocated halls. This instruction is part of the institute's office order and is strictly enforced.
All Y26 students must register on the anti-ragging portal at its official website . The reference ID generated after registration is extremely important and must be noted down, as it will be required for further stages. [ Instructions ]

Hall Allocation
Accommodation in the Hall of Residences (hostels) will be allocated to students on the reporting day, 23rd July 2026 , immediately after reporting. On the reporting day, boys are required to report at Hall 14 and girls are required to report at Hall 4 . Students will be assigned their respective halls and rooms at the reporting location. *Please bring the Hall Registration Form (see document checklist) pre-filled on the day of reporting. Information related to Day Scholar for New PG Students Freshers who intend to apply for Day Scholar status from the beginning of their programme must not opt for hostel accommodation at the time of reporting. A refund of hostel fees, wherever applicable, will be processed only after the approval of Day Scholar status for the concerned semester of the Academic Year 2026 - 27. Students may apply separately for the refund of MEC charges, if eligible. The duly completed and signed copy of the Day Scholar application form (after the generation of Roll No) must be submitted to the DOSA Office (Mr. Saurabh Shukla at ssac_sa@iitk.ac.in ) with a copy to Mr. D C Mishra ( officer2_sa@iitk.ac.in ) and headadmin_cmhw@iitk.ac.in by July 20, 2026, for consideration by the competent committee. Please mention the following in your email application: 1. Full name 2. Department 3. Degree program 4. IITK Roll No. 5. Must attach the offer letter. The Day Scholar application form is available on the DOSA website under Forms (Serial No. 6): https://www.iitk.ac.in/dosa/ Students are required to submit a fresh application for Day Scholar status in every semester. Please note: all students still have to report on 23rd July 2026 between 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM at their respective reporting venues and attend the orientation program.
