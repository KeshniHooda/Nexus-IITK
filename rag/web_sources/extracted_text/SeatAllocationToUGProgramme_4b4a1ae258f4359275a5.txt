Please Note: 
During the document verification process, if any discrepancy or mismatch is found 
between the documents submitted by the candidate at any stage of the admission 
process and the documents produced during verification, and such discrepancy is 
determined to be the result of willful forgery, misrepresentation, fabrication, or any act 
of cheating, the candidature/admission of the candidate shall be cancelled 
immediately. The candidate may also be debarred from the ongoing admission and 
future admission processes conducted by the Institute, as per the applicable rules 
and regulations. The decision of the Competent Authority in this regard shall be final 
and binding. 
 
Candidates are advised to regularly check their registered email address for 
further updates and important announcements related to the examination.
