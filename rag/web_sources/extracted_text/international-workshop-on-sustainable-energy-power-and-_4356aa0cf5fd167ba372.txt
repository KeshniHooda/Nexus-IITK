Clean and sustainable energy is of paramount importance for all applications in propulsion, power, energy, and mobility. The workshop will bring together renowned experts from around the globe to share the latest fundamental and applied research innovations for cleaner energy utilization in a wide range of devices extending from hypersonic propulsion to micro scale devices using fossil and renewable fuels. A round-table discussion will also be held with the aim to identify key areas of common interest that will help develop strategy to promote collaborative research.
The workshop will have several tailored technical tracks with presentations from world renowned technical experts in the field of multiphase flows, fossil and biofuels, combustion, power and propulsion and air toxins with special focus on current and future R&D needs, challenges and opportunities.
The International Workshop is a jointly organized workshop by University of Maryland, College Park, University of Illinois at Chicago, ACRI CFD, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur and Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, and conducted at IIT Kanpur-Delhi campus (Outreach Center Noida at Sector 62) from January 19 to 21, 2024. It is the 12th workshop in a Series on Status of Combustion and Energy.
The event is supported by NSF, ONRG, IITK, IITD, Begell House, Springer, CAFFEM, ATOS, BIS, Convergent Science & ELICO Marketing.
Invited lectures by renowned experts in the field of fuel, bio-fuels, energy, power, propulsion, environmental pollution including CO2, and sustainability.
Poster presentation competition for students.
Contributed chapters would be published by international publishers after the workshop.
Researchers and engineers from academia, R&D organizations and industries working in the areas of fuels, energy, combustion, power, propulsion, hypersonics, air pollution, sensors and diagnostics, modelling, fossil- and bio-fuels, alternative energy, energy-water nexus, droplets and atomization, novel combustion concepts, engine combustion, gas turbine combustion, swirl flows and other related areas.
