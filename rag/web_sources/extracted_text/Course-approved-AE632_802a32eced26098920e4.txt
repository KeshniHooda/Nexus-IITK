6. Course Description: 
(A) Objectives: The objective of this course is to enable the students to make the link between 
structural dynamics and vibrations, and control theory instead of studying these topics in 
isolation. The course begins with formulation of the eigen value problem, converting the 
vibration problem into the state space form, and then applying control theory for systems 
where instabilities (such as flutter, torsional vibrations under aerodynamic loads) can lead 
to catastrophic failures.
