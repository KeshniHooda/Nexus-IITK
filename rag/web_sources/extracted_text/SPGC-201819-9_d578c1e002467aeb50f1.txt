(a) To consider ‘soft copy only’ submission of all theses. 
The SPGC recommends the proposal to the Senate with the following consensus view of the SPGC (enclosed in 
appendix). 
(b) To apprise SPGC of cases of ethical concerns raised during PhD theses review. 
The SPGC took cognizance of ethical issues in PhD theses that have been recently pointed out by the external 
referees during the thesis review process. The SPGC felt that it would be appropriate to bring the matter to the 
knowledge of the Senate. 
(c) Waiver for summer term registration of Part-Time category students. 
The SPGC discussed waiver of summer term registration for Part-Time category students and recommends the 
following to the Senate 
i. 
Waiver be granted upon an explicit request by the individual. 
ii. 
The request should be duly recommended by the supervisor and DPGC. 
(d) Requirement of submitting revised PhD thesis in light of referee comments. 
The SPGC deliberated, requiring the student to submit a detailed point-by-point response to external referee 
comments on his/her submitted thesis. There is however no explicit requirement of submitting a revised thesis. 
The SPGC felt that no submission of the revised thesis tends to dilute the purpose for which the external thesis 
review procedure has been mandated by the Senate. It therefore strongly recommends that the submission of a 
soft copy of the revised thesis with referee specific changes highlighted in alternative colours, be made a 
mandatory requirement along with submission of the point by point response to referee comments. 
 
(e) To take cognizance of data on Masters programme getting extended beyond the stipulated 22 months duration. 
The SPGC constituted the sub-committee noted below to suggest the way forward.

SPGC VIEW ON SUBMISSION OF ALL THESES IN SOFT COPY ONLY 
Preamble 
The entire world is moving towards paperless transactions. At IIT Kanpur, M Tech/M Des theses are being 
submitted in soft copy since 2017. With respect to PhD and MS(R) theses that are subjected to external review, 
the PG office still requires submission of, respectively, four and one bound hardcopies of the thesis to facilitate 
the external review process. PG office experience suggests that less than 10% of external reviewers request a 
hard-copy of the thesis. Several reviewers also email back a soft-copy of the thesis with annotated comments. 
This suggests that a very large majority of reviewers are happy to review the thesis provided in soft copy only. 
 
Suggested Way Forward 
Taking into account the ground realities above, the SPGC recommends that: 
(a) All theses be submitted in soft copy only from now onwards. 
(b) The external thesis reviewers be informed that a hard copy of the thesis shall be provided on request. Since 
the number of such requests is expected to be small, PG office shall take the responsibility of providing the 
hard copy to the external reviewer. 
It is further clarified that the thesis supervisor shall remain responsible for arranging one hard copy of the final 
revised version of the thesis and the student response to review comments (if applicable) during the thesis oral 
defence for the benefit of the oral board members.
