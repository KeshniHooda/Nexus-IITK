Item 9: To discuss about those PG students who had neither registered in summer term, 2016 nor they were 
on leave during summer term. 

The SPGC recommends for termination of their academic programme of all such type of students as per 
rules. However, SPGC also noted that the students had gone for the internships which is a part of their 
programme template. Therefore, SPGC also recommends that the Senate can take a lenient view and 
reinstate them without any further appeal. However, this should be a onetime measure and from the next 
year, the department has to ensure that the students follow the rules and register for summer term and then 
go for academic leave.
