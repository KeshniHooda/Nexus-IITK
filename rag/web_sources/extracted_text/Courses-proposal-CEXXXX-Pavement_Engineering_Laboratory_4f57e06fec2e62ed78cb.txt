8. Course description 
o 
Objective: Currently, the Undergraduate Level (UG) students in the Dept. of Civil 
Engg. heavily lacks exposure on the experimental part of Transportation Engg. Therefore, 
this course is designed to provide them with the necessary exposure on the key 
experimental part of transportation engineering with particular orientation towards 
pavement engineering part as per the state-of-the-art practice.
