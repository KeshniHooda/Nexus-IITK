Item 19: Any other item with the permission of the Chair. 
i: 
 The SPGC considered the views of DPGC Convener, Dept. of Aerospace Engineering for allowing to 
waive off 2 credits (i.e., 72 credits) from minimum course work requirements to Y14 batch of M.Tech.

for the permission as an one time exemption for Y14 batch M.Tech. students.
