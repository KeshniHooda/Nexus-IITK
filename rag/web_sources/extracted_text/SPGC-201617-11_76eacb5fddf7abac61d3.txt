 The Senate Postgraduate Committee (SPGC) in its ninth meeting held on May 17, 2017 discussed the 
proposal to streamline the implementation of the provision for conversion of registration in the Ph.D. 
programme from Full-Time to Part-Time under Section 4.8 of the PG Manual. After considerable 
discussion, the SPGC sent the following proposal for the consideration of the Senate. The added clauses 
are in bold. 

 
 Section 4.8.1. Change of Registration from Full-Time to Part-Time Ph.D. Programme 
 
4.8.1. A student admitted to a full-time Ph.D. programme may be permitted to change to a part-time Ph.D. 
programme. A student requesting such a conversion must: 
 
(i) 
 have completed the coursework, passed the comprehensive examination, given the "State of Art" 
seminar which is adjudged as satisfactory. 
 
(ii) 
 get the request endorsed by the supervisor(s) and the DPGC, 
 
(iii) 
 produce a "No Objection" Certificate from the Head of the institution/organisation, which he/she 
proposes to join. 
 
 (iv) a student who wishes to convert his/her Ph.D. programme from Full-time to Part-time on 
account of a job offer may be granted leave up to a semester, to be extended by another 
semester if required, to join the job provided he/she satisfies conditions (i) and (ii) above. 
 
(v) 
in case the ‘No Objection Certificate’ is not submitted by the end of the period of leave, the 
student is required to rejoin the Institute, or else withdraw from his/her Ph.D. programme. 
 
 The proposal was discussed in the Senate in its 497th (2016-17/7th) meeting held on June 29, 2017. The 
Senate was of the view that a) Section 4.8 be revised to bring M.Tech. and MS (By Research) 
programmes withing its purview; b) the provision for leave be extended by two semesters; c) the 
modified proposal be sent to the departments./IDPs for feedback; and d) the proposal be brought to the 
Senate for a reconsideration. 

The proposal is accordingly modified for the consideration of the SPGC.
