Office Order 
 
As per the PGARC report, passing an English proficiency test is a part of 
partial fulfillment of the PG program of a student (except in MBA, VLFM, DIIT, 
MSc-2 
Years, 
MSc-PhD 
Dual 
degree 
programs 
and 
MSc-PhD 
dual/MSR/MTech migrated). All the newly admitted PG Students (except in 
MBA, VLFM, DIIT, MSc-2 Years, MSc-PhD Dual degree programs and MSc-
PhD dual/MSR/MTech migrated) must clear the English proficiency test in 
the first academic year of their joining. 
This order applies to all the newly admitted PG Students (except in MBA, 
VLFM, DIIT, MSc-2 Years, MSc-PhD Dual degree programs and MSc-PhD 
dual/MSR/MTech migrated) getting admitted from Academic Year 2023-24, 
beginning the First semester. 
The English Language Cell (ELC) of IIT Kanpur will conduct the English 
proficiency tests two times in an academic year at the beginning of the regular 
odd and even semesters. 
Students with more than the following IELTS/TOFEL/GRE/GATE-English 
scores are exempted from clearing the English proficiency test 
1. IELTS band score 6.0 and above 
2. TOEFL score of 65 and above, 
3. GRE - analytical writing 3.5 and above; Quantitative and verbal 
reasoning 280 and above, and 
4. GATE XH English 
Those students who cannot clear the English proficiency test in the first 
academic year will be required to credit a remedial course offered by the 
English Language Cell (ELC) (S/X grade) or any other relevant course as 
advised by the ELC Course Instructor.
