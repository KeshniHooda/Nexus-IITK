 MINUTES OF THE 1st MEETING OF THE SENATE ONLINE PROGRAMME 
COMMITTEE (2022-23) 
 
Item 1. Request from Programs to change the eligibility criteria in SOPC manual. Currently we 
have: 
Bachelor's degree (4 years program) or a Master’s degree in appropriate discipline with at least 
55% marks or 5.5/10 CPI + 2 years of work experience (an applicant need not be employed to be 
eligible). Candidates with relevant experience will be preferred. 
Minutes: SOPC members were not in favor of changing the eligibility criteria. 
 
Item 2. Timeline for addition of new programs: 
- 
Full proposal should be received asap so that it can be sent to Senate. 
- 
Ideally, application window will end in May first week, so must start advertising now. 
Minutes: SOPC members agreed with the timeline. 
 
Item 3. Name change of DMRM program to Quantitative Finance and Risk Management 
Minutes: SOPC members accepted the name change and the same will be sent to Senate 
Chairman for approval. 
 
Item 4. Conduct of online exams: requests have been received but suggestions needed on how to 
proceed 
Minutes: SOPC members agreed to take it up and create a proposal to be submitted to 
Senate. 
 
Item 5. New courses: if required, new courses must be floated via DOPC and send to SOPC for 
approval

Minutes: SOPC members agreed with the process. 

The meeting ended with a vote of thanks to the Chair.
