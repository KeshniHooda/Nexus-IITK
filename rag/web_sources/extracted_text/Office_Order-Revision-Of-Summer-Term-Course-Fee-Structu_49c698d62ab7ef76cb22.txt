SUBJECT: REVISION OF SUMMER TERM COURSE FEE STRUCTURE. 
 
In reference to the subject cited above, the Summer Term Course Fee structure has been 
revised with effect from the 2025–26 Semester (Summer). 
 
The existing “per course fee” structure has now been replaced with a revised “per credit fee” 
structure. The new fee is fixed at Rs. 1,800/- (Rupees One Thousand Eight Hundred Only) per 
credit, subject to a maximum cap of Rs. 18,000/- (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Only).
