Item 16: To discuss the policy of course dropping. 

The SPGC discussed the policy of course dropping. After discussion, it was decided that the student 
has to clearly specify the reason for dropping the course. After receiving the consent of the instructor, 
the DPGC Convener of the parent department has to give a clear recommendation with reasons 
before sending it to SPGC Chairman. A template can be designed and send to all DPGC, Conveners 
for their comments.
