Objective: 
Public procurement involves the expenditure of significant public resources and therefore requires a 
careful balance between economy, efficiency, transparency, fairness, accountability and timely 
execution. This course aims to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of the public 
procurement process in India, from identification of requirement and procurement planning through 
tendering, contract drafting, contract management and closure. 
The course will cover the legal, financial and procedural framework governing public procurement, with 
particular emphasis on GFR 2017 and procurement manuals. A significant component will focus on 
contract drafting, enabling participants to understand how appropriate contractual provisions can 
clearly define the rights, obligations, risks and remedies of the parties. 
The course will also address international procurement, including INCOTERMS® 2020 and Letters of 
Credit, wherever relevant to the procurement of goods and services from overseas. 
A distinctive feature of the course will be its practical and case-study-oriented approach. Participants 
will examine common procurement and contractual situations. The course will therefore seek to 
develop not merely an understanding of procurement rules, but the ability to apply them effectively 
while taking sound, defensible and timely procurement decisions.
