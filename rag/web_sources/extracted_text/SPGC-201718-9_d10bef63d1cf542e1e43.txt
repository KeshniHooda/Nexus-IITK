Item 4 Items Requiring SPGC Recommendations for Consideration of Chairperson Senate / Senate 
 
The SPGC considered the various items and makes recommendations as noted 
 
(a) To consider the cases of PG students who have failed to meet the stipulated academic requirements in the 2017-
18-II semester. 
 
The SPGC considered the cases of academically deficient students in the 2017-18-II semester. The annexed-A 
contains the list of borderline M Tech/M Des/MBA/MS(Research)/PhD students recommended to continue in 
their respective programmes. These students are to be issued letters of warning by their respective HoDs. 
Annexed-B contains the list of PhD students who have obtained at least one X in this semester, and have 
accumulated 2-7 Xs so far in their thesis work. The students are to be issued letters of warning by their respective 
HoDs. Annexed-C contains the list of M Tech/MBA/MS(By Research)/PhD students recommended by the SPGC 
for termination in their respective programmes as per extant rules.
