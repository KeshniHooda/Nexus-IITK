Course Objective
This course develops stochastic analysis as a mathematical method for studying local and nonlocal partial differential
equations. Probability, martingales, Brownian motion, Itô integration and SDEs provide the foundations for diffusion gen-
erators, stopped processes, Dynkin’s formula, Kolmogorov equations and Feynman–Kac representations. An introductory
treatment of Lévy processes connects jump generators to integro-differential equations and the fractional Laplacian. The
emphasis is on precise regularity and integrability hypotheses, selected proofs, and stochastic representations of initial
and boundary/exterior value problems.
