Art Appreciation Violin (Hindustani Style)
Course Duration 16 Aug to 15 Nov, 2023 (27 Classes)
Course Venue Approach Cell, Academic Area Gate 2 IIT Kanpur
Learning Objectives
To familiarize students with Indian classical and semi-classical music, particularly as played on the violin.
To make students sensitive to the modes of creative and artistic expression through instrumental music, enabling them to appreciate the world of notes and beats even without lyrics.
To inculcate perception and understanding of creative expression within rule- bound, systematically laid out (classical) music: students will be exposed to a way of thinking that is different from thinking styles in science/ engineering fields.
To make students appreciate the history of Indian classical music, and how this system judiciously adapted an instrument like the violin (mainly a Western instrument) into indigenous string instruments.
To provide opportunities for students to perform before an audience and to appreciate performances by others: such experiences will help in confidence-building, bring out hidden talents, and promote participation in groups.
Prerequisites: No prior knowledge is expected, except an interest in music. After two/three classes student has to arrange violin for him/her. A couple of practice violin instruments may be available.

Broad Course Outline/ Day-wise topics:
Day 1. Introduction to Hindustani classical music and this course. Day 2. Detailed description of violin and its parts. Holding of Violin and its bow. Day 3. Bowing on violin and few exercises on bowing on different strings. Day 4. Playing bow in different speeds and few more exercises on bowing. Day 5. Holding left hand on violin and playing 4 swaras on one string. Day 6. Playing 7 basic swaras on Violin on all the four strings with few simple exercises. Day 7. Playing few simple alankaars with basic swaras. Day 8. Learning Komal and Teevra swar on Violin with few alankaars using these swaras. Day 9. Playing five simple Thaats on Violin Day 10. Playing swaras of Thaat on all the four strings with few exercises. Day 11. Playing National Anthem on Violin. Day 12. Introduction to Raag and its important features with some rules. Day 13. Introduction to Raag Yaman and playing some ‘Swar Abhyaas’ on Violin. Day 14. Understanding ‘Bandish’ or composition and playing a ‘Madhyalaya Bandish’ of Raag Yaman. Day 15. Concept of Taan and making small ‘sthai’ ‘Taans’ in Raag Yaman.
Day 16. Practice Session with Tabla. Day 17. Playing full ‘bandish’ of raag ‘Yaman’ with Sthai Taan and Antara Taan. Day 18. Playing a song based on Raag Yaman. Day 19. Concept of ‘Nyaas Swar’ and ‘Alap’. Playing few small ‘Alaps’ of Raag Yaman. Day 20. Incorporation of ‘Alaps’ in Bandish with Taal. Day 21. ‘Tihai’ in Hindustani Classical Music- Basic formation and its use with in Bandish. Day 22. Practice Session with Tabla. Day 23. Introduction to Raag ‘Bhupali’. Day 24. Playing Bandish (Composition) of Raag Bhupali. Day 25. Making few Alaps and Taans in Raag Bhupali and implementing them in the Bandish. Day 26. Practice Session for performing Raag Bhupali Madhyalay Bandish with Alaps and Taans. Day 27. Performance and Assessment.
