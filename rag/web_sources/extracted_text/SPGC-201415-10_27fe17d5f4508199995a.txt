Item 11: To consider the explanation of leave of absence of Ms. Priyanka Sachan (Y9502064) PhD student, 
Dept. of Chemical Engineering. 

The SPGC considered the explanation of leave of absence of Ms. Priyanka Sachan (Y9502064) PhD

explanation for her leave of absence. Hence, committee turned down the request. 
Item 12: To discuss the feedback/suggestions received from various Depts./IDPs on operationalisation of 
IDC courses, referred by Dr. Mahendra Verma. 
 
The SPGC discussed the feedback/suggestions received from various Depts./IDPs on operationalisation 
of IDC courses, referred by Dr. Mahendra Verma. After considering the views received from various 
departments and after detailed deliberations on it and decided that the implementation details will take 
care of most of the issues (as discussed in FAQ’s). SPGC accepted the document as it is and 
recommended it to Senate for consideration and approval. 
Item 13: Any other with the permission of the Chair. 

The SPGC considered the request of Mr. Arun Raj S R (11201066) PhD student, Dept. of Aerospace 
Engineering, for change of academic registration from regular to external category. After discussion, it 
was opined that conversion to external PhD registration needs several other steps including the NoC 
from the organization, list of facilities, identification of supervisor of that organization and consent of 
the supervisor etc. SPGC did not approve the change of registration from regular PhD program to 
External PhD program. 

The meeting ended with the vote of thanks by the Chair.
