6. Course Description: 
A) Objectives: The course will build up from a basic Machine learning course and introduce 
the concepts of Deep learning. The students are expected to have a basic exposure of 
Machine learning and computer programming. The problems and examples relevant to 
engineering, especially chemical engineering, will be selected, whenever possible.
