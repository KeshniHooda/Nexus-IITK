 The SPGC discussed the issue of ‘Admission to part-time program of those employees whose place of 
work is out of Kanpur but who may attend the classes as per their requirements’ referred by Senate. 
After considering the views received from various departments and after detailed deliberations, SPGC 
did not recommend admission of any candidate under part-time category residing outside Kanpur. 
 Item 13: To discuss the issue of ‘Extension of SOTA seminar for those PhD students who fail to deliver 
the SOTA seminar within six months from the passing of Comprehensive exam’, referred by 
Senate. 

 The SPGC discussed the issue of ‘Extension of SOTA seminar for those PhD students who fail to 
deliver the SOTA seminar within six months from the passing of Comprehensive exam’, referred by 
Senate. After discussion and considering the views received from various departments and after 
detailed deliberations, it was recommended that after six months on the request of student Chairman, 
SPGC may grant the extension upto three months and another three months extension may be granted 
by SPGC. Student has to apply in advance for both extensions. If a student fails to deliver the SOTA 
seminar even after extension granted to him, then he/she has to re-appear for Comprehensive Exam. 
PG office is not obliged to send the reminder to the student abut deadline for SOTA seminar.
