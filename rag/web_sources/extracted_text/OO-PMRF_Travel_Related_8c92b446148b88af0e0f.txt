OFFICE ORDER 
 
Reg: 
Financial Support for Scholars registered under Prime Minister’s Research 
Fellowship (PMRF) Scheme 
 
PhD scholars registered under PMRF scheme, who have completed their State-of-the-Art Seminar 
(SOTA), are eligible for financial support from the grant received under PMRF scheme to attend 
national and international events/seminars/conferences and other programs aiding the research 
work. 
The support for an international events will be upto 80% of the expenses in the form of an 
advance with a maximum capping of Rupees Two Lakhs only per year/the balance amount of 
the grant, whichever is less. For events within India, the entitlement of travel, accommodation 
charges, and DA would be as per guidelines/rules of the Institute in vouge. 
This office order supersedes all previous orders on the subject.
