Item 4: To consider the request of Mr. Krishanu Sadhukhan (14109878) Ph.D. student, Dept. of Physics for 
dropping of the semester of 2016-17-I on medical grounds. 
 
 The SPGC approved the semester drop for the semester 2016-17-I on medical grounds to Mr. Krishanu 
Sadhukhan (14109878). 
Item 5: To consider the request of Ms. Abhilasha Singh (14105261) Ph.D. student, Dept. of Mechanical 
Engg. for leave from 16/09/2016 to 17/12/2016 on medical grounds. 

 The SPGC approved the leave to Ms. Abhilasha Singh (14105261) from 16/9/2016 to 17/12/2016 on 
medical grounds with a reduction in the thesis credits to 09. 
Item 6: To consider the request of Mr. Pravendra Singh (16111264) Ph.D. student, Dept. of Computer 
Science & Engg. for dropping of the semester 2016-17-I on personal grounds. 
 
The SPGC recommended semester drop for the semester 2016-17-I on personal grounds to Mr. Pravendra 
Singh (16111264). The recommendation is to be sent to the Chairman, Senate for approval. 
Item 7: To consider the request of Mr. Shantanu Sen (13218066) Ph.D. student, Dept. of Biological Sciences 
& Bioengineering, for exiting Ph.D. programme with M.Tech. degree. 
 
The SPGC recommends exit with M.Tech. degree for Mr. Shantanu Sen (13218066) under section 7.8 of 
the PG Manual. The exit option is subject to the completion of thesis requirements for the M.Tech. degree. 
Item 8: To consider the recommendation of Convener, DPGC, Dept. of Chemical Engg. for termination of 
M.Tech. programme of Mr. Vikram Singh (16102030). 
 
The SPGC approved the termination of M.Tech. programme of Mr. Vikram Singh (16102030) under 
section 5.7 of the PG manual. The decision is to be reported to Senate. 
Item 9: To consider the recommendation of Convener, DPGC, Dept. of Physics with respect to the 
undergraduate course PHY402A: Classical Mechanics II to be made open to PG students with the 
dual numbering PHY600/PHY402A. 
 
The proposal for a dual numbering of a UG course as a UG/PG course was not accepted by the SPGC. 
Item 10: To seek opinion on the suggestions made by Dr. Vinay K Gupta, Dept. of Civil Engg. regarding 
circulation of various proposals for getting the departmental feedback. 

The SPGC accepted the suggestions made by Dr. Vinay K Gupta, Dept. of Civil Engg. It was decided that 
all the major policy proposals mailed to the SPGC members for discussion in the department/DPGC, will 
be copied to faculty.
