6. Course Description: 
 
Objectives: By the end of this course, the students will be able to 
understand and analyse linear periodically time-varying (LPTV) systems and circuits, 
get familiarized to circuit theorems pertaining to LPTV networks, periodic-steady-state 
simulations, noise in LPTV networks, and practically used LPTV circuits, like the N-path 
filters, N-path mixers, multi-phase dc-dc converters, N-path chopping, etc.,

A) Pre-requisites: Consent of the Instructors 
 
B) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet: Linear periodically 
time varying (LPTV) networks, switched-RC filter, impulse response in LTI & LPTV 
networks, Zadeh expansion of LPTV networks, Harmonic transfer functions, N-path 
principle & examples: multi-phase converters, time-interleaved sampling, N-path 
switched RC filters, N-path mixers, mixer-first RF receiver, circuit theorems for LPTV 
networks, adjoint LPTV networks, inter-reciprocity in LPTV networks, noise in LTI & 
LPTV networks with sampled output.
