Proposal for award of MSR degree with PhD 
degree to those students who are admitted to 
PhD program in Sciences directly after a 4- 
year undergraduate degree 
There was a proposal from Department of Physics to offer MSR degree 
with PhD degree to those students who are admitted to PhD program in 
directly after a 4-year undergraduate programme (Annexure M.7-A). The 
feedback from the Department were sought on this as well (Annexure M.7-
B). 
It is to be noted that the Senate in its meeting held on 28-11-2014 
had approved MSR degree only for Engineering Departments. However, 
based on the PGARC report, the Senate has also approved MSR degree as 
an exit degree for students admitted to PhD program in the Sciences 
Department (clause 7.8.2 of the PG manual). With this background and for 
the benefit of the PhD students, SPGC proposed the following: 
(a) Award of MSR degree with PhD degree to those students who are 
admitted to PhD program in directly after a 4-year undergraduate. 
(b) To simplify, clause 11.7.1. and 11.7.2 to be combined, i.e., existing 
clause 11.7 to be restructured. Also, deletion of clause 3 in Annexure V of 
PG manual is needed. 
This proposed modification in the PG manual has been provided in 
Annexure M.7-C. This matter is to be placed in the Senate for discussion 
and approval. 
8. 
Offering Profession and Communication 
Skills Course (XYZ888; XYZ is department 
code) and seminar course (XYX801) in 
modes other than offline mode 
The Senate in its 569th meeting did not approve the proposal of SPGC for 
the waiver of XYZ801 for PhD students who are working professionals 
(documents attached). It was pointed out that compulsory courses cannot 
be waived. The Senate had also directed the SPGC to discuss this matter 
to meet the requirements of the two seminar courses and XYZ888. 
After due deliberations, SPGC requests the Senate to cancel the approval 
of waiver given to PhD students, who are working professionals, for 
XYZ888 in its 564th meeting. In addition, SPGC recommends that 
Departments/IDPs should be allowed to offer these courses (XYZ801 and 
XYZ888) in some other modes (such as online) for PhD students who are 
working professionals and have returned to their parent organization after 
completion of courses. This matter is to be placed in the Senate for 
discussion and approval. 
9. 
Changes in comprehensive exam timeline 
Currently, the course credits requirement in Departments/IDPs is 36-54 
for students admitted with masters degree, whereas it varies between 36-
72 (for almost all) for students admitted with bachelors degree. Therefore, 
it is expected that students can complete course work in 1-2 semesters (if 
no backlogs). 
Considering the Senate approved 'Open Seminar in 9th Semester or 
OS9S" policy and recommendations of Academic Practice Committee 
(APC) (Annexure M.9-A) and related feedback from Departments 
(Annexure M.9-B), SPGC recommended that students complete their 
comprehensive exam at the earliest after completion of course work. In 
this view, the proposed changes in the PG manual has been provided in 
Annexure M.9-C. 
This matter is to be placed in the Senate for consideration.

Permission to proceed to other Academic 
Institutions as Non-Degree Students" for 
MSc students 
Currently, both PG (M.Tech./M.Des./M.S. by Research/Ph.D.) and UG 
students are permitted to go to other academic institutions as non-degree 
students as per clause 6.1 (PG manual) and 11.5 (UG manual), 
respectively. There is no such provision for MSc students in MSc-MSPD 
manual. The proposal on this matter (Annexure M.11-A) from the SPGC 
subcommittee along with feedback from Departments/IDPs (Annexure 
M.11-B) were discussed. After due deliberations, SPGC recommended 
the subcommittee proposal. The proposed addition in MSc-MSPD 
manual (as clause 11.5) has been provided in Annexure M.11-C. This 
matter is to be placed in the Senate meeting for consideration.

Chapter 10 
Rules Governing Change, Addition in the MSc-PhD (Dual Degree) Programme 
10.1 Admission and conversion Procedure 
A) New Students: Admission to the Programme is strictly through the Joint Admission Test for Masters (JAM), according to 
the All-India Rank (AIR) and the preference given by the candidate. All admission rules are identical to MSc (2-year) 
programme. 
B) IITK MSc (2-year) Students: Students admitted in the MSc (2-year) programme may apply for MSPD programme in 
departments where it is available, subject to fulfilment of eligibility criteria as mentioned below and availability of seats in 
the programme. 
(i) Eligibility: CPI of 7.0 or more and no backlogs at the time of applying. 
(ii) Application Process: Students may apply at the end of their third semester (but before the date of registration for the 
fourth semester). Students should include two letters of recommendation with the application. At least one of the letters 
should be from an instructor with whom the student has done a course at IIT Kanpur. Applications shall be considered by a 
selection committee, to be set up by the head of the department, and approved by the Chairperson, SPGC. The 
recommendations of the selection committee shall be approved by SPGC. 
10.2 Academic Roadmap 
1) From the perspective of whether MSc or PhD rules apply, the time spent in completing the MSPD's first 3 years of approved 
coursework is split into two parts: 
A) First two years of coursework: During this period an MSPD student will be governed by the rules meant for MSc (2-year) 
students (except for the course template, see Annexure I). 
B) Third year ofcoursework: MSPD students migrate to the PhD part of the programme provided they have cleared all the courses 
of the first four semesters as per MSPD template with at least 6.0 CPI. Students who have converted from MSc (2-year) to MSPD 
are allowed to have PHY563 and PHY566 mapped to PHY501 and PHY502. Furthermore, 
(i) 
Till a student migrates to PhD part, they will be governed by MSc (2-year) rules (except for the course template). After 
migration, the student will be governed by the rules meant for the PhD students (except for the course template). 
(ii) When a student migrates late (after fifth or later semester), then the already done third-year-courses in the template should 
be counted towards their PhD coursework. 
2) If a student completes the 3-year coursework but is still unable to migrate (CPI less than 6.0), then the student is allowed to 
either (a) exit the programme with an MSc (2-year) degree or (b) continue doing extra elective courses to improve the CPI to 6.0. 
The maximum duration in such cases is (1.5 times of normal duration) 9 semesters. 
3) MSPD student will get an MSc (2-year) degree after successful completion of the full three-year-course-template of MSPD 
program. 
10.3 Summer Term 
Until MSPD students migrate to the PhD part of the programme, they are expected to spend a minimum period of 8 weeks during 
every summer term doing a project on campus with a faculty member of the Department of Physics, IIT Kanpur. A co-supervisor 
from within IITK or outside IITK is allowed. However, if they spend time outside IITK for the project, they will not get funding 
from two sources. A student who does not do such a project is not eligible to receive any assistantship during the summer term.
