Item 14: To consider the proposal of DPGC, Convener, Dept. of HSS for permitting to admission in PhD 
programme in HSS department from Centrally Funded Universities without the requirement of 
JRF/NET/ICMR/GATE/CEED. 

 The SPGC considered the proposal of DPGC, Convener, Dept. of HSS for permitting to admission in 
PhD programme in HSS department from Centrally Funded Universities without the requirement of 
JRF/NET/ICMR/GATE/CEED. After discussion, committee has unanimously agreed with the 
proposal with the following that applicant having with a CPI of at least 7.0/70% in his/her M.Sc. 
degree. Hence, SPGC recommends to Senate for consideration and approval of the proposal. 
However, the decision about the financial assistantship has to be taken by the Institute administration.
