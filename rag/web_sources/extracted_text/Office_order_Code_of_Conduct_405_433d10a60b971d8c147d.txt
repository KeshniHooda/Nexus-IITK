OFFICE ORDER 
Subject: Code of conduct during Examinations. 
It has been observed that students are carrying forbidden items such as cell phones and electronic 
gadgets during Examination. Unfair practises during exams are a violation of code of conduct and 
will result in initiation of disciplinary proceedings. 
 
Following rules will be followed throughout the examination period : 
 
a. Students are not allowed inside LHC during examinations unless their exams(s) are scheduled 
for that day and time. 
 
b. During examination days, entry to the Student Lounge (SL) will be permitted only after 
verification by the LHC staff. Students must provide their roll number along with a valid Institute 
ID (physical, digital via IITK App, or Pingala). Staff will cross-check examination timings from 
the exam scheduler website, and students with an ongoing examination will not be allowed entry. 
 
c. Carrying of Institute ID card is mandatory during the duration of the exam. 
 
d. No student shall possess analogue phones, smartphones, mobile or connected devices, 
Bluetooth-enabled gadgets, smartwatches, or any form of communication equipment inside the 
examination venue during examination hours. 
 
e. Possessing course-related materials during the examination hours in the exam venue is 
prohibited, unless the exam is explicitly open book. 
 
f. While the use of calculators in the examination hall will be monitored as per the course 
regulation, however sharing calculators during the test is not permitted. 
 
g. If a student is found in possession of Cell phone / electronic gadget(s), his exam will be 
cancelled, and disciplinary proceedings will be initiated against him/her. 
 
h. CCTV in the corridor and rooms of LHC will be switched on and monitored during the duration 
of examination(s). 
 
i. 
This office order will supersede any previous instructions issued in this regard and will come 
into effect immediately.
