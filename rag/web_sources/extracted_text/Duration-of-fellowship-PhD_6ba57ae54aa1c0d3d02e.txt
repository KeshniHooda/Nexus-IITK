OFFICE ORDER 
 
Subject: 
Duration of Fellowship for the PhD Programme 

(a) 
The duration of the Institute fellowship for all PhD scholars (Except PMRF Fellows) 
registered for the PhD programme at IIT Kanpur is for five years only (from all sources, 
including external fellowship/assistantship secured on Academic Leave during the 
tenure of the PhD programme). 
(b) 
The tenure of the Institute fellowship 
will commence from the date of 
joining/registration in the programme, subject to the satisfactory academic progress of 
the scholar, until the date of Final Viva Voce Exam (Thesis Defense) or until the End 
of Institute Fellowship Tenure (i.e. 5 years), whichever is earlier. The student should 
be a regular registered student while availing the fellowship (i.e. not in semester leave 
or leave till defense). This is applicable only for thesis submissions from April 1, 2023; 
no retrospective payments will be made. 
(c) 
For students becoming eligible for fellowship after joining/registration in the PhD 
programme (Category C and D), the obligations will continue for the remainder of the 
student's stay in the doctoral programme. No retrospective payment will be made.
