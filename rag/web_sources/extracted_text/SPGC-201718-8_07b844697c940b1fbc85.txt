Item 5 Policy matters requiring detailed discussions 
(a) To consider the sub-committee recommendations on proposal for ‘Promotion and encouragement of good 
teaching and review of strengths of various academic programmes’. 
The SPGC discussed the sub-committee recommendations in detail. Sub-committee convenor,in consultation 
with Chairperson SPGC, to finalize recommendation incorporating suggestions that emerged during the 
discussion. A summary of the recommendations shall be circulated to the departments/IDPs for feedback. Final 
recommendation post deliberation on received department feedback to be put up for the consideration of the 
Senate. Chairperson expressed gratitude for the commendable work done by the sub-committee. 
(b) To consider the recommendations of the sub-committee on apparent anomalies/ambiguities in the PG Manual. 
The SPGC discussed the anomalies/ambiguities in the PG Manual, submitted by the sub-committee. All 
modifications accepted. In particular, NET (lecturership) qualified candidates applying for admission to 
Economic Sciences Master’s program shall be considered eligible for Institute Assistanship. Recommendation to 
be put up for consideration by Senate post proof-reading and corrections (if any) to the document submitted by 
the subcommittee. Chairperson expressed gratitude for the commendable work done by the sub-committee.
