Username for moodle account
Please ensure that your Moodle ID is the same as your CC user ID . Otherwise, your account may be deactivated at a later stage , and a new account with your username may be created for any course.
Server time offset by ~5.5 hrs
Due to a server glitch the time being displayed was wrong and 5.5hrs in future. This problem has been rectified now by syncing time with network time provided by CSE server. The course instructor/teaching assistants are requested to kindly make sure the timing is proper on deadline for assignments.
