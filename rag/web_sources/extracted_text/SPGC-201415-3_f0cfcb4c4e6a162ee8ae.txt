Item 8: To finalize the policy of leave provisions for PG students. 

 The SPGC considered the leave provisions of PG students. After due consideration of the 
suggestions/feedbacks of various Departments/IDPs following changes are made in the sub-clauses of 
5.1.1. & 5.1.2. of the modified PG manual. 
 
5.1.1. “A postgraduate student may take a maximum of 30 days earned leave in an academic year 
(including winter break and mid-semester breaks) provided that normally no more than 10 days leave 
can be taken during a semester (including summer term). However, this 10 day cap will not be 
enforced when semesters/summer term are not in progress”. 

5.1.2. “In addition, a student may be allowed casual leave for up to 6 days per semester during 
regular semester and 4 days during summer term. The casual leave cannot be carried over”.
