6. Course Description: 
A) Objectives: 
The objective of this course is to introduce students to the features and structures of the Tibeto-
Burman languages of Northeast India. With examples from some languages of the northeast, 
students will learn comparative and contrastive analysis in Tibeto-Burman languages. Students 
will also learn how to analyze the phonological and morpho-syntactic structures of the Tibeto-
Burman languages. They will have the ability to undertake research on any topic in Tibeto-
Burman languages. Eventually, they will become experts in the Tibeto-Burman languages of 
northeast India.

C) Pre-requisites: Any course in linguistics 
D) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet: 
This course provides an introduction to the fundamental features and structures of the Tibeto-
Burman languages spoken in Northeast India. Students will learn the phonological and 
morphosyntactic structures and conduct a comparative and contrastive analysis of the Tibeto-
Burman languages. Ultimately, it will enable them to become experts in the Tibeto-Burman 
languages of northeast India.
