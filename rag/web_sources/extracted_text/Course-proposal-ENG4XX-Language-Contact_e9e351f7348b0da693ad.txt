6. Course Description: 
A) Objectives: 
 
To introduce students to the broad area of contact linguistics and provide an understanding of the 
various approaches to the study of language contact and its outcomes. A familiarity with the 
methodologies used to study languages in contact will enable the students to: 
a) identify a contact situation and describe the outcome of contact. 
b) identify contact features in a linguistic variety. 
c) Explore the motivation for language change.

D) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet 
The study of the impact of interaction between speakers of different linguistic groups is 
language Contact. The contact maybe geographically contiguous, social or long distance such 
as through books and electronic media. The course aims to provide an insight into the

[Page 2]
methodologies used to study languages in contact. This will enable the students toidentify a 
contact situation and describe the outcome of contact. The exploration of the motivation of 
language change will aid in identifying the contact features in a linguistic variety.
