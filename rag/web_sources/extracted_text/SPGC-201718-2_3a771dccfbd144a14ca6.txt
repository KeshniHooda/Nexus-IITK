Item 6 Policy Items Requiring Detailed Discussion and Deliberation 
(a) To consider the proposal on ‘Promotion and encouragement of good teaching and review of strengths of various 
academic programmes’. 
The SPGC decided to form a sub-committee for an exhaustive look at all aspects related to ‘Promotion and 
encouragement of good teaching and review of strengths of various academic programmes’. The sub-committee shall 
brainstorm, deliberate and finalize its recommendations in a brief report in a timely manner. The recommendations 
shall be taken up for further deliberation and necessary action (if any). The sub-committee is as below: 

 
Dr Bhaskar Dasgupta, ME (Convener) 
 
Dr Monica Katiyar, MSE (Member) 

 
Dr D Goswami, CHM (Member) 

Dr Bushra Ateeq, BSBE (Member) 
(b) To consider the proposal on ‘Operating Guidelines for the Student Entrepreneurship Policy’ referred to the SPGC by 
the Senate. The item was deferred in the last SPGC meeting. 
The students’ representative made a presentation which received a mixed response from the SPGC members. SPGC 
requests the student body to prepare a one-page executive summary highlighting salient features and potential 
benefits and pitfalls of the entrepreneurship policy for the benefit of the faculty. The policy document along with the 
executive summary shall be circulated to the departments for their feedback. The matter shall be taken up again with 
the department feedback. 
(c) Formation of an Inter-Disciplinary Curriculum Committee (IDCC) 
The SPGC discussed and approved formation of IDCC. Its mandate and functioning shall be similar to the CCC that 
functions under the SUGC. 
(d) Modes of participation of departments and IDP's in the course IDC603 
 
The proposal shall be circulated to all departments for feedback. Prof B Dasgupta shall coordinate the effort.
