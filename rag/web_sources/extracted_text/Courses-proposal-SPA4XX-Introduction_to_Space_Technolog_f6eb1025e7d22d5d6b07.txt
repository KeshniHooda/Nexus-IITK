6. Course Description: 
 
A. Objectives: The objective of this course is to introduce the students from interdisciplinary 
Engineering streams to various topics of Space Technology which are imperative for the design, 
development, operation, and application of a spacecraft system.

C. Pre-requisites: Not Applicable. 
D. Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet: The aim of this course is to 
introduce the students from interdisciplinary Engineering departments and centres to the 
fundamentals of the Space Technology topics related to manmade orbital space assets for 
different applications. The students will learn about Satellite orbits, launch vehicles, different 
segments of a satellite system, Navigation and remote sensing satellite systems, image 
processing and data products, space laws and policies. The course is targeted for all 
engineering disciplines.
