6. Course Description: 
A) Objectives: This course will introduce students to machine learning (ML) applications 
across various areas of chemical engineering. Students will also be introduced to various ML 
Python libraries and most lectures will involve hands-on case studies. The course is 
particularly targeted towards postgraduate students and has been designed to help both, 
experimentalists and computational researchers.
