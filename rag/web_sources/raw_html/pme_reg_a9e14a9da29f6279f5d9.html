    • infoAbout this Program

      The Program on Power Market Economics and Operation: provide insights to the economics, operation & regulatory aspects of power market. The Program would be conducted under the aegis of Office of Outreach Activities, IIT Kanpur.

      Program Duration:

      About 30 hours

      Relevance of the Program:

      Understanding the evolution of power market, and regulatory structure for the same

      Examining the cost drivers for regulation and ancillary services to maintain the grid security

      Understanding the economic operation of power market and power procurement plannin

      Learning from international experience with power market development

      Addressing the present and emerging challenges related to power market

      Understanding electricity derivatives

      Contracts for Differences

      Virtual Power Purchase Agreements for hedging and investment planning

      Key Topics:

      Power Market Regulation

      Operation of Power Exchanges

      Market Monitoring

      Open Access

      Distributed Energy Market

      Power Procurement Planning

      Ancillary Services

      Congestion Management

      Retail Competition

      Load Forecasting

      Security Constrained Economic Dispatch

      Market Based Economic Dispatch

      Economic Operation of Power System

      Resource Adequacy

      Power Market Derivatives

      Contract for Differences

      Virtual Power Purchase Agreement
