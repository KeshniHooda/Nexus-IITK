Turnitin

 

The Institute has subscribed to Turnitin for use by students and faculty to check originality in their work.

 

Frequently asked questions about Turnitin and also demo and help videos are available on the website:

www.turnitin.com

 

Please note that only members of academic staff are given an account on both these products. Students are not directly given an account.

 

The instructor can form a class of students, which her students can join. Then the students can directly submit their work for originality reports. The instructors can also upload their own work/or their students' work directly for checking.

 

For getting an account on one or both these products, only members of acadstaff list may mail their request to gopesh[at]iitk.ac.in in the following format:

 

            Subject:  request for creation of account on Turnitin

            Body:      First Name, Last Name, iitk email
