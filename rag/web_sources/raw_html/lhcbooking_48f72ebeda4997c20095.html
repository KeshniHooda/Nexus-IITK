Online Lecture Hall Room Booking System



    The Lecture Hall Complex (LHC) and allied classrooms consist of:

  • 20 rooms in LHC
  • 21 rooms in Tutorial Block (TB)
  • 04 rooms in Western Labs
  • 03 rooms + 02 Drawing Halls in DJAC

    • Many of these rooms are air conditioned and are equipped with state of the art presentation facilities including projectors, LCD screens etc. (these details can be found in the Room Details hyperlink on the left). A room in any of these locations can be booked using the Online Room Booking hyperlink on the left (to view a photo of the classroom or to know the facilities in the room Room Details hyperlink can be used).

    Once a request for a room has been registered using the Online Room Booking hyperlink, the LHC administrator needs to confirm the same and a mail of the same will be sent to your @iitk email account. To log into the system your iitk email ID and password can be used.
