M.Tech Programme

Eligibility Requirements for Admission to Programme:

The applicant must have a bachelor's degree in engineering or a 4-year bachelor's degree in science or a master's degree in science with marks/CPI not below a minimum of 55 percent marks /5.5 (on a 10 point scale).

A valid GATE score is required for admission to the M.Tech./M.S. by Research programme in the case of regular candidates.

The requirement of GATE score is waived for the candidates with M.B.B.S. degree as well as for the B. Tech. graduates from IITs with a minimum overall CPI of 6.5 and a minimum CPI of 8.0 in the last two years in B. Tech.

Procedure for admission in M.Tech. Programme

Admissions to the M. Tech. programme may be made directly based on the GATE scores of the candidates and performance in the qualifying examination, and in addition, some of the candidates may also be called for written tests and/or interviews.
