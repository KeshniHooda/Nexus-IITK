About the Programme

The Centre for Energy Regulation (CER) at IIT Kanpur is engaged in capacity building and regulatory research in the power and energy sector. As electricity markets evolve and procurement becomes increasingly exposed to price volatility and market uncertainty, effective procurement planning and risk management assume growing importance.

The Masterclass on Electricity Derivatives aims to develop a comprehensive understanding of derivative instruments and their applications in electricity procurement and risk management. It covers power market operations, derivative instruments and hedging strategies, Indian and international market developments, and the regulatory framework governing these instruments.

The programme also examines applications in power portfolio management and procurement strategies for generators, traders, and commercial and industrial (C&I) consumers, including captive and open-access consumers, with a focus on opportunities and challenges in India's evolving power market.

Relevance of the Programme
  • ❖ Understand the economic and financial foundations of electricity derivatives and their role in power markets.
  • ❖ Explore power procurement arrangements, market operations, and electricity price formation.
  • ❖ Identify price, volume, and basis risks and understand the principles of hedging.
  • ❖ Examine Indian and international experience in electricity derivatives markets.
  • ❖ Understand regulatory frameworks, market infrastructure, and product-design considerations.
  • ❖ Explore derivatives applications in portfolio management and procurement by C&I, captive, and open-access consumers.
Unique Features
Conceptual & Practice Oriented
Connect derivative fundamentals with practical electricity market applications.
Electricity Price-Risk Management
Explore strategies for managing procurement exposure and market risks.
India-Centric Derivatives Design
Examine reference prices, tenor, settlement, liquidity, and product design.
National & International Perspectives
Compare Indian market developments with international experience.
Portfolio & Hedging Applications
Understand exposure, hedge ratios, and risk–return trade-offs.
Industry-Relevant Learning
Focus on generators, traders, and C&I, captive, and open-access consumers.
Residential & Interactive Format
Three days of focused learning and in-person interaction at IIT Kanpur.
Programme Outcomes
  • ❖ Develop trading strategies and understand participation in electricity derivatives market.
  • ❖ Understand the characteristics and applications of forwards, futures, options, CfDs, VPPA and banking.
  • ❖ Identify key procurement risks and apply hedging principles.
  • ❖ Evaluate derivatives applications for RE generators, C&I consumers, data centers, traders and DISCOMs.
  • ❖ Identify opportunities to minimize power procurement costs through effective trading and hedging strategies.
Who Should Apply?

Officials from electricity regulatory commissions; professionals from generation, transmission, distribution, and trading companies merchant power plant renewable energy and energy storage developers power exchange participants and market analysts C&I, captive, and open-access consumers professionals from financial institutions, banks, consulting firms, investment funds, and risk-management teams researchers, policymakers, and other stakeholders interested in electricity markets and risk management.
