Guideline for Biometric attendance

  1. LHC biometric office will hand over the devices to the TA/Instructor in the LHC room just before the beginning of the lecture.
  2. The instructor is requested to take manual attendance for those students whose fingers are not been detected, student should be asked to visit the biometric unit at LHC for biometric registration in front of LHC offce.
  3. The devices will be collected after completion of the biometric.
  4. If any student missed the attendance, the biometric unit at LHC will not entertain the request of the student without a written approval from the Instructor.
