Item 13: To consider the request of Head, Dept. of MSE for change of minimum course work from 8 to 10
courses  to  those  students who  will  join  the PhD  program  with  qualifying  degree  of
B.Tech./BS/M.Sc.

The SPGC considered the request of Head, Dept. of MSE for change of minimum credits through course
work from 72 to 90 credits to those students who will join the PhD program with qualifying degree of
B.Tech./BS/M.Sc. After discussion, committee recommends for change of template of minimum
credits requirement through couse work from 72 to 90 credits for PhD program in Material
Science Engineering with B.Tech./BS/M.Sc. qualification.

Item 14: To consider the request of Mr. Rahul Kumar Gautam (14104125) M.Tech. student, Dept. of
Electrical Engg. for the semester 2015-16-II for joining of job. He has not yet completed minimum
credit requirements for his program.

The SPGC considered the request of Mr. Rahul Kumar Gautam (14104125) M.Tech. student, Dept. of
Electrical Engg. for the semester 2015-16-II for joining of job. He has not yet completed minimum credit
requirements for his program. After discussion, committee turned down his request.

Item 15:  To discuss the proposal to award M.Tech./MS degrees with PhD degrees to those students who
are admitted to PhD program directly after B.Tech./BS/M.Sc. degrees.

The SPGC discussed the proposal to award M.Tech./MS degrees with PhD degrees to those students
who are admitted to PhD program directly after B.Tech./BS/M.Sc. SPGC, in principle agreed with it.
However, modalities have to be worked out to determine the eligibility for getting M.Tech./MS/MA
degree. Some  of  the  suggestions  discussed  in SPGC were  completing  the minimum  course
requirements for the degree, submitting some report such as some project report, literature survey etc.
generally done for SOTA seminar etc.
