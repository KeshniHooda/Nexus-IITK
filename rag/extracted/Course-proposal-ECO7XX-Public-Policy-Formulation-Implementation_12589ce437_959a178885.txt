6. Course Description:

A) Objectives: The course introduces students to the theoretical constructs of public policy and
illustrates how it is applied in India with case studies relevant to India. The objective of this
analytical course is to prepare students for critical evaluation of public policy issues and to make
them informed participants in the related constructive dialogue.
