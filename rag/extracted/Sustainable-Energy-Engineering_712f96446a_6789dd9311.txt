RESEARCH

Five research verticals

The five verticals of the department follow energy from production to the end of its life. They overlap, and many of our faculty
members work in more than one. Our research ranges from basic science to working prototypes.

05  Critical materials and recycling

Critical minerals  · Urban mining  ·  E-waste recycling  ·  Circular economy  ·  Materials for clean energy

Clean energy depends on critical minerals such as lithium, cobalt, nickel, and the rare earths. We work on securing their supply,
recovering them from electronic, battery, and solar panel waste, and developing the materials and processes of a circular economy.
