OR NATIONAL CONFERENCE

attending the conference). The application form should be signed by
the thesis supervisor, DPGC, and HOD, and can be downloaded
through the below link.

https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/Form-Financial-Support-for-
Participation-in-I-C-Revised.pdf

https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/travel-support-form-national-
conference-Revised.pdf

2.    Acceptance Letter or Invitation Letter

3.    Abstract copy

4.    Attach a copy of the last semester’s grade sheet

5.    Receipt or letter about the registration fee
