NOTICE

Subject: MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS

BTech-BS students of Y21 batch who wish to apply for make-up examinations due to Medical
reasons for the End Semester Examination 2021-22-II should upload their application on the
Google Form available at the link given below:
https://forms.gle/ZudjTMgfR1WGLnVn7

The application form for the Make-up Examination is available here:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/make_upform.pdf

Requests for make-up examinations on medical grounds should be supported by appropriate
documents such as a medical certificate, medical report etc. The application form for the Make-
up Examination should be filled and sent to the DUGC Convener for his/her recommendation.
The Make-up Examination form duly recommended and signed by the DUGC Convener
should be uploaded on the Google form given above.

The list of DUGC Conveners is available here:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/sugcmember.pdf

Please note that if a student has already appeared in the End Semester Examination (2021-22-I)
in a given course, he/she is not eligible for applying for the Make-up examination in that course.

The deadline for uploading the application form for the make-up examination on the
Google Form is July 9, 2022 (11:59pm).

Applications uploaded on plain paper or applications not recommended by the DUGC
Convener will not be accepted.
