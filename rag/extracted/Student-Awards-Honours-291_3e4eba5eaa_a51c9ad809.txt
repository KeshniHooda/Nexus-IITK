Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Pratyush Arya

Programme and Department: MS/CGS

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Nisheeth Srivastava

Award/Honour: Best Student Paper Award

Host Organization: Pittsburgh, USA

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Pratyush Arya, has won the Best Student
Paper award at the annual SBP-BRIMS conference held during September 20-22, 2023
in Pittsburgh, USA. He won this award for his paper "Understanding Clique Formation
in Social Networks - An Agent-Based Model of Social Preferences in Fixed and Dynamic
Networks", which tries to understand, using cognitive modelling, when networks of
agents succeed and fail at arriving at consensus in their beliefs.
