Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Narendra Singh

Programme and Department: PhD/CHM

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Sandeep Verma

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award

Host Organization: University of Delhi
Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr Narendra Singh has received the best
poster award for his work titled "Peptide-Based Hydrogen Sulfide Responsive
Nanostructures For Biomedical Applications" in recently concluded XV JNOST-2019
conference, held at University of Delhi (Oct 18-21, 2019). This is a flagship meeting for
PhD and postdoctoral scholars in organic chemistry discipline and is also attended by
German and UK participants, by invitation.

His work described engineered, metabolizable soft structures for antibiotic release
mediated by hydrogen sulfide gasotransmitter interaction.

Date: October 28, 2019
