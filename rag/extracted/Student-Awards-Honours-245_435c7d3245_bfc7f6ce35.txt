Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Sreerag Ashok

Programme and Department: PhD/DES
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Nachiketa Tiwari

Award/Honour: Best Paper Award NSA 2023

Host Organization: The Acoustical Society of India (ASI) and Veer Surendra Sai
University of Technology (VSSUT) Burla, Odisha
Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Sreerag Ashok has received the Best
Paper  Award   at   50th   National  Symposium  on   Acoustics  2023  (NSA
2023, https://nsa2023.com/). The Symposium was organized by the Acoustical Society
of India (ASI) and Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology (VSSUT) at Burla, Odisha
during 24-26 February 2023.
Sreerag's oral presentation was titled "Investigating the asymmetry in tension in a tabla
membrane from a musical perspective".

Date: March 02, 2023
