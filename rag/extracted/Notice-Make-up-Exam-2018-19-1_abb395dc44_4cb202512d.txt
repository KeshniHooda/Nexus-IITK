NOTICE

Subject: MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS

The students who wish to apply for make-up examinations for the End Semester Examination

2018-19-1 should submit their application on a prescribed form available in the UG Section, DOAA

Office or downloadable from the DOAA website available at the following link:

http://www. iitk.a c. in/doaa/sugc-forms

Requests for make-up examinations on medical grounds should be supported by appropriate

documents such as a medical certificate issued by the Institute Health Centre.

The deadline for submitting the application form for make-up examination in the DOAA office
is November 29, 2018 (4:00pm).

Applications submitted on plain paper or late applications will not be accepted.
