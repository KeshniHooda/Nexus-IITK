Procedure for academic registration for students whose
Branch Change requests have been approved

Following points are worth noticing for students of 2017 batch who have been
given a branch change:

1) You have to do manual academic registration for the new branch.

2) For this, you need to collect manual registration form from Lecture Hall
L 8 at the Lecture Hall Complex from 9:45am onwards on July 25, 2018.

3)  Fill in the courses that you wish to register for in 2018-19-I Semester.

4) Take signatures of  instructors  for  all the courses other than the
compulsory ones and, finally take signatures of Convener, DUGC and
submit the form in L 8 at the Lecture Hall Complex on the same day i.e.
July 25, 2018.

5) Collect a stamped copy of the registration form from L 8 and proceed for
signing on the appropriate roll register at the DOSA desk to complete the
registration.
