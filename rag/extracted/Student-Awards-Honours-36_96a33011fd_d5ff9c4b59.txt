Award/Honour: Economic Sciences wins the RBI Policy Challenge 2019

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr. Arjun Gupta, Ms. Shreya Shree, Mr.

any department of RBI. RBI Policy Challenge is an initiative of Prof. Raghuram Rajan to
develop the interest of young minds in monetary policymaking.
