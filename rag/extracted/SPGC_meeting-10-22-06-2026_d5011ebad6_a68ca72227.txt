RATINALE FOR PROGRAM

There are two benefits of this program for the Department of Chemistry. The first is an
improvement in the quality of doctoral students. The MSPD students would have completed most of
the courses in the 2-year MSc program and will be strong in their fundamentals when they carry
out their PhD work. The second advantage of this program is that students can get an early start in
their PhD research and thereby complete their PhD within the stipulated period.

Differences from the Current MSPD Guidelines:

The Chemistry proposal has a few differences from the current MSPD guidelines of the institute.
They are:
1. The course template is different.
2. The chemistry department does not want to allow migration of M Sc students to MSPD as
described in Annexure VI of PG Manual.
3. The CPI for migration to PhD is 8.0 instead of 6.0.
4. The summer course description is more detailed than in the Annexure VI of the PG Manual.

MSPD CHEMISTRY: RULES & REGULATIONS AND PROPOSED INTAKE

ADMISSION PROCEDURE
Admission to the Programme is strictly through the Joint Admission Test for Masters, according to the All-
India Rank and the preference as given by the candidate. All the admission rules are identical as the ones for
MSc (2-year) program. The proposed intake is 20 students per year.

SCHOLARSHIP PRIOR TO PHD MIGRATION

1) All MSPD students are eligible to get an MSc scholarship of Rs.6000/- p.m. when in the first semester; for
subsequent semesters they have to have a minimum CPI of 6.5 to avail this scholarship. For a student with a
CPI of less than 6.5, the scholarship will be discontinued until his/her CPI rises to 6.5 or above and shall be
resumed in the following next semester.

2) If an MSPD student has not converted/migrated as PhD student at the end of four
semesters, due to reasons such as backlogs and semester drops, and the CPI is not
less than 6.5, then the scholarship, which had earlier been withheld, may be awarded.
The decision will be taken on a case-to-case basis, depending on the merit.

3) Under normal circumstances, an MSPD student shall receive the MSc scholarship for a total
of four semesters only.

SCHOLARSHIP AFTER MIGRATION TO THE PhD PROGRAM

Subsequent to migration to the PhD program, the student is eligible for the usual PhD scholarship starting
from the 5th semester.

SUMMER TERM

Until MSPD students migrate to the PhD part of the program, they can spend a minimum period of 8 weeks
during every Summer tem1 doing a project on campus with a faculty member of the Department of
Chemistry, IIT Kanpur. If the student wants the stipend to continue in the summer, he/she has to work on the
project. Co-supervisor from within IITK or outside IITK is allowed. However, in case they spend time
outside IITK for the project, they should not get funding from two sources. The MSPhD student can register
for minimum of 18 units and a maximum of 27 units which can be a combination of summer project course
(New course - needs to be approved) and backlog courses, if any. A student who does not do a project is not
eligible for receiving any scholarship for two months during the summer.

MIGRATION TO PhD PART & MSc DEGREE

1) From the perspective of whether MSc or PhD rules applies, the time spent in
completing the MSPD's first 2 years of approved coursework can be split into two parts:
A) First 2 years of coursework: During this period an MSPD student will be governed
by the rules meant for MSc (2-year) students (except for the course template).
B) Third year of coursework: MSPD students migrate to the PhD part of the program

[PAGE 25]
Annexure-D, Page 3/7

provided they have cleared all the prescribed courses during the first four semesters
with at least 8.0 CPI. Furthermore,
(i) If a student has migrated to this part on time (i.e., at end of fourth semester), then
during this period the student will be governed by the rules meant for the PhD students
(except for the course template). The student will be required to find a suitable supervisor for PhD and this
should be approved along with migration to the PhD program.
(ii) If a student has not migrated to this part on time, then the student keeps doing
the coursework meant for the the third year and in addition clears the backlogs in parallel.
The student will be governed by the rules meant for MSc (2-year) students (except for the course template).
(iii) When a student migrates late (after fifth or later semester), then, the student can choose to select
courses in the template that should be counted as their PhD coursework. The
remaining courses of the template should, however, be completed in addition to some
CHM799 credits such that minimum credits each semester is 36. The student will be
governed by the rules meant for PhD students (except for the course template).

2) If a students completes the three-year-coursework but is still unable to migrate (CPI
less than 8.0), then the student can either
(a) exit the program with an MSc degree
(b) continue doing extra elective courses to improve the CPI to 8.0.
The maximum duration in such cases is (6 semester times 1.5) 9 semesters after which the enrolment of the
student from the program will be revoked.

3) MSPD student will get an MSc (2-year) degree after successful completion of the full
three-year-course-template of MSPD program.

MAXIMUM DURATION: Usual PhD timelines will be followed after migration to the PhD program.

RESPONSES
After discussions in SPGC, the original MSPD proposal was circulated to DPGCs for department
feedback on April 14, 2026, and feedback was sought by April 29, 2026. Subsequently, feedback
was received. Based on the feedback, we have suitably modified the proposal. Below we list the
feedback and the department response.

Feedback from Physics Department:
The collective feedback from Physics as follows:

l. MSPD is an approved program of the institute like BT/BS/MSc/MS etc. The timelines and
guidelines shouldbe changed by individual departments for the same program type. If such a
proposal is accepted then any department, in principle, can have its own rules for any other
institute-wide program which of course is not desirable.

Department Response: We agree and have modified the program to be in line with the MSPD
program approved by the institute

2. There is a prescribed CPI cutoff for migration. Changing it to 8.0 will be detrimental to the
department. In fact, with 8.0 CPI, direct PhD admission can be offered without having the MSPD
route.

Department Response: This is the one deviation from the MSPD program of Physics. Direct PhD
admissions in Chemistry are being offered for students with CPI greater than 8.5. The Chemistry
department feels that a CPI cutoff of 8.0 should be there for students to migrate to the PhD
program.

3. PHY799 can not be taken during summer term by students who have not migrated to PhD
program yet.

Department Response: This point has been noted and modified accordingly.

4. What is the need for a new "MSc by thesis" degree. A student failing to migrate to PhD due to
CPI, but finishes all required courses should be awarded MSc degree as an exit option. For PhD
students, Institute already has exit option as MSR degree.

Department Response: This point has been noted and modified accordingly.

5. Not only the rules (CPI/migration/etc) but also the credits per semester should be highly
comparable with other MSPD programs of the institute.

Department Response: We agree and have modified the program to be in line with the MSPD
program approved by the institute, except for the migration CPI.

6. "Scholarship for the remaining period of the PhD program" in page 2 is ambiguous and should be
modified.

Department Response: This has been edited in the revized proposal.

7. Any decision for routine matters on case-to-case basis (page 1) can be challenged (even in the

8. To say that the student finishes M.Sc part in 3 semesters and migrates to PhD, but will get PhD

student migrates to PhD.

Department Response: The revized proposal removes this ambiguity and is in line with the MSPD

9. Conclusion: CHM should first make their program adhere to all the existing approveds of MSPD,
