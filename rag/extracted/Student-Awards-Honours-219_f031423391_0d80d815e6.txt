Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Aqib Khan

Programme and Department: PhD/AE
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Sanjay Kumar and Prof. Rakesh Kumar

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award

Host Organization: IIT Kharagpur (Kolkata)

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Aqib Khan from the AE department has
received the "Best Poster Award" under the "Granular Flows" category in the 16th.
Complex Fluids Symposium – 2022 (CompFlu 2022) held at the Research Park of IIT
Kharagpur in Kolkata from December 19 -21, 2022.
The title of his poster is "Characteristics of shallow granular flow past a cylinder” which
is authored by three other students of Aerospace Engineering - Yash Jaiswal, Adarsh
Kumar and Deepika Chimote.

Date: December 27, 2022
