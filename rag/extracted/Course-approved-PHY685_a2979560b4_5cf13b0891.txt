5.  Proposing Instructor: Arjun Bagchi (PHY)
6.  Course Description:

A) Objectives: Quantum field theory is the basic framework of understanding the laws of nature and the
basic building blocks of the universe. This is fundamental to all branches of theoretical physics starting from
condensed matter physics to the theory of elementary particles. This course is a first course on the subject
aimed at students who have a background of quantum mechanics and statistical mechanics.
