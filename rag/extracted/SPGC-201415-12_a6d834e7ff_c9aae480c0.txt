Item 11: To consider the request of Mr. Arun Kumar Sharma (13205065) PhD student, Dept. of Mechanical
Engg., on following issues:

i.      Leave from June 22, 2015 to August 26, 2015 on medical grounds.
ii.     Reducing of thesis credits of summer term on medical grounds.
iii.     Underload permission in 2015-16-I semester
The SPGC considered the request of Mr. Arun Kumar Sharma (13205065) PhD student, Dept. of
Mechanical Engg., on above mentioned issues. After detailed discussion, it was decided that (i) it is not
possible to give one month leave during the semester (ii) the thesis credits for summer term can not be
reduced post-facto after the grades were awarded. It is recommended that the student should be advised
by DPGC to seek semester leave for the current semester on medical grounds and should register for next
semester after submitting medical fitness certificate.

Item 21: To discuss on course drop by PG students.

The SPGC discussed on last date of course drop by  all PG students. After discussion, committee
recommends to the Senate that all PG students be permitted to drop a course one month prior to end-
semester examination with  the consent  of  the  instructor  in-charge  of  that  particular course and
recommendation of the Convener, DPGC.

Item 22: To discuss on the issue of overloading the thesis credits during a semester/summer term.

The SPGC discussed the issue of overloading the thesis credits during a semester/summer term. SPGC
decided that no overloading should be permitted in thesis credits. The maximum number of thesis credits
should be 36 in a semester and 18 during a summer term.

Item 23: To discuss on section 2.3 of PG Manual regarding eligibility for admission to PG programmes.

SPGC was informed about inputs received on section 2.3 of PG Manual regarding eligibility criteria from
some of the departments/IDPs (CE, EE, MSE, CSE, EEM & DES). However, since there may be some
problems with the equivalences of degrees, the matter will be discussed in SPGC after receiving details
for other department. If needed, the required approvals may be sought from Senate".
. Item 24: To discuss on reducing of minimum duration for M.Tech. programme from 4 semesters to 3
semesters for students who are not availing any assistantship from the institute/projects.

The SPGC discussed the proposal on reducing of minimum duration for M.Tech. programme from 4
semesters to 3 semesters for students who are not availing any assistantship from the institute/projects.
After discussion and considering the feedback received from various departments, SPGC is not in favour
of reducing the duration of M.Tech. program from 4 semesters to 3 semesters.
