Advertisement

The Thesis Processing Cell (TPC) at the DOAA office, IIT Kanpur is in
need of English Language Editors for its Editorial Help Desk. The desk
caters to PhD students who volunteer to get the pre-submission draft of
their thesis vetted.

Those interested may send their Curriculum Vitae to the Joint Registrar,
Office of Dean, Academic Affairs, IIT Kanpur at jr_doaa@iitk.ac.in or
mdiwakar@iitk.ac.in  or personally submit the same at Room No. 202,
Academic Affairs Building, IIT Kanpur latest by November 29, 2019.

The minimum eligibility requirement is Master’s degree in English.

Prior experience in editing is desirable.

The applicant will be expected to serve on a panel of editors that the
Thesis Processing Cell will contact on a rotational basis for editorial help
with PhD Theses. Payment will be made by the PhD student directly to
the Editor concerned at the rate of Rs. 50/- per page.

The other terms and conditions will be made available at the time of the
interview.

Dean, Academic Affairs
