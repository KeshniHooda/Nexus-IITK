Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Shreepad Ravindra Gunge

Programme and Department: MTech/CHE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Goutam Deo

Award/Honour: Best Oral Presentation

Host Organization: CSIR-IICT Hyderabad

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Shreepad Gunge has been awarded the
best oral presentation at CEES (Catalysis for Energy, Environment and Sustainability)
2024 conference jointly organised by Catalysis Society of India and CO2 India Network
held at CSIR-IICT Hyderabad during 18-20 September 2024.
Mr. Shreepad Gunge is working for his PhD with Prof. Goutam Deo in the Department
of Chemical Engg. The title of the awarded paper is "Effect of metal loading over
Ni/Al2O3 for ammonia decomposition" which is coauthored with Dr. Pradeep Yadav
(post-doc fellow in SEE department) and Prof. Goutam Deo.

Date: Sep 25, 2024
