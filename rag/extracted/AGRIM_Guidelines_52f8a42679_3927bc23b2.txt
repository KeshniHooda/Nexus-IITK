Guidelines for Award for Graduate Research Impact and Merit (AGRIM) Grant

1. Core Concept & Purpose
The IITK AGRIM (Award for Graduate Research Impact and Merit) Grant is an institutional recognition scheme
designed to reward research excellence in the early or mid phase of PhD.
Unlike standard travel allowances or post-thesis awards, AGRIM targets early-to-mid tenure doctoral scholars.
IITK AGRIM Grant is a one-time, merit-based award with grant value of up to ₹1,00,000. This grant is over
and above the standard conference travel grant and is non-renewable and non-transferable. Additionally, this
grant is non-retrospective, i.e., it is effective from the date of issuing this office order.

2. Permitted Uses of the Grant
The AGRIM Grant must be utilized for high-impact academic and research activities. It can be spent across the
following approved categories:

Category A: International Academic Exposure
•  Conference, Workshop, or Symposium Registration Fees
•   Round-trip International Travel & Visa Processing Fees
•   Travel Insurance & Accommodation Expenses
•  Summer / Winter School Fees

Category B: Research & Exchange Visits
•  Foreign / Indian Host Institution Semester Exchange
•  Research Internships
•   Joint Degree Programme (JDP) Mobility Visits

Rules of Utilization: All expenses must receive prior written endorsement from your thesis
supervisor. Final reimbursements and processing are governed by the prevailing IIT Kanpur
Travel Guidelines.

3. Mandatory Eligibility Checklist

Before preparing an application, verify that you satisfy every single condition listed below:
•  Student Status: You are a regular, full-time registered PhD scholar at IIT Kanpur. [Note: PMRF scholars
(past or present) are not eligible for AGRIM].
•  Coursework Timeline: Applicants must have completed  all prescribed coursework in accordance with
Clause 7.1 of the P.G. Manual, from the applicable initial registration date, without dropping any registered
course during a regular semester. For regular PhD scholars, the  initial PhD registration date shall be
considered. For students converting from the MSR/MTech programme to the PhD programme, the initial
registration date in the MSR/MTech programme shall be considered. The same coursework completion

4. Detailed Scoring Matrix (100 Marks Total)
Applications are evaluated using a strict 100-mark selection matrix. You must score at least 80 out of 100
marks to qualify for consideration.

Formula: Total Evaluation Score (100 Marks) = Academic Score (Max 50) + Research
Output Score (Max 50)

Part A: Academic Performance (Maximum 50 Marks)
Your academic performance is calculated directly from your Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) appearing on
your transcript:

Academic Marks = 5 × CPI
•  Example: A CPI of 8.40 yields 5 × 8.40 = 42 marks.

Part B: Research Output (Maximum 50 Marks)
Research output is rated in distinct tiers based on work done at IIT Kanpur. Please see the Appendix for more
details.

Critical Rule (Non-Cumulative Scoring):  If you meet the criteria for multiple tiers, you
receive marks only for the single highest tier achieved. Marks from different tiers cannot be
added together.

6. Application Process & Governance Workflow

Step 1: Self-Audit Eligibility
Verify your CPI and publication status. Calculate your score: Total Score = (5 × CPI) + Research Tier Marks.
Proceed only   if your score  is ≥80, your PhD  registration duration  is under 5 years, your coursework,
Comprehensive Exam, and SOTA milestones are cleared, and you have not yet submitted the PhD thesis.

Step 2: Compile Mandatory Evidence
•   Prepare supporting documents. Collect the following documents into a single PDF bundle:
•    Official Transcript: Showing CPI as on the date of application.
•   Milestone Proof: Clearance dates for Coursework, Comprehensive Exam, and SOTA Seminar.
•   Research Proof: DOI link  / paper PDF for Q1/Q2 publications, OR Patent Certificate + 25% contribution
proof.

Step 3: Supervisor Endorsement
Obtain formal recommendation. Fill out the prescribed AGRIM Grant Application Form. Obtain the signature and
written endorsement of your PhD supervisor outlining the proposed utilization of the grant.

Step 4: Submission & Selection Committee Review
Administrative processing. Submit the complete dossier to the Office of Dean of Academic Affairs (DOAA).
Applications are evaluated by the Selection Committee, comprising the Dean of Academic Affairs (Convener),

Step 5: Sanction & Disbursement
Post-approval execution. Upon committee selection and approval, the DOAA issues an official sanction letter.
