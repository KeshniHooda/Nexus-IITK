Eligibility Requirements:
The eligibility conditions given below are the absolute minimum. Departments may prescribe any requirements over and above.
The
specified minimum
marks/CPI (Cumulative Performance Index), referred to in subsequent sections, implies a minimum of 55 percent marks /5.5 (on a 10 point scale) as long as it is not less than the minimum pass marks/CPI; otherwise, the
specified minimum
marks/CPI implies the minimum pass marks/CPI.
Admission to the Ph.D. programmes will be based on written tests and/or interviews of the candidates shortlisted by the DPGC of the department concerned.
The candidates with bachelor’s degree in Engineering/Design/Architecture or 4-year bachelor's degree in Science must have a valid GATE / CEED / CAT / GMAT / COGJET score or must have qualified JRF/NET, except in the case of the degree from the MOE Funded Technical Institutes (MOEFTIs).
The candidates with master’s degree in Science/Arts/Commerce must either have a valid GATE/CEED/ CAT/GMAT/COGJET score or must have qualified JRF/NET, except in the case of the degree from IITs/ IISERs/NITs with a minimum CPI of 8.0.

Financial Assistanships:
Financial support (in the form of Institute Assistantship) is available to the regular (non-sponsored/ non part time/ non external) students of MTech, MS (By Research), MDes and PhD Programmes.
The student is expected to carry out the assigned duties during the programme. MTech, MDes and MS (By Research) students are currently entitled to a monthly assistantship of Rs. 12400/- for 8 hours of work per week. For PhD students the corresponding financial support amounts to Rs. 37000/- for the first two years, and thereafter, it is raised to Rs. 42000/- for another three years.
Students may also avail financial assistantship from external funding agencies (such as UGC/CSIR/NBHM/DBT/ICAR/ICMR/PMRF etc.).
In addition to the above, several attractive fellowships from Industry/DAE are also available.
