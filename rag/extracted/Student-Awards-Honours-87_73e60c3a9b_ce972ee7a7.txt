Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Anjali Sifar

Programme and Department: PhD/CGS

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Nisheeth Srivastava

Award/Honour: Marr Prize award by the Cognitive Science Society

Host Organization: Cognitive Science Society

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Ms. Anjali Sifar  - has won the Marr Prize
awarded annually by the Cognitive Science Society to the best paper with a student as first
author in the annual meeting of the Society.

She has won the prize for her paper, wherein she characterized fundamental limits on the
predictability of choice behavior in some microeconomic settings, and showed that existing
models of such choice behavior are more accurate in their predictions than nature actually
allows them to be. Thus, her work suggests that the leading models in this field are too good
to be true.
