Internship for Students from Ladakh at IIT Kanpur, IIT Delhi & IIT Bombay

Dated: 16th May 2026

Provisional selected list of students from Ladakh for Long-Term and Short-Term Internship at IIT Kanpur, IIT Delhi, and IIT
Bombay.

Online Applications are invited to the following programs for the Academic Session 2026-27 from students belonging to Ladakh in all areas of Engineering,
Sciences, and Humanities & Social Sciences

1. Short-Term Internship (6-8 weeks duration)

2. Long-Term Internship (4-6 months duration)

There are a total of 30 seats available at the three IITs for short-term internships and 15 seats for long-term internships.

Students for short-term internships would be selected based on their application. However, for the long-term internship, the selection would be based on
application as per the norms of IITs.

The students selected for the short-term would be paid consolidated scholarships of Rs. 15,000/-, respectively.

The students selected for the long-term internships would be paid, Rs. 10,000/- per month respectively.

The last date to apply for the internship program was 22/04/2026 (11:00 PM).

The sponsorship Certificate will be provided by the Government of UT Ladakh.

Please Note:

The offer letter will be issued by the respective IITs to the selected candidates only after verification of all relevant details, including residence and
academic records. If any discrepancies are found in the documents provided, the offer may be cancelled.

All selected candidates must present proof of current enrollment (NOC from the parent institution) or a final degree/degree completion certificate at
the time of joining the institutes.
