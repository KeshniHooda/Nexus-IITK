Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Basil M. Idrees

Programme and Department: PhD/ EE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Ketan Rajawat

Award/Honour: Best Presentation Award

Host Organization: PIERS 2025

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Basil M. Idrees, a PhD student in the
Department of Electrical Engineering has won the Best Student Presentation Award
(3rd  Prize)  at PIERS 2025  for  his work "Constrained  Stochastic Non-Convex
Optimization Algorithm for Machine Learning Problems in Electromagnetic Signal
Processing" held during May 4- May 8, 2025 in Abu Dhabi, UAE. The award includes a
certificate, a cash prize of USD 100, and invitation to a special banquet dinner for the
event.

Date: May 09, 2025
