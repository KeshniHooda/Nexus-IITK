Agenda Item : Suggestions on the ARC report  :

The faculty meeting was called to discuss the ARC report and the
Department’s reaction. The discussion was centered around Table# 7 of
the ARC report. The department’s reactions to the various points (on
which the department had not opined earlier) are as follows : -

1. GRADUATING CPI 4.0: Graduating CPI 4.0 is acceptable to the department.
2. MODULAR COURSES:  Modular courses are a good idea. Two hour mid sem
(only one mid sem) is good.
3. OPTIONAL BTP : Yes with an exit option.
4. ATTENDANCE: Department is in favour of minimum attendance requirement.
5. ABOLISH AP: If a student gets CPI below 4.0 in one semester and goes
to probation in the next semester he gets a SPI below 4.0 then his/her
program will be terminated.
6. BS THROUGH JEE: Faculty is concerned that the four year BS program
will be in conflict with the 3 year B.Sc degree in rest of the country.

Additional comments :
Department is in favour of stating B.Tech in Engg. Sciences. It requires
close maintaining in early stages. Two months courses (which are
multimodular) are o.k.
