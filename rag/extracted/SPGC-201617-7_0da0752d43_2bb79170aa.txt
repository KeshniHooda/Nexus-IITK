Board of Governors, IIT Kanpur on grounds of plagiarism.
The SPGC considered the request and after deliberations it was decided the recommendation of

desired to see the decision of the Board of Governors with respect to the revocation of the
M.Tech. degree of Mr. Singh. The matter will be taken up for discussion in the next SPGC
meeting.

Item 16: To consider the feedback from the departments on guidelines for the award of M.Tech./MS
degree to those students who take a direct admission into the PhD programme after
B.Tech./BS/M.Sc as per the decision of the Senate (491st/2016-17/2nd).
The discussion was deferred as many of the departments are yet to submit their feedback on the
issue.
Item 17: To consider the request of Ms. Ankita Jain (14116261) Ph.D. student, Photonics Science &
Engineering programme, for the award of additional M.Tech degree as per the recent
decision of the Senate with regard to PhD students with a B.Tech./BS background
(491st/2016-17/2nd).
The SPGC was of the view that the provision for additional M.Tech/MS degree would be
implemented with effect from the academic semester 2017-18-I. In view of this Ms. Ankita
Jain’s request could not be considered at this stage. The student would, however, be asked to
make a fresh request once the provision is implemented.
Item 18: To consider the feedback from the departments on the proposal from the DOAA
regarding use of TAs for grading in core and professional courses.
The SPGC  considered  the  proposal.  After  considering  the  views  of  the  various
Departments/IDPs, the SPGC following:

4

[PAGE 5]
(a)  The provision of financial compensation from the DPA for courses outside the IPSA-DIPA
framework may be reconsidered as it does not find favour with most of the departments.

(b)  Since no remuneration is being proposed for the grading work, the departments be advised
to ensure that the total TA work assigned to a student does not exceed the stipulated 8
hours per week.

(c)  PG courses (PhD and/MTech) with a large enrolment of 50 or more be brought under the
purview of the proposal.

(d)  BT-MT Dual Degree final-year students may also be involved in the TA work.

Item 19: To discuss the formats for dropping of P.G. courses and P.G. course registration in S/X

mode as proposed by the Convener, DPGC, Dept. of Civil Engineering.

The SPGC approved the proposed formats with minor changes (Annexure-1 and Annexure-2).

Item 20: Any other item with the permission of the Chair.

i.      The SPGC considered the request of Mr. Tuhin Dolai (16106034) M.Tech. student, Dept.
of Materials Science & Engg. for leave on medical grounds for the semester 2016-17-II.
After deliberation, SPGC recommended leave for the semester 2016-17-II on medical
grounds. The recommendation is to be sent to the Chairman, Senate for approval.
ii.     The SPGC considered the proposal from DPGC Convener, Dept. of Mechanical Engg., for
a new category of PG courses, PGO (postgraduate option), similar to ESO/BSO in the
undergraduate programme, to be conducted through multi-department collaboration. After
deliberation, it was decided that the proposal be sent to departments/IDPs for feedback.
iii.     The SPGC considered the formats for the Thesis Board and Evaluation Report for MS by
Research (MSR). After deliberation, the formats were approved with suggested changes
(Annexure-3 and Annexure-4).
iv.     The SPGC considered a modification in the procedure for M.Tech. thesis evaluation
whereby the thesis examiner would have the option of reviewing the thesis in soft copy.
After deliberation, the proposal was approved.
v.     The SPGC considered the request of Ms. Seema Kumari (15223262) Ph.D. student, Dept.
of  Earth  Sciences,  for  addition  of  Prof. Andreas  Stracke,  Westfalische Wilhelms-
Universitate, Institut Fur Mineralogie, Munser, Germany, as a co-supervisor outside IIT
Kanpur. The SPGC recommended the addition of Prof. Andreas Stracke as a co-supervisor
from outside IIT Kanpur. The recommendation is to be sent to the Chairman, Senate for
approval.
vi.     The SPGC considered a modification in the selection procedure of PhD/MTech admission
to foreign nationals. It was decided that the selection committee for such admissions would
have a member from outside the department.
The meeting ended with the vote of thanks to the Chair.
