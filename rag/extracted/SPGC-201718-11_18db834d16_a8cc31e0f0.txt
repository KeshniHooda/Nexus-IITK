(c)  To consider requests for academic extension for the semester 2018-19-I from those P.G. students who have
completed four years (M Tech/M Des.), six years (Ph.D.) and seven years (Part-Time/External/enrolled in Ph.D.
engineering after B Tech/M Sc).

The SPGC considered the requests of 108 PhD students for academic extension for the semester 2018-19-I. The
SPGC was alarmed at the high number of students exceeding the maximum programme duration. Cases requesting
extension for the sixth or more than sixth time were individually discussed. Overall the following recommendations
are made for consideration of the Senate (enclosed as Annexed-1).

The academic programme of all students positively recommended by their DPGC should be extended.

The programme of Mr. Assiz M P (Y8101061) (external) is recommended for extension with strong reservations.
SPGC suggest that this extension be the last one and a strongly worded letter of warning be issued by the DPGC
along with the academic extension letter.

(d) To consider the request of DPGC, Convener, Dept. of Computer Science & Engg. for change in the minimum course
requirement for M Tech (CSE) degree.

The item was deferred for next meeting of the SPGC.

(e) To consider the proposal of DPGC, Convener, Dept. of Biological Sciences & Bioengineering, for time frame for
thesis submission of M Tech programme under Section 7.8 of exit from PhD programme with M Tech degree.

The SPGC considered the proposal of DPGC, Convener. After detailed discussion, the SPGC opined that students
permitted by the Senate to exit the PhD programme with an M Tech degree should submit M Tech thesis as soon
as the six months ‘waiting period’ gets over. Such students shall receive their assistantship upto a maximum of only
seven months from the date of applying for the exit option.

The meeting ended with a vote of thanks to the Chair.
