Course Objectives:

•  The course introduces the brief process of execution of works and procurement of
materials through contracts with emphasis on different modes of execution, types of
bids, parties for the contract and the required ethics.

•  Present a brief over view of the applicable laws like Indian Contract Act, Arbitration
and Reconciliation Act etc

•  Present the structure and broad content of the bid documents in relation with the
applicable laws.

•  Present the following processes being followed by various departments with case
studies:
o  Bid evaluation
o  Entering into the contract
o  Managing contracts
o  Managing the change requests
o  Dispute resolution

Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet:

The emphasis of the course is on the contract management. Initially, the course introduces
brief process of execution of works and procurement of materials through contracts with
emphasis on different modes of execution, types of bids, parties for the contract and the
required ethics. Subsequently the course delves into the applicable laws and their judicious
use in the preparation of bid documents. The bid evaluation process and contracting process
will be explained. The key aspects of contract management will be explained with emphasis
on change requests and extension of contracts based on delay analysis. Then the course delves
into Dispute resolution process and the importance of avoiding disputes and administrative
measures to be taken to avoid the disputes. Case studies related to Contract Management
and Arbitration will be presented for the better application of the applicable laws and contract
conditions.
