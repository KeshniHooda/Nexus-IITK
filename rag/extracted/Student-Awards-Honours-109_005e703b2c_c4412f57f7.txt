Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Sneha Gupta

Programme and Department: PhD/BSBE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Ashok Kumar

Award/Honour: Best Oral Presentation award -ICBMI 2020("Prof. S. Rajeswari award")

Host Organization:
Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Ms. Sneha Gupta, Ph.D. student BSBE under
supervision of Prof. Ashok Kumar has been awarded the "Prof. S. Rajeswari award" for
the best oral presentation in the recently concluded "International Conference on
Biomedical Materials Innovation (ICBMI) 2020" held as e-conference in 30th Annual
Conference of the Society for Biomaterials and Artificial Organs India (SBAOI) from 6th
December to 9th December 2020. She presented her research work titled "Periosteum
mimicking tissue-engineered composite for treating periosteum damage in critical-
sized bone defects" as an oral presentation.

Date: December 23, 2020
