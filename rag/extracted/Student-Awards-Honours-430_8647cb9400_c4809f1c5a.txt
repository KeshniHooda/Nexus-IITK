Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Harish Ranot

Programme and Department: PhD/MSE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Anish Upadhyaya

Award/Honour: Best Oral Presentation Award

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Harish Ranot has received the 'Best Oral
Presentation Award'  in the  "Physical Metallurgy" theme of 6th  International
Conference on Processing and Characterization of Materials (ICPCM 2024), organized
by the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, NIT Rourkela from
December 5-7, 2024. The title of his presentation was "Optimizing Ni-Fe-Cu Ternary
Binder Composition for Enhanced Mechanical Properties in Liquid Phase Sintered
Molybdenum Alloy" and is coauthored with Prof. Anish Upadhyaya.
