Brief Description of the Award/Honour Sharath H. Padmanabha, Fahad Shaikh,
Mayank Bansal, Debanjan Chatterjee, Preeti Singh have been selected as the best
paper in the 16th Innovations in Software Engineering Conference to be held in
February 2023. the paper titled ``Advances in Automated Pedagogical Error Repair''.
The paper is about innovative use of machine learning techniques typically used in
ranking and recommendation systems to perform program repair on erroneous
code submissions by students in our ESC101 course. The system isbeing integrated
into the Prutor platform.
