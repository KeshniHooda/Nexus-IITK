Item 9: To consider the appeal for reinstatement in the M.Tech. programme of Mr. Ashish Sharma
(Y6202001) Dept. of Chemical Engg. His M.Tech. programme was terminated due to unauthorized
leave, under section 5.7 of the PG manual.

The SPGC noted that Mr. Sharma is a student of the Y6 batch, whose prgramme was terminated six years
back. It was felt that it would be inappropriate to reinstate a student with a one-time academic extension
to account for a gap of eleven semesters. Hence, SPGC does not recommend reinstatement of Mr.
Sharma in the M.Tech. programme.

Item 10: To consider the appeal for reinstatement in the M.Tech. programme of Mr. Sayantan Marik

programme under part-time scheme. As the candidate had not submitted the NoC issued by his
organization along with his application, the SPGC could not take a view on whether the said NoC was

action in this regard.

iii.    The SPGC considered the requests of the following M.Tech. students for change of academic registration
from M.Tech. to Ph.D. programme  in their respective departments, under clause 4.6 of the PG manual.
After discussion, committee approved the change of academic registration from M.Tech. to Ph.D.
programme of all these students in their respective departments. The matter will be reported to Senate.

1.  Aarush Mohit Mittal (14118001) Dept. of BSBE
2.  Ropesh Goyal (15103033) Dept. of CE
3.  Ramswarup A Bhaskar (14114019) Dept. of IME

iv.  The SPGC discussed implementation of the newly approved name for M.Tech. in Earth Sciences as
M.Tech. in ‘Geological Technology’ with effect from the graduating batch in 2016. After detailed
discussion, SPGC took the view that the issue concerned interpretation of a decision of the Senate, and as
such, did not come within the purview of SPGC. It was decided to refer the matter to the Senate.
v.   The SPGC considered the appeal of Mr. Shubham Sahai Srivastav (12211063), Dept. of Computer Science
& Engg., for reinstatement in the Ph.D. programme with an option to exit the programme with M.Tech.
degree. His programme had been terminated by the Senate because the student could not clear his
comprehensive examination within the stipulated time as per Section 8.2 of the PG manual. After
discussion, committee recommended to the Senate the reinstatement of Mr. Srivastav’s programme, with
permission to exit with M.Tech. degree as per Section 7.8 of the PG Manual.
