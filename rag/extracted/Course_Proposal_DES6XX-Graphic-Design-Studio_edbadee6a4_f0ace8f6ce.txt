5. Course Description:

Graphic Design Studio introduces students to the process of developing visual
communication solutions for different contexts and audiences. The course covers visual
identity and logo design, branding, communication collaterals, packaging design, motion
graphics, and signage & wayfinding systems. Students learn to translate concepts into visual

forms using image, typography, colour, elements of composition and visual narratives. The
course emphasises research, concept development, visual exploration, design refinement and
presentation.

6. Objectives

The course aims to develop conceptual, visual and technical skills required to address graphic
design problems. The objectives are to:

1. Understand the principles and processes involved in developing graphic design solutions
for different communication contexts.
2. Develop skills in identity and logo design, branding and communication collaterals.
3. Learn and communicate concepts effectively.

4. Develop the ability to conduct visual research, generate concepts, evaluate design
alternatives and refine design outcomes.
5. Design and develop graphic design solutions supported by research, and rationale for the
intended audience.

Pre-requisites:

None. Basic familiarity with design processes and the principles of visual design is desirable.

Learning Outcomes:
Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to:
•  Explain and apply fundamental graphic design principles to visual communication
problems.
•  Develop visual identities, logos and branding systems based on research and concept

development.
•  Design coordinated communication collaterals using appropriate design language.
•  Develop packaging concepts that integrate product information, visual identity and
structural considerations.
•  Create basic motion graphics using principles of timing, movement, sequencing and visual
narrative.
•  Develop signage and wayfinding proposals that respond to user needs, spatial conditions

and information requirements.

Assessment Criteria and Weightage:
• End Semester Exam: 10%
•  Assignments: 50%
•  Presentations: 20%
•  Project: 20%

8. Short Summary for Course Booklet

Graphic Design Studio introduces students to visual identity, logo design, branding,
communication collaterals, packaging, motion graphics, and signage and wayfinding.
Through visual research, case studies and studio projects, students will learn to develop and
present graphic design solutions for print, digital and physical environments.
