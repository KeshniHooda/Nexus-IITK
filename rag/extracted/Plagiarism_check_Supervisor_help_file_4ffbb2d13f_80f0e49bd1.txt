In this module

• The module also provides an option to upload the Similarity Report.
• The report can be uploaded either by the student or the supervisor/co-
supervisor.
• Uploading the  Similarity Report  is optional  for both student and
supervisor – it is not mandatory.
•  If the report is uploaded, theThesis Processing Cell will cross-verify it.

4

[PAGE 5]
Supervisor / Co-Supervisor Responsibilities

• Check that the student’s thesis, form details and uploaded documents are
correct.
• Verify the Similarity Report if it has been uploaded (when required).
• Approve the thesis submission on time to avoid delays in the student’s
submission process.
• Provide the declaration on time once the plagiarism check is within the
permissible limit (as confirmed by the PPD).

At this stage of process

• Declaration Submission Only: In this stage, you are allowed to submit only
the declaration. No other actions are permitted.
• No Document Changes: Document cannot be modified, replaced, or
removed during this process.
• No Form Edits: The form becomes non-editable at this stage. You will not
be able to make any corrections or updates to the details already entered.

Conclusion

•  Supervisors and Co-Supervisors are responsible for endorsing students' plagiarism
reports and ensuring compliance with academic integrity policies. By following the
outlined process, they contribute to maintaining the quality and credibility of research
submissions.

•  For any issues, supervisors can contact the PPD Cell (ppd_doaa@iitk.ac.in) for
assistance.

•  For technical issues, supervisors can contact Pingala Support (pingala@iitk.ac.in) for
assistance.
