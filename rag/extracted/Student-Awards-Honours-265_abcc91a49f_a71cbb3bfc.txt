Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Dr Apurva Panjla

Programme and Department: PhD/CHM
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Sandeep Verma

Award/Honour: The 2023 IUPAC- Solvay International Award for Young Chemists for
the Best Ph.D. Thesis

Host Organization: The Hague, Netherlands

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Dr Apurva Panjla has received The 2023
IUPAC- Solvay International Award for Young Chemists for the Best Ph.D. Thesis.
She will receive a commemorative plaque and a cash prize as part of The IUPAC–Solvay
Best Ph.D. Thesis Award and will be invited to present a poster at the 49th World
Chemistry Congress (WCC), at the World Forum, The Hague, Netherlands (August
2023), and contribute a review article in Pure and Applied Chemistry.
This is an outstanding recognition to be selected from ~60 nominees from over 27
countries. The IUPAC–Solvay Award Selection Committee consisted of several members
of the IUPAC Bureau and a Senior Science Advisor from Solvay.

Date: May 01, 2023
