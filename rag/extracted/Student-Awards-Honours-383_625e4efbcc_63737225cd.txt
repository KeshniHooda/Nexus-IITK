Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Priyanka Maity

Programme and Department: PhD/EE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Aditya K. Jagannatham

Award/Honour: QIF India 2024

Host Organization:

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Priyanka Maity has been awarded the
prestigious Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship (QIF) India for the year 2024.  This is
awarded for her proposal titled "Delay Alignment Modulation (DAM) for Ultra-High
Data Rates in Next Generation mmWave and Terahertz MIMO Systems", coauthored
with Prof Aditya K. Jagannatham. This proposal focuses in advancing Terahertz (THz)
communication technology to achieve ultra-high data rates in next generation 6G
wireless systems.

Date: Jul 24, 2024
