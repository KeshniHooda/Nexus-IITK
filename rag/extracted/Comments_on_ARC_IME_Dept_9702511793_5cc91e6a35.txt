Department of Industrial & Management Engineering

Comments on ARC

IME department views on the Interim Report of ARC are as below.

1.   The Institute must introduce the BTech-MBA dual degree.

2. In order to provide wider options to students, the department is
contemplating a course on 'Introduction to Management'; details
will be worked out later.

3. The minimum attendance requirement of 80 % is not necessary. The
department feels that there are other mechanisms, such as quizzes
and in-class discussions and presentations to ensure attendance.

(Prof Narendra K Sharma)
