Brief Description of the Award/Honour:  Mr. Sujit Kumar Muduli and Mr. Gourav
Takhar along with their supervisor Late Professor Pramod Subramanyan has been
selected to receive the IEEE/ACM William J McCalla best paper award in the front-end
category of this year's IEEE/ACM International Conference on Computer Aided Design
(ICCAD 2020). The paper is titled "HyperFuzzing for SoC Security Validation". This award
will be given during this year's ICCAD conference scheduled to commence on 2nd
November.ICCAD is considered to be one of the most rigorously reviewed conferences
in the area of computer-aided design.
