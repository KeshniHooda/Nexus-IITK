Subject   :    Extension of Financial Assistantship for PhD Scholars (2018 – 2019 – I)

This is in reference to Letters No. MHRD/TS-1 F.No. 17-2/2014-TS-1 dated September 01, 2015 from

MHRD, Govt. of India, on revision of rates of PhD scholarship in AICTE funded and Centrally Funded

Technical Institutions under the Ministry of Human Resource Development. In this letter it was clarified

that PhD students are to receive a fellowship (JRF/SRF) uniformly for a duration of 5 years, irrespective of

whether they are B Tech / M Sc or M Tech degree holders.

According to the current procedure for assistantship extension, the following applies:

1.    For assistantship  after 5 years, the student may be considered for the award of a Research
Studentship. This award will be valid until the date approved for or the date of submission of thesis,
whichever is earlier. This extension will be given on a semester-to-semester basis (max. for one
additional year) against the application of student. The extension will be based on recommendations
of the Peer Group Review Committee/Doctoral Monitoring Committee in each case.

2.    The Heads are requested to identify a Peer Group/Doctoral Monitoring Committee for each student
and ensure that the report of the Peer Group/Doctoral Monitoring Committee is submitted along with
the recommendations for extension of assistantship/research studentship.

3.    Please find enclosed the proforma for application. Copies of this may be made available to Ph.D.
students who wish to apply for Research Studentship. Please note that the student should fill up the
form and submit all enclosures to DPGC, who should coordinate with supervisor, Head and Peer
Group/Doctoral Monitoring Committee. The comments and recommendation of supervisor, Head,
and Peer Group/Doctoral Monitoring Committee should not be shared with student.

You are requested to please bring this information to the notice of all concerned students and their Thesis
Supervisors.

All Applications for extension of assistantship should reach this office by 31 / 07 / 2018. Applications
received after last date will not be considered.

(Achla M. Raina)
