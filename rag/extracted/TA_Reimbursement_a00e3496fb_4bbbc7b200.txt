OFFICE ORDER

Subject: TA Reimbursement for FARE Fellows

In order to streamline the reimbursement process, it is hereby notified that FARE Fellows must submit
a single consolidated TA claim form that includes the following:
•  TA Bills
•  Visa Fees
•  Contingency Expenses
•  Registration Charges for Conferences/Workshops

Please note that prior travel approval as per FARE norms need to be taken, without which, the TA
reimbursement will not be processed.
The combined TA claim form must be routed through the respective Department Office to the DOAA

office. Each submission should be duly endorsed by the Fellow’s Supervisor and must be
accompanied by original invoices and relevant travel documentation. Split or partial TA claims
submitted separately will not be accepted.

This is effective immediately and is mandatory for all current and future FARE Fellows.
