6. Course Description:

A) Objectives: This course aims to deliver a practical exposure to the use of engineered products
used in healthcare delivery to familiarize the students with the engineering and product space for
biomedical engineering. The course will help students to understand the following topics: the current
state-of-the art biomedical devices and diagnostics used in different clinical practices; the gaps and
issues faced by doctors in such practices and finally thinking about possible ways of solving such
gaps using concepts combining different engineering domains.

There is no recommended textbook for this course. This is an experiential learning course.
The students will observe, interact with clinicians and practicing biomedical engineers. The
