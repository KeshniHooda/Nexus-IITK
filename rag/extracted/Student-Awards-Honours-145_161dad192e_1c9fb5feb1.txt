Student’s Name: Ms. Apala Banerjee

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Ms. Apala Banerjee was awarded the First
Prize in the Best Female Student Paper Category in the recently held International
Microwave and RF Conference (IMaRC 2021). IMaRC is the flagship conference of the
IEEE Microwave Theory and Techniques Society (MTT-S) which is held annually in India
for the international academic, scientific, and industrial community working in the
broad areas of RF and Microwave engineering.
The  title  of the paper was  "Wireless CSRR Based  Planar Sensing System  for
Sensitivity Evaluation of Dielectric Materials in Nearfield ISM Band".

Date: December 25, 2021
