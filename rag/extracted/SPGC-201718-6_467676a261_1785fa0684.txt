(b)     Registration in PHY663 in dual numbering mode with PHY422
The SPGC considered the cases of MSc-PhD (dual) degree students of the Dept of Physics for registering in
course no PHY663: Mathematical Methods II. This course has been running in dual numbering with PHY422.
Dual numbering of courses is not approved by the Senate. Keeping in view the interest of the students as well as
prevalent practice with regard to PHY663, SPGC approved registration in PHY663 in dual numbering mode
ONLY as one time exception. Such an exception will not be made in future and DPGC, PHY must get formal
approval for PHY663 for it to be offered in future.
