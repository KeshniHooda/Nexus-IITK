(i)    DA: JRFs, SRF and Research Associates will not be entitled to DA.
(ii)   House Rent Allowance (HRA): All research fellows may be provided hostel

from time to time would be available to female candidates in all categories.
(v)  Bonus & Leave Travel Concession: JRFs, SRFs, and Research Associates will not
be entitled to these allowances.
(vi)  Retirement Benefits: JRFs, SRFs, and Research Associates will not be entitled to
these benefits.

SupervisoriAdvisor.  lt should be ensured by the fellow that the assistance provided by the
