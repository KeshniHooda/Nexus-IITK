Course Description:
Designers professionally work across independent practices like studios, consultancies, startups and
multidisciplinary teams in a corporate environment. Designing meaningful solutions for the users / society needs
to be complemented by ability to identify opportunities, engage with external clients, define projects scopes, plans
and manage design processes to create and communicate value upstream and downstream and eventually build
sustainable professional practice.

The course combines conceptual input, case discussions, practical exercises and a simulated consulting
engagement. It aims to equip future design entrepreneurs with the knowledge, tools and confidence to take steps
towards professional practice and build a sustainable design practice.

It exposes fresh designers to how professional design work is initiated, proposed, priced, organized,
communicated and delivered in real client contexts.

Course Objectives:
•   Introducing students to different pathways to professional design practice
•   Develop the ability to understand their strengths, and position & communicate design practice to potential
clients and collaborators
•   Develop foundational skills to identify opportunities, understand businesses, interpret briefs and define scope.
•   Prepare professional design proposals, estimating time and efforts, planning, resource allocations and
managing projects
•   Learn how to navigate and understand legal and financial documents like NDA, Intellectual Property, Payment
terms etc.
•   Build awareness and professional responsibility, needs for specialist collaborations and towards larger goals
like society

Learning Outcomes :
1.   Articulate an emerging area of design practice and value creation
2.  Business modeling and positioning with supporting portfolio, case studies
3.   Identify potential clients, business development and project discovery
4.   Translate a client brief into a designed scope, deliverables and project approach
5.  Prepare basic consulting proposal with professional fees
6.  Apply best project management practices, communication strategies, risk mitigations and collaboration with
external experts
7.  Learn how to read legal and financial documents for informed practice.
