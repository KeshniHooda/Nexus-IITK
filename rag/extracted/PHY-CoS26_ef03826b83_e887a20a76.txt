the  previous  modular  Thermal  Physics
course  (PHY210M)  and   includes  an
introduction to microscopic thermal physics
in the larger version. This course is designed
to offer a first course of thermal physics to
undergraduate physics  students. The  first
half of the course will be dedicated to the
systematic development of thermodynamics
PHY211        11 [3-1-0-0]         Thermal Physics    from a physicist’s perspective whereas the
second  half  is  mainly  dedicated  to  the
introduction of microscopic understanding
of the same in the framework of kinetic
theory. The course offers an avenue towards
advanced   topics   like   non-equilibrium
thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, soft
matter physics etc. An attempt to present
entropy in the general context of multiplicity
and information theory is also included.
