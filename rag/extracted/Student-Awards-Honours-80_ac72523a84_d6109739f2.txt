Student’s Name: Mr. Harsh Chittora

Programme and Department: BT-MT/EE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Sundar K. Iyer

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award at IWPSD 2019

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr. Harsh Chittora working from Samtel
Centre (SCDT) with Prof. Sundar K. Iyer for his M.Tech thesis was among the recipients
of a ‘best poster’ award at the International Workshop on the Physics of Semiconductor
Devices (IWPSD 2019) held in Calcutta from 17th to 20th December, 2019.
The topic of Harsh's presentation was "Equivalence between constant phase element
and transmission line models in impedance spectroscopy analysis". His presentation
was made in the “Photovoltaics, Flexible and Organic Devices” poster session at the
workshop.

Date: January 10, 2020
