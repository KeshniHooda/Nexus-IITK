NOTICE

SUBJECT: MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS OF THE END SEMESTER EXAM OF 1ST HALF MODULAR
COURSES

Makeup exam date: Sunday, March 2, 2025 (only).

Students who wish to apply for make-up examinations due to medical reasons for the End Semester
Examination of 1st half modular courses offered in 2024-25-II should upload their application on the
Google Form available at the link given below:

https://forms.gle/Fh9SKtcBEnMXParEA

The application form for the Make-up Examination is available here: Makeup form

Requests  for make-up examinations on medical grounds should be supported by MEDICAL
CERTIFICATE issued preferably by the Institute Health Centre. The application form for the Make-
up Examination should be filled and sent to the DUGC/DPGC Convener for his/her recommendation.
The Make-up Examination form duly recommended and signed by the DUGC/DPGC Convener
should be uploaded on the Google form given above.

The list of DUGC/DPGC Conveners is available here:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/sugcmember.pdf
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/spgcmembers.pdf

Please note that if a student has already appeared in the End Semester Examination of 1st half
modular courses (2024-25-II) of the given course, he/she is not eligible to apply for the Make-up
examination in that course.

The deadline for uploading the application form for the make-up examination on the Google
Form is February 28, 2025 (11:59 pm).
Note: The request for the makeup exam of PHY205M can be made on the Google Form upto March
01, 2025 (09:59 pm).
Applications uploaded on plain paper, sent over emails or applications not recommended by
the DUGC/DPGC Convener will NOT be processed.
