Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Paramita Dasgupta

Programme and Department: PhD/PHY

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Pankaj Jain

Award/Honour: Diploma for the Best Talk at the Young Scientists

Host Organization: Moscow International School of Physics

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Paramita Dasgupta's presentation at the
"Young Scientists' Forum" of the "Moscow International School of Physics (Moscow
ISP) 2020" has been selected as the "Best Talk" by the committee and she has received
a Diploma for the best talk at the HSE study Centre, Voronovo, Moscow, Russia
organized by P.N. Lebedev Physical Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences. Her
visit was funded by NASA.

Her presentation at the Young Scientists' Forum of the Moscow ISP 2020 School was
based on her PhD thesis on the NASA funded Antarctica based ANITA (Antarctic
Impulsive Transient Antenna) experiment that detects the ultra-high energy cosmic
Neutrinos.

Date: April 6, 2020
