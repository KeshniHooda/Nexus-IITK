6. Course Description:

Objectives: The following are the course objectives.
  Understand the necessity for clock and delay generation circuits
  Learn basics of time-to-digital converters
  Understand basics of phase noise and jitter
  Learn to analyze and design delay-locked loops
  Learn to analyze and design ring oscillators
  Learn to analyze and design phase-locked loops
