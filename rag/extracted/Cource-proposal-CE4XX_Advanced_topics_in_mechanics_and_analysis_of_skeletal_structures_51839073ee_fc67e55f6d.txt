7.  Course Description:

A) Objectives: Skeletal structures such as trusses, frames, etc., are widely used in structural engineering
applications such as buildings, bridges, long span roofs, reticulated shells etc. Design of these structural
systems require advanced knowledge in structural mechanics, and matrix structural analysis covering
material and geometric nonlinear behavior. This course aims to cover the necessary structural mechanics
theories, and their implementation using matrix method for the analysis of skeletal structures.
