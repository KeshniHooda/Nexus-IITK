NOTICE

(This notice is meant for BTech-BS students of Y22 batch only)

Subject: MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS

BTech/BS students of Y22 batch who wish to apply for the make-up examinations due to
Medical reasons for the End Semester Examination 2022-23-I should upload their application
on the Google Form available at the link given below:
https://forms.gle/GM4DfvtsjpGmPzMh6

The application form for the Make-up Examination is available here:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/make_upform.pdf

Requests for make-up examinations on medical grounds should be supported by MEDICAL
CERTIFICATE issued by the Institute Health Centre. The application form for the Make-up
Examination should be filled and sent to the DUGC Convener for his/her recommendation. The
Make-up Examination form duly recommended and signed by the DUGC Convener should
be uploaded on the Google form given above.

The list of DUGC Conveners is available here:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/sugcmember.pdf

Please note that if a student has already appeared in the End Semester Examination (2022-23-I)
in a given course, he/she is not eligible for applying for the Make-up examination in that course.

The deadline for uploading the application form for the make-up examination on the
Google Form is February 19, 2023 (11:59pm).

Applications uploaded on plain paper, sent over emails or applications not recommended
by the DUGC Convener will NOT be processed.
