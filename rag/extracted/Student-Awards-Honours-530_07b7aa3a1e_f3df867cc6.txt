Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Deepika Behmani (17109265, Ph.D/PHY)
has received the 'AAPPS-DPP Poster Prize 2025' at the 9th Asia Pacific Conference in
Plasma Physics (AAPPS-DPP 2025) held in Fukuoka, Japan, from 21st to 26th
September 2025. The title of the poster was "Flow field dynamics in an atmospheric
pressure plasma jet: A tale of turbulence and transition." and is co-authored with
Deepika Behmani (presenting author), Pawan K. Karn, Debopam Das, and Sudeep
Bhattacharjee (thesis supervisor).

Date: November 03, 2025
