Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Prabal Pratap Singh

Programme and Department: PhD/IME

Thesis supervisor/mentor: Prof. Deepu philip

Award/Honour: Best paper award

Host Organization: IIM Nagpur

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr. Prabal Pratap Singh received the third
Best Paper Award in the conference `International Society for Data Sciences and
Innovation – Global (ISDSI-G 2021)' for his paper titled as “An iterative outlier-based
sequential filtering approach to extract high impact terrorist attacks data of India & its
neighbors”.
The conference was hosted by IIM Nagpur during December 27 to December 30, 2021,
and academicians from more than 400 institutions from 20 countries attended the
conference. The award carried a cash prize of Rs 10,000 and a certificate.

Date: January 12, 2022
