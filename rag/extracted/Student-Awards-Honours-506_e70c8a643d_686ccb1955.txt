Student’s Name: Ms. Sylvie Rana

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Sylvie Rana presented the paper “Effect
of Phase Gradient and Angle of Incidence Reversal in Phase Gradient Surfaces for IRS
Applications” at the IEEE Wireless Antenna and Microwave Symposium (WAMS), 2025
held during 5-8 June at IIITDM Kancheepuram, India and received WAMS best paper
award. The paper is co-authored with Yallanki Sai Sreenija, Rishi Mishra, Gul Naqvi
and Prof. A. R. Harish.
