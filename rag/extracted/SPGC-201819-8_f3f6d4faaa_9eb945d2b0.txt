6     Any other item with the permission of the Chair.

One member raised the issue of multiple PG-10s which had to be filled this time for admission to the M Tech
program while operating under the COAP protocol. Following a brief discussion, the Chairman was requested
to convey the concern of the SPGC on the confusion and difficulties caused this year to the Dean of Academic
Affairs, and impress the need to streamline the process. Some members also highlighted the need to relook at
the template of the present PG 10 and its utility.
