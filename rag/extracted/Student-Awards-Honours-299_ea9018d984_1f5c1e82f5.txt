Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Ananya P. Mukherjee

Programme and Department: PhD/ES

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Animesh Mandal

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award

Host Organization: CUSAT, Kochi

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Ananya P. Mukherjee has been awarded
the second position for the 'ONGC-IGU Best Poster Presentation Award-2023' at the
'60th Annual Convention of Indian Geophysical Union (IGU)' which was held at CUSAT,
Kochi, Kerala during Nov 22-24, 2023. Her poster was on 'Estimation of Curie depth
and heat flow for central part of Eastern Ghats Mobile belt: insight into non-volcanic
geothermal system'.

Date: Nov 25, 2023
