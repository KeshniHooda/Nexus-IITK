Course Description

This course introduces the analytic theory of modular forms. Students study the modular
group, the fundamental domain, Eisenstein series, cusp forms, the dimension formula, the
Petersson inner product, Hecke operators, and associated L-functions, including their
analytic continuation and functional equation.
