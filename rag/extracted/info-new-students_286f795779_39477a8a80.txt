Scholarship FAQs
What if I arrive before 21st July?
You will have to reside outside IITK as your hostel room will be allotted only on 21st July.
When should we report to campus?
Please note that the date of reporting to campus is 21st July from 7:00 am- 7:30 pm, and rooms will be allocated on arrival itself. Students are very strongly recommended to reach on 21st July, otherwise, they will miss a part of their Orientation.
Orientation will be from 22nd July onwards. Parents Orientation is scheduled on 22nd July itself.
When will I receive IIT Kanpur Email ID and Roll Number?
You will receive an e-mail containing your Roll Number and IIT Kanpur Email ID before the reporting date.
What is Pingala Portal?
Pingala is a comprehensive online portal for IIT Kanpur, designed to streamline administrative, academic, and procedural requirements. Avoid
logging in
before getting credentials, otherwise the account will be locked.The details of account creation in Pingala will be communicated by Dean of Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DDIA) office before your arrival.
Are parents required in the orientation/registration process?
No, parents are not required in any step of the orientation/registration process. However, there is a Parents Orientation planned for 22nd July which the parents are encouraged to attend.
What things to bring to IITK?
Here is a pdf containing a suggestive list of items to bring to IITK:
Items to Bring
Are Parents allowed to stay on campus?
Parents can visit their ward’s allocated hall. They must vacate the Hall at 7:30 pm at night. They must make arrangements for their lodging themself. It's recommended that parents make arrangements in advance.
Will Digi-locker Documents work?
Digilocker files will work for online uploading and verification. But for offline on-campus document verification, we require original tangible documents.
Does any repeater have to upload extra documents?
No. He has to upload the same files as the rest.
From where I can get the details of fees to be paid by me?
Approved fee structure is available at your Pingala web page and
DoSA website
under the head 'News and Events' 'For New Students'.
I had applied for Bank Loan, how I can get the fee structure for the whole course?
Tentative fee certificate for new batch of B.Tech/BS/MSc is available in
DoSA website
under the head 'News and Events' 'For New Students'. However, if you require semester wise fee details, please write to dosarecep@iitk.ac.in.
What kind of fee remissions are allowed?
The fees waiver are given to the following category of students:
- MEB: Most Economically Backward families (income < ₹1 lakh) get 100% tuition waiver.
- OEB: Other Economically Backward families (income ₹1–5 lakh) get 1/3rd tuition fee.
- SC/ST/PWD: Fully exempted from tuition based on category certificate.
No waiver for M.Sc./Ph.D/MBA/M.Des/DIIT students except SC/ST/PWD category.
What is the valid income proof for fee remission/waiver for BS/BT students?
Copy of complete Income Tax Return (ITR) of family (Self, Father, Mother, Unmarried Siblings) and Tuition Fee Waiver Affidavit on Rs. 20/- Stamp paper duly signed by First Class Magistrate/Public Notary as per format available on DoSA website. Submit original affidavit to DoSA office in first semester.
Whether the EWS certificate can be considered valid for fee remission for BS/BT students?
No. EWS certificate alone is not considered. Income proof must comply with Para 4(a).
If a student fails to submit valid income proof at registration?
The student shall deposit full fees at registration and excess fees may be claimed on submission of valid income certificate to DoSA office.
What to do if name mismatch between Institute records and provided income certificate?
Submit Request Letter, Publication of names in Gazette/News Papers for reconsideration of income certificates.
On what grounds can I get refund of excess fee amounts?
Excess fees can be refunded on submission of valid proof within one year through Pingala Portal.
What is the procedure to apply for refund of excess fee?
Apply in Pingala Portal in Excess Fee Refund Module.
I have paid my all fees but Pingala shows 'Not Registered'?
Reasons: a) Scrolling issue on Pingala portal b) Online payment reconciliation c) Payment via RTGS – send UTR to receipts@iitk.ac.in, brsac1@iitk.ac.in, dosaregd@iitk.ac.in. Also check for pending dues and biometric attendance.
How to claim Fee Waiver/Refund as a newly joined student?
1. Upload income certificate in Pingala portal after roll allotment.
2. After DoSA approval, submit excess fee refund form with payment slip through Pingala portal.
3. Refund sent through Account section or TSA account.
Who is eligible for fee refund?
a) Paid fee twice.
b) Paid excess fee due to missing Income certificate.
c) Late Day Scholar approval.
d) State scholarships paid directly.
e) Any other case-specific excess fees.
Procedure for education loan fee clearance?
Bank transfers payment to IIT Kanpur SBI account. Collect advise note from bank and send to receipts@iitk.ac.in, brsac1@iitk.ac.in, dosaregd@iitk.ac.in. For tentative fees, write to dosaregd@iitk.ac.in.
Contact for Fee issues
1. Registration: Sapna Gupta, dosaregd@iitk.ac.in, 0512-679-6945
2. Excess Fee Refund: Sahil Singh Rathour, sdr@iitk.ac.in, 0512-679-6924
3. Parental Income Declaration & Tuition Fee waiver: Mayuk Das, dosahelp@iitk.ac.in, 0512-679-6924
What kind of fee remissions are allowed?
For BS/BTech students: MEB 100% tuition waiver, OEB 1/3rd tuition fee, SC/ST/PWD fully exempted. Income proof includes ITR + Affidavit.
Will my EWS certificate be applicable for Fee waiver?
No, EWS certificate is not applicable for fee waiver.
Do I need to file ITR if I want fee remission and have never filed ITR?
It is mandatory for parents to submit their ITR. ITR can be filed online. Student or siblings submit if asked.
If I have income certificate and affidavit, is ITR/AIS still required?
Yes, ITR + AIS from Income Tax Department is mandatory to receive fee remission.
Is PAN Card compulsory?
PAN Card is mandatory for filing ITR. If zero income, declare PAN number and zero income. If parents don’t have PAN, they must apply for it.
What is AIS/TIS?
AIS (Annual Information Statement) is a detailed statement from Income Tax Department. TIS is condensed version. AIS required along with ITR.
What scholarships are available through IITK?
Visit this link:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/sspc/
Eligibility criteria for scholarships?
Refer to
https://www.iitk.ac.in/sspc/
for eligibility and details.
How can I apply for eligible scholarships?
Notification sent to students. Submit forms to SSPC Office via email (sspc@iitk.ac.in) or by hand at SSPC Office.
Process for signature on External Scholarships?
Submit duly signed forms by applicant & parents to SSPC Office either in hardcopy or scanned manner (sspc@iitk.ac.in). Attach fee receipt of 1st semester and grade sheets till 2nd semester of previous academic year.
When should I apply for scholarships as a first-year student?
First announcement mail sent after semester starts.
Are scholarships semester-wise?
No, all scholarships are yearly.
Do I need to apply every year if I availed scholarship in first year?
Yes, apply every academic year separately.
Can I get Bonafide certificate for scholarship from SSPC?
Yes, only for scholarship purposes.
What kind of no-objection certificates related to scholarships are issued?
Only scholarships related to NOC are issued by SSPC.
Whom should I contact for scholarship queries?
Call 0512-259-6187 or email sspc@iitk.ac.in

Reaching IITK
By Air
The most popular air route to IIT Kanpur is through Lucknow Airport (IATA: LKO | ICAO: VILK). Lucknow is connected by direct flights to and from major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Bengaluru
Note:
Kanpur airport (Chakeri) is now open to civilian purpose. The Airport is located around 21 Km from IITK and cabs are available to and fro.
By Road
Kanpur is connected to NH-2 that runs between Amritsar and Kolkata and connecting cities like Delhi, Agra, Allahabad, Patna. Kanpur is also connected to Lucknow on NH25 and is about 91 km from Lucknow. Cabs are available from Lucknow to reach IIT Kanpur.
By Train
Kanpur is well connected by railway network with nearly every major city. Numerous overnight trains are also present from major cities like New Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore. IIT Kanpur is about 17 km from the Kanpur Central Railway Station.
Direct buses to campus front gate are there from Jhakarkati. Tempos and Autos are also available which take about Rs. 200 - Rs. 250 from Station to IITK campus. One can also find shared auto services (commonly known as Vikram) for as low as 40 Rupees from Kanpur Railway Station, outside Platform 9 to campus front gate.
Metro services are also available from Kanpur Central Metro Station to IIT Kanpur Metro Station from 6am to 10pm daily.

Important Notes for New Students
What if I arrive before 23rd July?
You will have to reside outside IITK as your hostel room will be allotted only on 23rd July.
When should we report to campus?
Please note that the date of reporting to campus is 23rd July from 7:00 am- 7:30 pm, and rooms will be allocated on arrival itself. Students are very strongly recommended to reach on 23rd July, otherwise, they will miss a part of their Orientation.
When will I receive IIT Kanpur Email ID and Roll Number?
You will receive an e-mail containing your Roll Number and IIT Kanpur Email ID before the reporting date.
What is Pingala Portal?
Pingala is a comprehensive online portal for IIT Kanpur, designed to streamline administrative, academic, and procedural requirements. Avoid
logging in
before getting credentials, otherwise the account will be locked.The details of account creation in Pingala will be communicated by Dean of Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DDIA) office before your arrival.

Are Parents allowed to stay on campus?
Parents can visit their ward's allocated hall. They must vacate the Hall at 7:30 pm at night. They must make arrangements for their lodging themself. It's recommended that parents make arrangements in advance.
After paying the seat booking fee for JAM counselling, how do I pay the remaining admission fees?
You will receive an email regarding payment of the remaining admission fees at a later date.
Do tuition and hostel fees get deducted monthly from the stipend?
No. Tuition fees and hostel fees have to be paid semester-wise.
Is the Mess Establishment Charge (MEC) a one-time fee or paid every semester?
MEC has to be paid every semester.
Is mess food charge included in MEC?
No. Mess establishment charges and mess food charges are separate.
What is the average monthly mess food bill for M.Tech students?
It is approximately ₹3000 per month and may vary.
Is electricity included in hostel fees?
No. Electricity charges have to be paid separately every month.
Do we need original documents during admission?
Yes. Original documents mentioned in the offer letter are required during document verification. Students should also bring sufficient photocopy sets.
Is a gap certificate required after a long gap after B.Tech?
No. A gap certificate is not required.
Do SC/ST/Pwd category students need parental income certificate for tuition fee waiver?
Yes. Both parental income certificate and caste certificate are required.
What income proof is acceptable?
ITR or a government-issued income certificate is acceptable.
Are parents' PAN and Aadhaar required?
Yes. PAN card, Aadhaar card of parents are required.
Can I get accommodation if I arrive before 23rd?
Hostel rooms will only be allocated on the day of reporting i.e 23rd July. Please book your own accommodation outside of the campus if you're arriving before 23rd.
Is there any accommodation available for parents?
No, accommodation will only be given to the student. However, parents are allowed to accompany the student from 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM.
What facilities will be available in hostel rooms?
Hostel room will have basic furniture i.e. bed/cot, table, chair and cupboard. Students need to arrange their own Mattress, Pillow, Blanket etc. There are two shopping complex at IIT Kanpur – old shopping complex and new shopping complex for students to buy their essential items. Apart from this each hall have their own stationary shop and canteen as well.
Who is eligible for SBRA accommodation?
Only married candidates are provided accommodation to reside with dependents.
Can unmarried students with dependent family members apply for SBRA?
No. For exceptional cases, contact the DoSA office regarding possible options such as HRA.
SBRA contact details?
Email: dosahelp@iitk.ac.in and dosarecep@iitk.ac.in. Website:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/sbra/
Can we opt to not stay at Hostel?
PG students who want to be day scholars i.e. do not want to stay in the hostels may apply same to headadmin_cmhw@iitk.ac.in, keeping officer2_sa@iitk.ac.in and pg_cmhw@iitk.ac.in in CC.
When will course selection procedure and TA/RA duties be explained?
Details regarding assistantship duties, course selection, and additional courses will be explained during the DPGC meeting. Attendance is mandatory.
Can students take extra courses beyond the prescribed first semester curriculum?
This will be discussed during the DPGC meeting and depends on department rules.
What are typical TA/RA duties?
Students receiving institute assistantship may be assigned teaching or research assistance duties as decided by the department/faculty.
Do I need to buy a bicycle for commuting in the campus?
IIT Kanpur campus is huge so you will need a cycle to commute, there are several bicycle shops in IITK, students can also buy new or second-hand bicycles.
What should new students keep in mind?
Regularly check official IIT Kanpur emails, keep scanned copies of documents, carry originals during reporting, and attend orientation and DPGC meetings.
Reaching IITK
By Air
The most popular air route to IIT Kanpur is through Lucknow Airport (IATA: LKO | ICAO: VILK). Lucknow is connected by direct flights to and from major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Bengaluru
Note:
Kanpur airport (Chakeri) is now open to civilian purpose. The Airport is located around 21 Km from IITK and cabs are available to and fro.
By Road
Kanpur is connected to NH-2 that runs between Amritsar and Kolkata and connecting cities like Delhi, Agra, Allahabad, Patna. Kanpur is also connected to Lucknow on NH25 and is about 91 km from Lucknow. Cabs are available from Lucknow to reach IIT Kanpur.
By Train
Kanpur is well connected by railway network with nearly every major city. Numerous overnight trains are also present from major cities like New Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore. IIT Kanpur is about 17 km from the Kanpur Central Railway Station.
Direct buses to campus front gate are there from Jhakarkati. Tempos and Autos are also available which take about Rs. 200 - Rs. 250 from Station to IITK campus. One can also find shared auto services (commonly known as Vikram) for as low as 40 Rupees from Kanpur Railway Station, outside Platform 9 to campus front gate.
Metro services are also available from Kanpur Central Metro Station to IIT Kanpur Metro Station from 6am to 10pm daily.

Information related to Day Scholar for New PG Students
Freshers who intend to apply for Day Scholar status from the beginning of their programme must not opt for hostel accommodation at the time of reporting.
A refund of hostel fees, wherever applicable, will be processed only after the approval of Day Scholar status for the concerned semester of the Academic Year 2026 - 27. Students may apply separately for the refund of MEC charges, if eligible.
The duly completed and signed copy of the Day Scholar application form (after the generation of Roll No) must be submitted to the DOSA Office (Mr. Saurabh Shukla at
ssac_sa@iitk.ac.in
) with a copy to Mr. D C Mishra (
officer2_sa@iitk.ac.in
) and
headadmin_cmhw@iitk.ac.in
by July 20, 2026, for consideration by the competent committee.
Please mention the following in your email application:
1. Full name
2. Department
3. Degree program
4. IITK Roll No.
5. Must attach the offer letter.
