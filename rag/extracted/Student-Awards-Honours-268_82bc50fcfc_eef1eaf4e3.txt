Student’s Name: Mr. Pushpendra Kumar Yadav

Programme and Department: PhD/PHY
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Amit Agarwal

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award

Host Organization: International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP), Triesty, Italy

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Pushpendra Kumar Yadav has received
the award for the best poster in the International Workshop on "Frontiers in Excited
State Electronic Structure Methods: from Spectroscopy to Photochemistry".
This workshop was held during 16  - 19 May 2023 at the International Centre for
Theoretical  Physics  (ICTP),  Triesty,  Italy. Pushpendra's poster was  titled "Room
temperature electron-hole liquid phase in monolayer MoSi2Z4 (Z=pinctogen)".
