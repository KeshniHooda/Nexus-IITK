Office Order

The computation of the honorarium for the Summer Courses has been revised, as under, with
immediate effect:

Number of                                          Maximum
1          2 to 85      86 and above
students                                                   honorarium
Lectures (3-0-0-0)   Rs. 40,000/-,   Rs. 6,000/-     Rs. 1,500/-        Rs. 7,50,000/-
fixed          per student    per student
Number of                                          Maximum
1       2 and above   Section strength
students                                                honorarium /section
Lab (0-0-3-0)        Rs. 15,000/-    Rs. 2,000/-          40          Rs. 40,000/-
per student
Tutorial (0-1-0-0)   Rs. 15,000/-    Rs. 2,000/-          40          Rs. 40,000/-
per student

Note:

1. The faculty member may choose the quantum of money to be paid to the personal
account and/or PDA.
2.  For courses with the number of lectures/tutorials/lab hours different from the above,
the honorarium will be calculated proportionately.
3.  For modular courses, the honorarium will be proportional.
4.   If a course is shared between more than one instructor/tutor, the honorarium will be
split strictly according to their teaching load.
5.  Tutorials and labs conducted by students will also be compensated according to the
rates proposed here.
6. One TA may be allotted for 40 students. TAs will be paid an honorarium of Rs. 10,000/-
for a course.
7.  Courses with class size less than 10 will require appropriate approval.

All concerned are requested to take a note of the above.

This has the approval of the competent authority.

(Shalabh)
Dean of Academic Affairs
