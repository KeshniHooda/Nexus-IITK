Notes:

1. All academic departments will remain open on December 26, 2015 and July 23,
2016.

2. If the requisite number of lectures cannot be scheduled (requisite number being 40
lectures of 50 minutes each for a 3-lecture a week  full semester course), the
instructors may, in consultation with the students in their respective courses, decide
on a schedule of extra classes.   Extra classes should not to be scheduled on
Gazetted Holidays.

3. No class, quiz, presentation, or any other academic activity can be scheduled on
April 16, 2016, July 09, 2016 and similarly on November 12, 2016.

4. Saturdays and Sundays  falling within the examination period  will be used  for
scheduling examinations.     If the need arises (only in extra ordinary situations)
examinations of some courses may also be scheduled on Sundays  falling on
14.2.2016, 21.2.2016, 17.4.2016, 12.6.2016, 10.7.2016, 11.9.2016 and 14.11.2016.

5. Lectures for modular courses offered in first half of semesters/summer term will
commence on December 30, 2015/ May 17, 2016/ July 27, 2016, whereas lectures
for modular courses  offered  in second  half  of semesters/summer term  will
commence on February 22, 2016/ June 13, 2016/ September 19, 2016.

6. Final examinations of modular courses offered in first half of semesters/summer
term will be conducted during Feb. 15-20, 2016/ June 10-11, 2016/ Sept.13-18,
2016, whereas final examinations of modular courses offered in second half of
semesters/summer term will be conducted during April 18-28, 2016/ July 11-12,
2016/ Nov. 15-25, 2016.

7. Pre-Registration of all modular courses (irrespective of whether they are offered in
first half or second half of the semester) will be done along with Pre-Registration of
regular (non-modular) courses during the dates mentioned in above table.
