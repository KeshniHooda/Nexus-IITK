The SPGC considered the request of Ms. Madhusree Sarkar (13115062) NET programme, for inclusion of Dr. K
Velusamy, Dept. of Atomic Energy, Government of India as a co-supervisor outside IITK. After discussion,
committee has recommended it to Chairman, Senate for approval.

was deferred by last SPGC meeting.

The SPGC discussed the guidelines for operationalisation of IDC courses, referred by Dr. Mahendra Verma. It was
decided to send the proposal to all departments/IDPs for their comments/feedback.

Item 8: To discuss the modification in M.Tech./M.Des Thesis defence form. It was deferred in last SPGC
meeting.

The SPGC considered the modification in M.Tech./M.Des Thesis defence form. After discussion, committee
agreed that the defence form should have a certificate “I/We certify that corrections suggested by the examiners
have been incorporated in the thesis” signed by the thesis supervisor.
