SUBJECT: MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS OF THE MID-SEMESTER EXAM OF REGULAR COURSES AND
END SEMESTER EXAM OF FIRST HALF MODULAR COURSES OFFERED IN THE 2026-27-
1 SEMESTER

Makeup exam date: September 26-27, 2026.

Students who wish to apply for make-up examinations due to medical reasons for the end-semester exam
of the First half modular courses and the mid-semester examination of Regular courses offered in the 2026-
27-1 Semester should upload their application on the Google form available at the link given below:

https://forms.gle/Qv7wbyMiNPTC75MP9

The application form for the Make-up Examination is available here: Makeup form

Requests for make-up examinations on medical grounds should be supported by a MEDICAL
CERTIFICATE issued preferably by the Institute Health Centre. The application form for the Make-
up  Examination  should be  filled  out and  sent  to  the DUGC/DPGC Convener  for  his/her
recommendation. The Make-up Examination form, duly recommended and signed by the
DUGC/DPGC Convener, along with the supporting document (single PDF), should be uploaded
to the Google form provided above.

The list of DUGC/DPGC Conveners is available here:
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/sugcmember.pdf
https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/spgcmembers.pdf

Please note that if a student has already appeared in the exam of a given course, he/she is not eligible
to apply for the make-up examination in that course.

The deadline for uploading the application form for the make-up examination on Google Form
is September 21, 2026 (11:59 PM).

Applications uploaded on plain paper, sent over emails, or applications not recommended by
the DUGC/DPGC Convener will NOT be processed.
