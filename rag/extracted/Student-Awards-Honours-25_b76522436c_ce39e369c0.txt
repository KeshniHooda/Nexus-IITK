Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name(s): Mr. Bidhan Hazra, Mr. Banshi Das and Ms. Nidhi Sorout

Programme and Department: PhD/CHM
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor(s): Prof. Manabendra Chandra and Prof. Amalendu
Chandra

Award/Honour: Best poster Awards

Host Organization: BITS Pilani and IISc Bangalore
Brief Description of the Award/Honour: The following three PhD students of the
Chemistry Department received best poster awards in recent conferences as per the
details given below.

1. Mr. Bidhan Hazra received a ‘best poster award’ for his presentation at the 16th
Theoretical Chemistry Symposium (TCS 2019), which was organized at BITS Pilani,
Pilani Campus, during February 13-16, 2019. Mr. Bidhan Hazra is a PhD student of Dr.
Manabendra Chandra.

2. Mr. Banshi Das received a ‘best poster award’ for his presentation at the conference
“Dynamics at the Interface of Chemistry and Biology” held at IISc Bangalore during
February 18-20, 2019. He is a PhD student of Dr. Amalendu Chandra.

3. Ms. Nidhi Sorout received a ‘best poster award’ for her presentation at the
conference “Dynamics at the Interface of Chemistry and Biology” held at IISc Bangalore
during February 18-20, 2019. She is a PhD student of Dr. Amalendu Chandra.

Date: March 6, 2019
