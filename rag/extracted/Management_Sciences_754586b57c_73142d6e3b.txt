About Us
Greetings from the Department of Management Sciences!!
The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) (erstwhile Department of Industrial and Management Engineering), IIT Kanpur, is first of its kind in the country to leverage the analytical education of engineers to train them into management professionals. Established as an interdisciplinary program in 1974, it subsequently evolved as a full-fledged department in 1988 and given the diversity of its programs from MBA, MTech, PhD, eMasters etc. has metamorphosed into the Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) from Department of Industrial and Management Engineering (DIME).
The department presently comprises a substantial number of faculty members with doctorates & post doctorate qualification from top institutions within and outside India, having interests in diverse fields. The department places strong emphasis on high-impact research published in leading journals in the areas such as operations research, operations management, decision support systems, organization sciences, finance, marketing, economics, analytics, entrepreneurship, energy etc.
The department's approach to management education has always been unique and successfully leveraging and complementing the core strength of IIT Kanpur and its ethos as a numero uno in technical education. Most of our students are placed in leadership positions in fortune 500 entities across the world and our alumni have made us proud by assuming senior leadership positions both in industry as well as academic institutions.
