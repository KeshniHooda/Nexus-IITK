Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Namita Kumari has received the best
paper award in the 4th International Conference on Power Engineering Applications
(ICPEA) in Penang, Malaysia held on 4-5th March 2024. The title of her paper is
"Enhancing Short-Term Load Forecasting: A Hybrid Approach with Prophet and
Custom Feature Engineering." The paper is coauthored with Mr. Ritik Rajput and
Professor Ankush Sharma.
