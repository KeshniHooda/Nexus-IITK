ANNOUNCEMENT FOR THE NEW (Y25) UNDERGRADUATE STUDENTS

INSTITUTE COUNSELLING SERVICE
IIT Kanpur

  Undergraduate students of the Y25 batch are required to report in-person to the institute
on July 21st, 2025 (Reporting Day) between 7:00 am and 8:00 pm.

  On the 21st, the boys to report at Hall 13 and girls to Hall 4.

  Accommodation in the Hall of Residences (hostels) will be allocated to students on the
reporting day i.e., July 21st.

  The Orientation Program, Academic Registration etc. for the Y25 batch is scheduled to start
from July 22nd July 30th, 2025. All Y25 UG students are required to stay on their halls during
the entire duration.

  The commencement of regular classes for the Y25 batch is set for July 31st.

   Parents or guardians are welcome to accompany the students; however, please note that we
do not provide or arrange accommodation for them. A list of nearby hotels can be found here.
They are welcome to spend time with their wards in the halls from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm and
may have meals in the mess on a payment basis. We kindly ask that parents leave the halls by
8:00 pm.

  We sincerely invite parents or guardians (two per student) to the Parents' Orientation
Program scheduled on July 23rd, 2025, from 2:00 PM to 6:30 PM at the Main Auditorium of
IIT Kanpur.

   Please visit the Institute Counselling Service (ICS) webpage
(https://www.iitk.ac.in/counsel/index.php) for information and updates.

   Please join the IIT Kanpur Y25 Discussion Forum Facebook page and follow us in Instagram.
