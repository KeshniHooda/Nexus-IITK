Walk-in interviews: Shortlist

PhD Program, Department of Intelligent Systems
Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur

(GN= General, OBC = Other Backward Class, OBC-NCL= OBC-Non Creamy Layer, SC =
Scheduled Caste, ST= Scheduled Tribe, EWS= Economically Weaker Section, DAP = Differently
Abled Persons)

CPI = Cumulative Performance Index/marks, normalized to 10 and rounded to 1 decimal place.

Candidates are shortlisted for the PhD interviews based on the following criteria:

1.  Candidates applying for direct PhD admission with BTech from CFTI
a.  Candidates should have a CPI greater than or equal to 7.5
2.  Other candidates applying for direct PhD admission with BTech:
a.  Candidates should have a CPI greater than or equal to 8.0
b.  Should have a valid GATE score.
3.  Candidates with Masters degree in Science
a.  Candidates should have a CPI greater than or equal to 7.5, and
b.  Candidates should have a valid GATE/JRF score
4.  Candidates with Masters degree in Engineering
a.  Candidates should have a CPI above the following threshold:
GN/OBC             OBC-NCL/EWS         SC/ST/DAP
7.5                      7.0                      6.5
Please keep checking your emails for announcements regarding the scheduling of interviews.
