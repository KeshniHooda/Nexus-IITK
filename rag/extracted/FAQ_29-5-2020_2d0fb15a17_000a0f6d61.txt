Frequently asked questions (FAQs) on the Senate Guidelines (533rd/2019-
20/9th e-meeting/May 19-20, 2020) for the closure of the current Semester
(2019-20-II)

Q1     Will the semester close without the End Semester Examination for 2019-20-II Semester?

A1    The End Semester Examination for 2019-20-II Semester will not be held. The current semester
will close with grade submission based on the evaluation criteria as determined by the
instructor. This, in most cases, would imply considering the performance up to Mid Semester
Recess. However, instructors may also consider online evaluations, e.g., assignments etc. for
grading if they find these uniformly applicable to the class.

Q2    Can I get a Fail grade in my courses in the current semester?

A2    As a one-time exception, only A/B/C/S grades shall be awarded. D/E/F grades will not be
awarded (See also Q41).

Q3    What does the ‘S’ grade in A/B/C/S grading scheme mean?

A3       ‘S’ is the grade that will be awarded when a student's performance is lower than that deemed
fit for grade ‘C’, as determined by the instructor (See Q13).

Q4     Will the ‘S’ grade count towards completion of credits and SPI/CPI?

A4    The ‘S’ grade will be counted only towards completion of credits. It will not be counted in
SPI/CPI calculation (See Q14).

Q5    Can a student apply for an improvement of the grades awarded in the current semester?

A5    The non-graduating students, who have got B/C/S grades in the current semester have the
option of improving their grades by appearing in a re-examination to be conducted once the
academic activities resume.

Q6     Will it be mandatory to appear for the re-examination?

A6     No, the re-examination is optional and only for the non-graduating students. The non-
graduating students who have got B/C/S grades and wish to improve their grades may apply
for the re-examination.

Q7   When and how can I apply for the re-examination?

A7    A notice for the re-examination detailing the application procedure will be put up on DOAA
website and also communicated via email in due course of time.

Q8    What will be the grading policy for the re-examination?

A8    The normal letter grades A/B/C/D/E/F shall be awarded. This grade revision, if any, will be
considered final.

Q9    What will be the schedule of the re-examination?

A9    The re-examination will be conducted after the arrival of the students in campus for 2020-21
Semester I.

Q10   What will be the syllabus and weightage for the re-examination?

A10   The syllabi and the weightages will be announced by the Course Instructor to his/her class.

Q11   By what time will the grading for the current semester be completed?

A11   The grading for all the courses shall be completed by the instructors by June 20, 2020. The
Grade Submission Portal will be available during June 20 to June 30, 2020. The last date of
grade submission for all the courses is June 30, 2020.

Q12   Can a student ‘opt’ for a grade?

A12    No, students cannot ‘opt’ for grades. It is solely the Instructor’s prerogative to award a grade
to a student based on his/her performance in the course.

Q13   When can an instructor award the ‘S’ grade in the A/B/C/S grading scheme?

A13      If, on the basis of his/her performance in the course, a student is likely to get grades lower
than ‘C’, the Instructor may award the ‘S’ grade (See Q3). Whether a given performance
deserves ‘S’ or the student needs to be de-registered will be determined by the Instructor
based on all the available performance indicators (See Q24).

Q14   A student getting 'S' grade can finish with a higher CPI than one getting 'C' because 'S' does
not count towards CPI. How is this proposed to be resolved?

A14    This will be looked into and an optimal solution will be found.

Q15    Will the ‘S’ grade(s) reflect in the transcript?

A15   The grading scheme of A/B/C/S adopted as a one-time measure for the current semester will
be reflected in the transcript with an appropriate note.

Q16   What will the note on the transcript referred to in A15 say?

A16    This will be decided in due course.

Q17    Will the students get to know the marks obtained in the courses they are currently registered
in?

A17    Yes, the marks up to Mid Semester Recess (and in online assessments,  if any) will be
communicated by May 31 so that the students make an informed call with respect to the
DROP option. Since online teaching/evaluations continue until June 15 as per the Senate
decision, these can also be used for final evaluation

Q18   Can a student drop a course at this stage?

A18    Yes, the COURSE DROP option is now active on Pingala. Students may apply for the COURSE
DROP on Pingala between May 27 and June 5, 2020 June 8, 2020.

Q19   What if the registered credit count for a student goes below the prescribed credit limit after
dropping a course?

A19    Lesser than the prescribed number of registered credits will not be considered as underload
for the current semester as a one-time exception.

Q20   Can a student drop a Modular Course offered in the first half of the current semester?

A20    No, the Modular Courses offered in the first half of the current semester have already been
graded and hence, cannot be dropped now.

Q21   Can a student drop a Modular Course offered in the second half of the current semester?

A21   The Departments/Instructors may choose to award A/B/C/S grades using the grading policy
adopted for the regular courses, if the participation in the online component of the course has
been full. The course may be withdrawn if the online participation has been partial. The
student can drop the course if the Department/Instructor chooses to grade the course. The
information on Modular Courses that are being withdrawn will be made available shortly. (See
also Q39)

Q22    Will the student(s) get a waiver if a Modular Course offered in the second half of the current
semester is withdrawn?

A22      If a Modular Course offered in the second half of the current semester is withdrawn, the credit
requirements of the course will be waived for the graduating students only. Please refer to
Q26 regarding maximum limits on course waiver. Non-graduating students may register for
such courses in the subsequent semesters as and when offered in consultation with the DUGC
Conveners.

Q23      If a student has dropped a course earlier, i.e., during the MID SEMESTER DROP, can he/she
request revocation of the course drop?

A23    Yes, students who may have dropped a course after the Mid Semester Examination, can
request for such course drops to be revoked through a REVOKE DROP request to be sent over
email to the course instructor along with a reason. This request can be sent between May 27
and June 5, 2020. Whether or not the REVOKE DROP request is accepted is solely the
discretion of the instructor.

Q24   Can a student be de-registered from a course by the instructor?

A24    Yes,  if the  student’s  participation  in performance  indicators such as Mid Semester
exams/quizzes/assignments before or after the suspension of the semester as well as
attendance in classes is found to be inadequate, the Instructor can de-register such a student.
All such de-registrations will be approved by SUGC/SPGC and are to be completed by June 15,
2020.

Q25      I am a BTech/BS final year student. Can I apply for conversion to the Dual Degree Programme?

A25   As a one-time measure, BTech/BS students in their final year (including extended degree
students) may apply for the Dual Degree option, if they wish to. Details regarding procedure
and eligibility criteria will be announced when the call for applications is sent. The call will be
sent in due course.

Q26      I am falling short by a few credits/courses for graduation by the end of the current semester.
Can I get a course/credit waiver so that I may graduate on time?

A26    Yes, the graduating BT/BS/DM students will be given a waiver of any 3 courses or 27 credits,
whereas the graduating students in MSc-2Year/MTech/DD/MDes/MBA/MSR programmes
will be given a waiver of any 2 courses or 18 course/thesis credits. The course/thesis credit
waiver given under this provision will reflect in the transcript with an appropriate note.

Q27    Will the waivers referred to in A26 be automatic?

A27    No, the graduating students will be asked to apply for the waiver at an appropriate time.

Q28   What is the procedure to avail the course/credit waiver for graduating students?

A28   The application procedure for availing the course/credit waiver will be announced shortly.

Q29   How will thesis credits be graded in the current semester?

A29    Only ‘S’ grades will be awarded for the thesis credits in this semester as special case and no
‘X’ grades will be awarded. If the performance of the student has not been satisfactory,
appropriate number of thesis credits will be dropped through an off-line procedure to be
made available to the thesis advisors/DPGC Convenors across departments.

Q30      I could not complete the research work and, as a result, I am unable to submit my Master’s
thesis. What options do I have?

A30   The submission of a Master’s thesis towards partial fulfilment of the requirements of the
Master’s programme has been relaxed by replacing the thesis with a Master’s Project Report
as a one-time exception. This option is available to MTech/DD/MDes/MSR students who
would have normally graduated by the end of current semester. The procedure will be
announced shortly by the Chairperson, SPGC/SUGC.

Q31      I am a Master's student in my second year. Can I apply for conversion to the PhD programme?

A31    Yes, eligible Masters students may apply for conversion to PhD programme as per the existing
guidelines for conversion (Refer to clause 4.6 of PG Manual).

Q32   Based on my cumulative performance, I am falling under the termination criterion as per
UG/PG Manual(s). Will my programme be terminated at the end of the current semester?

A32    No, as a one-time exception, there will be no terminations at the end of the current semester.

Q33      I am a UG student. Based on my academic performance, will I be placed under Probation (AP)
or Warning at the end of current semester?

A33   No student will be placed under Probation (AP) or Warning at the end of the current semester.

Q34      I am a UG student. Based on my academic performance in the 2019-20-I Semester, I was
placed on Probation (AP) or Warning for the current semester. With my performance in the
current semester, I can move out of AP/WR status. Will I be moved out of AP/WR?

A34    Yes, you will be moved to a higher academic status depending on your performance in the
current semester.

Q35      I wish to apply for Minors, Branch/Programme change. When can I apply?

A35   The call for applications for Minors, Branch/Programme change will be sent once the
resumption of next semester is announced.

Q36      I am completing the maximum residency period applicable for my Programme by the end of
the current semester. Do I need to seek academic extension in the next semester under the
prevailing rules for my programme?

A36   You do not need to seek academic extension in the next semester. The current semester
(2019-20-II) will not be counted for the purpose of determining the maximum residency
requirements of UG/PG students.

Q37      I was on academic extension and could not finish within this semester. Do I need to seek
academic extension for the next semester?

A37    No, you do not need to apply for academic extension as the current semester will not be
counted towards the duration of your programme.

Q38      If a non-graduating student with B/C/S grade taking the re-examination to improve his/her
grades, does not manage to improve the grades, can he/she opt for the earlier grades?

A38    No, grades received in the re-examination will be taken as the final grades.

Q39     Is there a deadline for the Instructors/Departments to withdraw the modular course(s)
offered in the second half of the current semester?

A39    Yes, June 15, 2020 is the deadline for withdrawing modular courses offered in the second
half of the current semester (See also Q21).

Q40.   The course policy announced in the First Course Handout has undergone changes. Will the
course policy changes be made available to students?

A40.   The course policy changes will be conveyed by the Instructor to the students, DU(P)GC and
DOAA office by June 12, 2020.

Q41   Can a student get 'A*' in the current semester?

A41    Yes, a deserving student may be awarded 'A*' if deemed appropriate by the instructor.

Q42    Will the re-examination for grade improvement be held for all the courses offered in the

A42   The re-examination for grade improvement will be conducted only for those courses that use

Q43    Will there be a SRS (Student Reaction Survey) for the courses offered in the current semester?

A43    No, there will be no SRS for the courses offered in the current semester.
