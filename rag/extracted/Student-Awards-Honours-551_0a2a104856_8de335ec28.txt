Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Thangavel Sudeep (241050086, M. Tech
/ME) has won the "Best Presentation Award" at the Ninth International Conference
on Theoretical, Applied, Computational, and Experimental Mechanics (ICTACEM-
2025), held from December 15 to 17, 2025, at the Indian Institute of Technology,
Kharagpur, India. Their paper, titled "Wake Modifications arising from a Bio-inspired
FlexibleFilament placed on NACA012 Airfoil," explores the impact of a flexible
filament, which can be thought of as a 2D analog of the covert feathers found in birds,
on induced wake modulations for a symmetric airfoil.
