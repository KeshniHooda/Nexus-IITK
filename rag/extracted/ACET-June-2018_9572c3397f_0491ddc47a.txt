Greetings from IAI!

Your continued support over the last 6 years has enabled us to reach out to a large number of
students across the country by giving them information and access to this globally recognised
niche profession of Actuaries. Our next ACET exam will be held in the month of June 2018.

Institute of Actuaries of India is a body formed under the Actuaries Act, 2006 and is the only
Institute in India authorized to formulate and carry out exams, policies and procedures leading to
qualification as an actuary.

The registration for ACET Online Examination June 2018 has already started from
20th February, 2018 and will close on 28th April, 2018.

The details are available at www.actuariesindia.org

The Examination will be conducted on 23rd June, 2018 in Twenty four (24) cities across India.

With a view to ensuring that students of your Institute and others come to know of it and take
advantage of this opportunity, we are sending some marketing material by email and request you
to have the same displayed at prominent places in your Institute.

Further, we request you to communicate this information to the students of your Institute vide an
official email/sms, the content of which may read as under:

“Registrations already started on 20th February, 2018 for ACET (Actuarial Common Entrance
Test). All details about ACET are available on our website, www.actuariesindia.org under the
ACET Tab. We encourage you to attempt this exam for a career as an Actuary.”

Please revert to acet@actuariesindia.org for any further queries.
