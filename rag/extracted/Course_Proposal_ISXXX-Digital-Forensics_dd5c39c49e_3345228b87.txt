5.  Proposing Instructor: DUGC (WSAIS)
6.  Course Description:

A) Objectives: The objective of this practical-based course is to impart hands-on training in the digital, network,

mobile forensics, VM forensics, and memory forensics, and in the proper analysis and reporting of digital evidence

using standard forensic tools and techniques.

C) Pre-requisites:

•  Working knowledge of Windows and Linux OS
•   Basic networking concepts
•   Familiarity with virtual machines
•   Introductory cyber security knowledge

D) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet: This course provides hands on training to the
students on the concepts and techniques of digital forensics and preserving evidence. The students will be able to
analyze operating systems and memory artifacts, examine the same, and prepare simple forensic reports.
