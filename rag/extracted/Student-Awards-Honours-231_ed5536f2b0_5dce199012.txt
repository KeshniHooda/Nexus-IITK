Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Apala Banerjee

Programme and Department: PhD/EE
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Jaleel Akhtar

Award/Honour: IEEE MTT-S Fellowship 2023 Award

Host Organization: San Diego, California

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Apala Banerjee has received the IEEE
Microwave Theory and Technology  Society (MTT-S) Graduate Fellowship Award
bestowed for excellence in research and academics for the year 2023.
This award is given every year by the IEEE MTT Society to PhD students selected from
all over the world pursuing the field of RF and Microwave Engineering, and she is the
only student from India this year to receive this award. The award consists of a
certificate of recognition, fellowship, and travel support to attend the annual IEEE MTT-
S Microwave Symposium to receive the award. The award will be presented to Apala on
June 15, 2023, at the IEEE IMS 2023, San Diego, California.

Date: January 29, 2023
