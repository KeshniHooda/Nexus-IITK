6. Course Description:
A) Objectives:
The objective of this course is to provide students with knowledge of how the human mind
and brain represent and process language. It also aims to address the following questions:
a) How do we acquire knowledge?
b) How is language produced and understood?
c) Which areas of the brain play a vital role in the production and perception of speech?
d) What happens to language when the brain is damaged?

C) Pre-requisites: NONE
D) Short summary for including in the Courses of Study Booklet:
This course aims  to provide the students with an understanding of how language  is
represented and processed in the human mind and brain. It focuses on language as a system
controlled by the brain that is different from but closely linked to the human mind. It covers
topics such as the relationship between language, mind, and brain, the biological basis of
language, language production, comprehension, language acquisition, etc. It also covers brain
pathology and aphasiology.
