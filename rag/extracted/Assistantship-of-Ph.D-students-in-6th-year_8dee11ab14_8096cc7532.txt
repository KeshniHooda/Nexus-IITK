Office Order

Sub: Revision of Financial Assistantship for the Ph.D. students in the 6111 year.

The  revised  rates of Financial Assistantship  to PhD students  in  their sixth year into the Ph.D.
programme shall be as follows:

(i)     Rs. 10,000/- per month for the first six months (August-January or January - June) subject to
satisfactory recommendations of Assistantship extension committee.
(ii)     Rs.8,000/- per month for the next six months (February   July or .July - December) subject to
satisfactory recommendations of Assistantship extension committee.
(iii)    Revised rates will be applicable retrospectively from August 01,2016.
(iv)   The tuition fee paid by the students in their 6 111 year will be the same as that paid by students
drawing full Institute Assistantship.
(v)     If the student submits the  thesis during the period of extension. the assistantship will be
provided only till the month of submission.
(vi)   A student is expected to devote up to eight hours per week towards job(s) assigned to him/her
by the Head of the Department.

This onicc order is being issued based on the approval of the Director.
