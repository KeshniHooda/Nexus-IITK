Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Darshan Prakash Borthakur, Mr. Ashok Narayan Tripathi, Mr.
Nimmala Pavan Kalyan

Programme and Department: PhD/ EE and MTech/EE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof Alok R Verma

Award/Honour: Best Student Paper Award

Host Organization: North Carolina, USA

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr. Darshan Prakash Borthakur (Ph.D./EE),
Mr. Ashok Narayan Tripathi (Ph.D./EE) and Mr. Nimmala Pavan Kalyan (MTech./EE)
have received the best student paper award in the 2022 Joint Conference on
Electrostatics held June 12-15, 2022 in Charlotte, North Carolina, USA. Their papers
have been selected for consideration of the EPC and invited for publication in the IEEE
IAS Transactions on Industry Applications.

The conference was jointly organised by the Electrostatic Society of America (ESA), the
Institute of Electrostatics Japan (IEJ), la Société Française d'Electrostatique (SFE), the
Electrostatic Processes Committee (EPC) of the IAS/IEEE and the Electrostatic
Committee of the Chinese Physical Society (EC-CPS).
