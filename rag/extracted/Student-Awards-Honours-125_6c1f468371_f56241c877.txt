Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Mendu Rama Krishna and Mr. Chirag Jain

Programme and Department: PhD/AE, MSR/AE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Abhishek and Prof. Mangal Kothari

Award/Honour: First Responder UAS Endurance Challenge Stage 4 Winner Notification

Host Organization: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), USA

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr. Mendu Rama Krishna and Mr. Chirag Jain
have designed a small helicopter Unmanned Aerial System from VTOL and Helicopter
Laboratory, guided by Dr. Mangal Kothari and Dr Abhishek. They have won three Best-
in-Class categories prize in stage 4 of 2021 First Responder Endurance Challenge
organized by National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), USA. The National
Institute of Standards and Technology is a physical sciences laboratory and a non-
regulatory agency of the United States Department of Commerce. Its mission is to
promote innovation and industrial competitiveness. Earlier, they had won first prize in
stage 3 as well.

Date: June 25, 2021
