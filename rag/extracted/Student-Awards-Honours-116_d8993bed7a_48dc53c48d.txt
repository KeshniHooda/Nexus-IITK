Dear Applicants,

We are very pleased to advise you that your application for a Mitacs Globalink Research Award – SICI has been
approved. Please refer to Appendix B for the specific funding details and terms and conditions of this award.

Mitacs Globalink builds a living bridge between Canada and international partners by establishing and reinforcing
global links through student mobility. Mitacs has partnered with SICI to support the two-way mobility of students
between Canadian universities and SICI. The Mitacs Globalink Research Award – SICI is an exciting initiative that
provides the opportunity for faculty members and students at SICI Research Centres to build an international
research network and undertake research abroad at Canadian universities.

All awards are subject to ongoing program eligibility and continuation of funding by our government partners.
This award is offered in partnership with SICI. Mitacs gratefully acknowledges the financial support of the
Government of Canada for their ongoing support to the Globalink program.

Please feel free to contact Grant Management Specialist Nicole Smith at nsmith@mitacs.ca if you require further
information.

Thank you for your proposal and welcome to Mitacs Globalink!
