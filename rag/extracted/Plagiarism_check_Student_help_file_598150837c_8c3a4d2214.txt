Introduction

Plagiarism is a serious academic concern, and it is essential for students to
follow the correct procedures to ensure their work meets institutional
integrity standards. This document provides detailed guidelines on how to
apply for a plagiarism check and the resubmission process when required. By
following these steps, students can avoid delays and ensure a smooth thesis
submission.

Guidelines for PPD Form

•  You may also upload the similarity report (if required). The upload is non-mandatory in nature.
•   If you uploaded an incorrect file, click on the Remove button to delete it.

•  Verify that your Thesis Supervisor/Co-supervisor's name is correctly updated. If incorrect, contact your
Department Office for corrections.
•  Double-check all documents and details before clicking the "Submit" button, as no modifications can be made
after submission.
• Once submitted, the Supervisor and Plagiarism Prevention Desk (PPD) will review it.

10•  For any issues related to the Plagiarism Check Process, contact the PPD Cell at: ppd_doaa@iitk.ac.in.

Declaration Submission

11

Processing Form

12

[PAGE 13]

13

[PAGE 14]
Step 3: Select the declaration check-box (you may also add remarks, if any)
and then click on the “Submit” button. Once clicked, it will be directed to your
supervisor or co-supervisor.

14

[PAGE 15]
At this stage of process

• Declaration Submission Only: In this stage, you are allowed to submit only
the declaration. No other actions are permitted.
• No Document Changes: Document cannot be modified, replaced, or
removed during this process.
• No Form Edits: The form becomes non-editable at this stage. You will not
be able to make any corrections or updates to the details already entered.

Guidelines for resubmission

• Resubmission is allowed only if the oral board suggests modifications to
your thesis.

• The “Update Thesis” button will only be active after if your application
status is “Approved” by DOAA or ADAA.

Conclusion

Following the correct plagiarism check and resubmission procedures
ensures that students meet the required academic integrity standards. By
adhering to these guidelines, students can avoid unnecessary complications
and complete their thesis submission smoothly. For any further queries,
students are encouraged to reach out to the PPD Cell for assistance.
