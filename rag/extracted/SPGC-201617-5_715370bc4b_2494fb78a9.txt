(v)    The SPGC considered the request of Ms. Amrita Goswami (16102261) Ph.D. student, Dept. of
Chemical Engg., for continuation of her academic programme. Her Ph.D. admission was cancelled
due to non-fulfillment of eligibility criteria as per under Section 2.3 of the PG manual. The SPGC
discussed at length the appeal of Ms. Goswami as well as the DPGC recommendation on her appeal,
and noted the following:

1.    The decision of the Senate cancelling Ms Goswami's provisional admission cannot be revoked
as the candidate does not fulfill the eligibility criteria for Ph.D. admission. In this regard, the
document submitted along with the appeal (Annexure 4 of Ms. Goswami’s appeal) cannot be
considered because, as per the AKTU norms available on the University website, and indeed,
rightly observed by the DPGC in  its recommendation, the procedure for conversion from
percentages to CGPA invoked in this document comes into force with effect from 2016-17
whereas Ms Goswami graduated from HBTI in 2015-16.

2.    The candidate cannot be considered for admission in MS (Research) retrospectively as requested
by her and as recommended by the DPGC, since she had not been selected for admission in the
MS (Research) programme in 2016-17-I as per the records available with the SPGC office.

In view of the above, Ms. Goswami’s appeal cannot be considered favorably. The SPGC
recommends that the candidate may be considered for fresh admission in MS (Research)
through open advertisement.
