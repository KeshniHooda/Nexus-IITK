Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Deepak M. Khushalani

Programme and Department: PhD/BSBE
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Nitin Mohan

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award

Host Organization: IIT Madras

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Deepak M. Khushalani has won the Best
Poster Presentation Award at the Annual PMRF (Prime Minister's Research Fellowship)
Symposium organized at IIT Madras on 17th & 18th February 2023.
The annual symposium selected 51 among 2600 fellows from across the country to
present their work in front of the Union Minister of Education. Deepak was among the
selected 51 and won the Best Poster Award in the  Biological Sciences & Biomedical
Engineering discipline. He has received the award for his work "Regulation of cellular
transport probed by super resolution microscopy".

Date: February 22, 2023
