PRE-REGISTRATION SCHEDULE FOR 2025-26-I SEMESTER

Guidelines:
(i)  Please note that the pre-registration for the upcoming semester is mandatory. In case a student
does not pre-register for some reason, the responsibility will entirely be that of the student’s.

(ii)  Please note the fine for non-completion of pre-registration is Rs. 2500/-.

(iii) For pre-registration, please make sure you have entered your APAAR/ABC ID in your student
profile on Pingala. Registration process/steps for ABC/APAAR ID creation is given in the
attached manual: https://www.abc.gov.in/assets/resources/Academic-Bank-of-Credits_User_Manual_V3.pdf

(Steps to enter APAAR ID on Pingala: https://iitk.ac.in/doaa/data/APAR-ID-Help-File.pdf)

(iv) The DoAA Office is in the process of uploading student credits for the Academic Year 2024-25.

(v) Please check your credits for the Academic Year 2024-25 by 21st May 2025 on ABC portal.
In case of any discrepancy, please inform oars_office@iitk.ac.in by 31st May 2025. After
31st May 2025, no updation/correction in credits for the Academic Year 2024-25 is possible
on ABC portal.

Points to remember:

1. Students are strongly advised to complete their pre-registration.
2. After filling all accepted courses in the online registration form, students must submit the same
for DUGC/DPGC approval as per the given timelines.
3. If the number of credits in the submitted form is less than the minimum load allowed, then the
form may get rejected. This does not apply to graduating and extended degree students.
4. It is the sole responsibility of the student to ensure that no two courses with clashing time
slots are  filled  in the submitted pre-registration form. Failure to do so may lead to
cancellation of the registration in all clashing courses.
5. Students on semester leave due to any reason(s) must participate in pre-registration for
the upcoming semester if they wish to register for courses in the upcoming semester.
Not submitting the pre-registration form would result in the fine being imposed.
6. Students whose pre-registration form is rejected will have to do online academic registration on
the due date as detailed in the Academic Calendar for 2025-26-I Semester.
7. Please note the fine for non-completion of pre-registration is Rs. 2500/-.

Helpdesk / Assistance / Alerts

a)   If you face any technical issues while accessing the pre-registration Module on Pingala, please
write to pingala@iitk.ac.in.

b)   If you have issues related to academics regarding the pre-registration, please write to
reg_help@iitk.ac.in.

c)  To keep track of your pre-registration activities on Pingala, you will receive an auto-generated
email for every transaction. If you receive an email for a transaction not made by you on Pingala,
you should immediately report to the DoAA office by dropping an e-mail to courses@iitk.ac.in.

d)  Kindly do not share your Pingala User ID and password with anyone.
