Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Ankita Kumari

Programme and Department: PhD/EE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. M. J. Akhtar

Award/Honour: Best Paper Award

Host Organization: Ahmedabad

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Ankita Kumari has received the best
paper award (3rd position) in the academia category at the IEEE Microwave Antennas
and Propagation Conference, held during December 10-14, 2023, in Ahmedabad,
India. This award is received for her paper titled "Adulteration Detection of Glycol in
Medical Grade Solvents Using the CSRR Loaded Stepped Coplanar Waveguide", co-
authored with Prakrati Azad, Abhishek Kumar and M. J. Akhtar, presented during
MAPCON 2023 in Ahmedabad, India.

Date: Dec 27, 2023
