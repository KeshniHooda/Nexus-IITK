Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Swati Mahato

Programme and Department: PhD/MSE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. N.P. Gurao and Prof. K. Biswas

Award/Honour: Poster Presentation Award

Host Organization: IIT Jodhpur

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Swati Mahato has received the second
prize in poster presentation at an international conference. "Industrial Insights and
Academia Perspectives on High Entropy Alloys (IIAP-HEA 2024) organized jointly by
IIT Jodhpur, IIT Bhubaneswar and IIT BHU Varanasi" from 5-7, April 2024 at IIT
Jodhpur. The title of her work is "Elucidating the effect of temperature and strain on
the inverse grain size dependence of strain rate sensitivity in (FeCrNi)99Si1 medium
entropy alloy".
