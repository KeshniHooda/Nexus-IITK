Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Shantanu Sen

Programme and Department: PhD/CHM

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Sandeep Verma

Award/Honour: Gandhian Young Technological Innovation (GYTI) Award - 2023

Host Organization:

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Shantanu Sen has been selected as one
of the 11 nationwide recipients of 'Gandhian Young Technological Innovation (GYTI)
Award' for the year 2023. Shantanu has received this outstanding recognition for a
patented technology from IIT Kanpur concerning detecting spurious aggregated
insulin hormone in commercial injectable formulations, which also forms a part of his
PhD thesis. The title of Shantanu's award-winning research innovation is "See your
insulin: Proposing a self-investigated insulin quality monitoring method".
