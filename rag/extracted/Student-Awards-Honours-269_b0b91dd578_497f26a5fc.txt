Student’s Name: Ms. Sephora Jose

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Sephora Jose has been awarded the
Bluestone Rising Scholar Award for 2023 by the editorial board of Caste: A Global
Journal on Social Exclusion which is affiliated with Brandeis University. The award is
based on her paper titled "When Fists Write (of) the Past: Conceptualizing Dalit
Historiography through the Cultural Productions of Dravida Varga Aikya Munnani".
