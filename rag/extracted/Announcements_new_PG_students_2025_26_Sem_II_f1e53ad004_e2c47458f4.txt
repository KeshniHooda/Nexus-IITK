Announcement for New Post-Graduate
(PG) Students 2025 – 2026 Sem II, IIT
Kanpur

• Please visit Centre for Mental Health and Wellbeing (CMHW),
IIT Kanpur webpage  (https://www.iitk.ac.in/counsel/)  for
regular information related to the PG Orientation Program
2025 – 2026 Sem II.
• New PG students are required to report to the institute on
December 30, 2025 (Tuesday) between 9:00 am – 6:00 pm.
• Accommodation in the Hall of Residences will be allocated to
students on the reporting day,  i.e. December 30, 2025,
immediately after reporting.
• The orientation program, registrations, knowing the campus,
department, academic and administrative affairs for the new
PG batch are scheduled between December 30, 2025 –
January 3, 2026. Attendance in all such events is mandatory.
Students will be timely intimated regarding the same.
• Classes for the new PG batch will commence on January 5,
2026.
• Parents  or guardians  are welcome  to accompany the
students; however, please be advised that IIT Kanpur does
not provide or arrange accommodation for the parents or
guardians.
• Please contact relevant departments for further queries
related to admission. The department contacts can be seen
at https://www.iitk.ac.in/doaa/pgadmission/contact.php.

Please keep visiting this page for regular updates.
