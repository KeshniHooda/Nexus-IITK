Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Nimisha Agarwal has been awarded the
Best Paper Award at the 2nd ACM Global Computing Education Conference (CompEd)
2023 held during December 7-9, 2023. She also received ACM India scholarship
offered at CompEd 2023. The title of her paper is “A Bug's New Life: Creating Refute
Questions from Filtered CS1 Student Code Snapshots” which is coauthored with Viraj
Kumar (IISc), Arun Raman (BITS Pilani, Goa), and Amey Karkare (IIT Kanpur).
