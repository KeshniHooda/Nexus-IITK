Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Sushanta Barman has won a poster award

2020), held as e-conference, from 26th October to 31st October, 2020. The title of his
poster was "Observation of Nonlinear Demagnification in Plasma-based Ion beam
Optics". The authors were Sushanta Barman, Sanjeev Kumar Maurya, and Sudeep
Bhattacharjee.
