6.  Course Description: This course focuses on the implementation of Monte Carlo algorithms to solve a variety of

problems in finance and insurance. It begins with a brief introduction to no-arbitrage pricing and the dynamics of

asset prices. The course also covers statistical inference for asset price models using real-world data. Students will

study the pricing of standard and exotic financial derivatives across asset classes, along with model calibration and

sensitivity analysis. Additionally, the course explores various loss-reserving techniques in life and non-life

insurance.
