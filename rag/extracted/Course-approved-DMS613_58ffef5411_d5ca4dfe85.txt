6.  Course Description: This introductory course on Mathematical finance focuses on.the principle of pricing
contingent claims under absence of arbitrage. The course will build a working knowledge of topics in Stochastic
calculus with respect to Brownian motion and convex analysis, and use them to rigorously build the theory of no-
arbitrage pricing. Special emphasis will be on the characterization of no-arbitrage, in terms of its existence and
relation to pricing. The course will apply the theory to hedging and pricing financial derivatives. The course will
also discuss topics relevant in contemporary research and practice, including pricing-hedging duality and pricing in
incomplete markets.
