8. Course Description:
A) Objectives: The course aims to provide adequate exposure to the foundational concepts

of financial risk in an economy. It also highlights the role of financial markets in

financial stability and recent developments in policy thinking.

C) Pre-requisites: Familiarity with macroeconomics (Instructor’s consent)

9. Summary: The critical role of financial institutions in driving economic growth and

development is well documented in the literature. However, rapid financial development

can also introduce significant challenges and risks to the system. Therefore, understanding

various aspects of financial risk, policy applications, and recent developments is essential.

This course explores the following topics: nature of  risk, financial institutions and

regulations, banking balance sheet risk, market risk measurement, financial stability,

financial instability, and recent developments in policy thinking.
