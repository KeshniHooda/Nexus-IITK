NOTIFICATION

December 30, 2020

Subject: Meeting of the Re-appeals Committee

The Re-appeals Committee will meet on Tuesday, January 5, 2021 to consider
the re-appeals submitted by the students whose academic programmes were
terminated at the end of 2019-20-II or earlier. The meeting will be held over
Zoom, an online meeting platform.

At this meeting, the students who have submitted their re-appeals will get an
opportunity to present themselves to the Committee and share their academic
plan with the Committee.

All the students who have submitted their re-appeals are advised to provide
their contact details in the online Google form available at the link given below.
They will be sent the link for the online meeting platform over email, as well as
the time slot during which they are to make themselves available for the
meeting.

The last date to fill in the online form is January 3, 2021.

Link to the Google form:
