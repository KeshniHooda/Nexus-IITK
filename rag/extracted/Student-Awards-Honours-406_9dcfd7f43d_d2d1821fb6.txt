Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr Shantanu Sen has secured 2nd position
at the prestigious Falling Walls Lab Event India, hosted by the Falling Walls Foundation
Germany & country organizers, IISc Bangalore. Falling Walls is a unique global hub
connecting science, business, and society. Their philosophy revolves around shaping
the future of humanity through impact-oriented ideas and discoveries, driven by a
shared dedication to creating breakthroughs across borders and disciplines. Falling
Walls is a research and innovation pitching competition where the participants are
given just 3 minutes to present their innovative solutions to some of the most pressing
global challenges. In his pitch, "Breaking the Wall of Insulin Dosage Wastage",
Shantanu presented our patented technology on thermostable insulin and long-lived
protein therapeutics invented at IIT Kanpur.
