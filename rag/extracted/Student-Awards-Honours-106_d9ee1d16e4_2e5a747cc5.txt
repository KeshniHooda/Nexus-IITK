Student’s Name: Mr. Aman Mahajan

Programme and Department: PhD/BSBE

Award/Honour: Bajpai-Saha Student Award (First place)

Host Organization: Society for Biomaterials and Artificial Organs India (SBAOI)
Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Aman Mahajan has won the “Bajpai-Saha
Student Award (First place)” for the best student paper presentation in the recently
concluded 30th Annual Conference of the Society for Biomaterials and Artificial Organs
India (SBAOI) titled International Conference on Biomedical Materials Innovation
(ICBMI) 2020 held as e-conference, from 6th December to 9th December 2020. The
Title of his research work was “Self-Contracting and Stiffening Hydrogel promotes
Cartilage Regeneration”.
