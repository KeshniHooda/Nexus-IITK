Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Baisakhi Bandyopadhyay has been
awarded the 2nd Position in Student Category Papers Award for the paper titled
'Reduction of RCS of a Series Fed Linear Array Antenna Using Artificial Magnetic
Conductors'  at the  IEEE Microwaves, Antennas, and Propagation Conference
(MAPCON-2023), held during December 11–14, 2023, in Ahmedabad.
The title of the paper is "Reduction of RCS of a Series Fed Linear Array Antenna Using
Artificial Magnetic Conductors" authored by Baisakhi Bandyopadhyay, Rahul Kumar
Jaiswal, Ranjit Kumar Dutta, and Kumar Vaibhav Srivastava.
