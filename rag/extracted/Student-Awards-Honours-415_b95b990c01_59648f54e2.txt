Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Sunil Kumar Maurya has received the
GRID-INDIA Power System (GIPSA) 2024 award. The GRID-INDIA Power System
Awards (GIPSA) stands as a Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) endeavour aimed at
acknowledging outstanding research achievements in the Power System domain. It
specifically targets recent doctoral and master’s research accomplishments from
technical institutions offering postgraduate and PhD programs in power systems and
related fields.
