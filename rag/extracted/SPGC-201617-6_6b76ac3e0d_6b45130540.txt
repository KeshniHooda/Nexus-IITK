Item 24: To discuss the formats of dropping of P.G. courses and P.G. course registration in S/X mode as
proposed by the Convener, DPGC, Dept. of Civil Engineering.
The SPGC deliberated on the proposed format for course-drop and decided to take the feedback from the
departments on the new format.

The proposed format for PG course registration in S/X mode was also discussed. One of the members
pointed out that the format was already available with the departments. It was decided that the matter
would be looked into, and, if required, taken up for consideration in the next meeting of the SPGC.

In the course of the discussion, the issue of imposing restrictions on the number of courses that a student
can drop in the entire duration of his/her programme was raised. It was decided that a sub-committee of
the SPGC will look into the various aspects of the issue and come up with a proposal. The sub-committee
comprises Prof. S K Mehta, Dept. of CSE, Prof. B Dasgupta, Dept. of ME, and Prof. Nitin Kaistha, Dept.
of ChE.
