Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Ms. Sadhana Naskar

Programme and Department: PhD/HSS/FA

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Ritwij Bhowmik

Award/Honour: Certificate of Merit Award for Excellence in Art for Ms. Sadhana
Naskar.
Host Organization: Ministry of Information & Culture, Government of West Bengal
Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Ms. Sadhana Naskar, won a prestigious
"Certificate of Merit" award in the recent state-level art exhibition, Charulaka Utsav
organized by the Ministry of Information & Culture, Government of West Bengal in
2019.
Two of Ms. Naskar's (student of HSS) recent paintings were selected for the final round
(professional category) display of their annual exhibition at the Academy of Fine Arts
Gallery, Kolkata.
The prize distribution ceremony was held on 5th December at Rabindra Sadan
Auditorium in Kolkata. The certificates and the awards were handed to the recipients by
the renowned artist and Chairman of Rajya Charulaka Parishat, Mr. Jogen Chowdhury in
the presence of the cabinet ministers of the West Bengal government.

Date: January 10, 2020
