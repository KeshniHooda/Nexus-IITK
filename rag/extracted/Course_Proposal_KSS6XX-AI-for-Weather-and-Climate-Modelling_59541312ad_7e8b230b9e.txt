6. Course Description:

A) Objectives:

This course introduces students to artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and scientific machine
learning techniques for weather and climate modelling and prediction. Students will gain foundational knowledge
of atmospheric processes, climate datasets, Earth system modelling and numerical weather prediction. The course
covers classical machine learning methods, deep learning architectures, graph-based learning, neural operators,
and emerging foundation models for weather forecasting. Students will learn to analyze observational, reanalysis,
and climate datasets, develop data-driven forecasting models, evaluate modern AI-based forecasting systems and
gain hands-on experience with state-of-the-art tools for weather forecasting, and Earth-system modeling.

C) Recommended pre-requisites: Basic Linear Algebra, Probability and Statistics, and programming
experience (preferably in Python), A fundamental course on Machine Learning.

D) Short summary for inclusion in the Courses of Study /Booklet

Introduction to weather and climate prediction; atmospheric observations, reanalysis and climate datasets;
fundamentals of numerical weather prediction; machine learning methods including regression, random forests,
and XGBoost; deep learning techniques including CNNs, LSTMs, and Transformers; scientific machine learning
approaches such as Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), and Neural
Operators; modern weather foundation models including GraphCast, GenCast, FourCastNet, Pangu-Weather,
Aurora, and ClimaX; downscaling, data assimilation, and ensemble forecasting; applications of AI to weather and
climate prediction.
