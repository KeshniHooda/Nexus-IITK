Course Objective

This is an advanced course on the regularity theory of elliptic partial diﬀerential equations. The
course develops the principal analytical techniques used to obtain qualitative and quantitative
regularity estimates for weak and viscosity solutions.

The course begins with a review of Sobolev spaces, weak derivatives, weak solutions, and funda-
mental inequalities, followed by the study of H2-regularity for elliptic equations. The classical
Schauder theory is then introduced, providing regularity estimates in H¨older spaces.

We next develop the De Giorgi–Nash–Moser theory for uniformly elliptic equations in diver-
gence form. The course emphasizes Caccioppoli inequalities, De Giorgi and Moser iteration,
measure-to-point estimates, oscillation decay, H¨older continuity, and Harnack inequalities. The
connection with Hilbert’s 19th problem is also discussed.

The ﬁnal part of the course is devoted to the Krylov–Safonov theory for uniformly elliptic
in non-divergence form. Topics include viscosity solutions, the Aleksandrov–Bakelman–Pucci
estimate, the Krylov–Safonov measure estimate, H¨older regularity, and Harnack inequalities. If
time permits, we will also discuss analogous results for parabolic PDEs.
