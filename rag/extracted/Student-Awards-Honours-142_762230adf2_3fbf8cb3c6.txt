Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Dr. Bappa Maji completed a large mural at
Fort William, Kolkata, to celebrate the 50 years of the decisive victory in the 1971
Liberation War of Bangladesh.

This Mural was commissioned by the Ministry of Defense and the Eastern Command of
the Indian Army. The enormous artwork consists of 4 large panels (each 7 feet in
height and 10 feet in width). It is inspired by the various historical moments from the
war. Bappa single-handedly completed this impressive artwork earlier this month,
which is now permanently installed at Fort William. The Mural was formally unveiled
on the 16th of December 2021 to celebrate the 50 years of ‘Vijay Divas.’

Date: December 21, 2021
