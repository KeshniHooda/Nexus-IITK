Rename existing Annexure VI as VI (a) and add a new annexure VI (b)

Integrated PhD program: Academic roadmap and other details:

1. Academic Roadmap

-    In the first year of the program the students will follow the course template provided in Annexure
Vll(b).

-   Students migrate to the PhD part of the programme provided they have cleared all the courses
of the first year as per the template with CPI of at least 6.5. After successful migration, the
student  will be governed by the rules meant for the PhD students joined  directly  after
B.Tech./B.S. (4 years)/M .Sc. degree.

-   Students failing to migrate to the PhD program within the first year are allowed to credit 600
and above level courses in the second year. Any such courses taken during the second year
will be counted towards their total credit requirement.

-   Failing to migrate to the PhD program within 2 years of admission may result in the termination
of the student's programme.

2. Summer Term

Students must register following clause 4.4. of PG manual. However, until the students migrate to the PhD
part of the program, they are not allowed to register for thesis credits in summer.

Dear Prof. Chakraborty,

As you are aware  of, the Department of Statistics and Data Science (DSDS) has recently been
established at IIT Kanpur. The academic programmes currently under DSDS are:

BS (Statistics and Data Science)
MS {Statistics and Data Science)
MSc (2-Year) Statistics
PhD (students supervised by the DSDS faculty members)
As part of the academic reorganization, the M.Sc. (2-year) Statistics and Ph.D. in Statistics
programmes are transferred from the erstwhile Department of Mathematics and Statistics to the
Department of Statistics and Data Science.

In this regard, the DPGC of the department seek the approval of SPGC for the folfowing items:

1. Revision of the existing M.Sc. (2-year) Statistics Programme Template

To align the programme template with the new departmental nomenclature, all courses currently
having the prefix MTH is proposed to be renamed as SDS. The numerical course codes (XXX) as
well as the course contents of these courses will remain unchanged. We are also requesting the
creation of the M.Sc project (elective) course codes SDS598 and SOS599.  Kindly note that the
existing MTH598 and MTH599 shall continue to be in operation by the Department of Mathematics.
There  is no change  in the credit structure, or semester-wise credit requirements  in the
proposed template of the programme. The proposed template for Y26 and later batches, is given in
Annexure 1.  For your kind reference, the current Senate approved template of the M.Sc. (2-year)
program is given in Annexure 2.                              •

2. List of M.Sc. (2-year) Statistics DC and Project courses with new numbering

List of  all the DC and M.Sc. Project courses of the M.Sc. (2-year) Statistics program, with their
existing MTH numbering (and credit) and the corresponding proposed SOS numbering (and credit),
are given in Annexure 3.

PhD in Statistics
The minimum credit requirement for the PhD Statistics program with M.Sc. and B.Tech/B.S.
are 54 credits and 72 credits respectively, where the following three courses are compulsory,
adding up to 27 credits (credits are mentioned inside the parenthesis):

1. SOS 652: Advanced Calculus (09)
2. SOS 754: Probability Theory (09)
3. SOS 755: Statistical Inference (09)
4. SOS 888M: Introduction to Profession and Communication Skills (SIX)

The minimum research credit requirement for the programme is 72, and students are required to
register for 36 research credits per semester after finishing the course-work.

Note: Students admitted to the PhD program on templates before Y26 (<= Y25) will require
taking courses with MTH course numbering. Accordingly, all the courses will now run with their
current SOS numberings and their old MTH numberings, until all the backlog requirements are
met. The list of courses with both the numberings and their credits is available in the following
table.
