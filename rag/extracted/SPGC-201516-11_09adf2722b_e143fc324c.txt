Item 11: To discuss the feedback received from various departments for award of M.Tech./MS degree to
those students who take direct admission into a PhD program after B.Tech./BS degree on the
recommendations of Prof. Manindra Agarwal’s committee formed by Senate. Item was deferred in
last SPGC meeting.

The SPGC considered the feedback received from the departments on Prof. Manindra Agarwal committee
on award of the M.Tech. degree along with PhD in Engineering disciplines. The SPGC was broadly in
agreement with the EPC recommendations. It was noted that the departments be asked to specify the
requirements (thesis work or additional course work) for the M.Tech. degree to be awarded to PhD
students of their respective departments. The departments may decide whether they would like to give the
option to their students for degree by thesis or by course work or both options will be available to the
student.

Item 12: To discuss the proposal for rescheduling of make-up exams as proposed by DOAA.

The SPGC after discussion and considering the feedback received from various departments, SPGC does
not recommend the proposal.

Item 13: To discuss the proposal for duration of summer term from 8 to 7 weeks.

The SPGC after discussion and considering the feedback received from various departments, SPGC was
not in favour of reducing the duration of summer term from 8 to 7 weeks. However, it was suggested that
the number of lectures per week may be reduced to 5.
