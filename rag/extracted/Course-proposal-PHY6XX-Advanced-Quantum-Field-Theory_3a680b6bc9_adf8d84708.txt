10. Objective of the course: Quantum Field Theory (QFT) is one of the fundamental tools of modern physics with
broad ranging applications in all of theoretical physics including condensed matter physics, particle physics, and
other areas of high energy physics.

This is designed to be a second course on the subject which will follow PHY685 – Introduction to Quantum Field
Theory. This course would introduce the student to the methods of path integral quantization and will focus on
the understanding of non-Abelian gauge theories which generalize electrodynamics and is mathematical
framework for understanding the strong and weak nuclear forces.

A major part of the course would deal with renormalization. Answers to various processes in QFT often turn out
to be infinite. Regularization and renormalization are used to extract physical answers out of these infinites. This
course will lay out these formal procedures and show how to renormalize various QFTs.

11. Course outline:

•   Introduction [2 lectures]

•   Quantization of constrained systems [4 lectures]
o   Classification of constraints
o  Dirac method of quantization

[PAGE 2]
•   Classical aspects of gauge theories [4 lectures]

•   Functional methods [8 lectures]
o  Path integrals for quantum mechanics
o  Path integral quantization of scalar fields, fermions and electrodynamics

•   Quantization of Yang-Mills theories [8 lectures]
o  Path integrals, Fadeev-Poppov ghosts.
o  BRST quantization.

•   Renormalization [10 lectures]
o  Systematics of renormalization
o  Renormalization group

•  Advanced topics [3 lectures]
o  Anomalies
o  A glimpse of Conformal Field Theory

12. Prerequisites: Quantum field theory (PHY681)

13. Short summary for including in the courses of Study Booklet: Quantum Field Theory (QFT) is one of the
fundamental tools of modern physics with broad ranging applications in all of theoretical physics
including condensed matter physics, particle physics, and other areas of high energy physics. This
course would carry forward the ideas and methods of QFT introduced in PHY685 into advanced topics.
The topics covered would be quantization of constrained systems, functional methods, path integral
quantization for fields of various spin, classical and quantum aspects of Yang-Mills theories, Systematics
of renormalization and renormalization group.
