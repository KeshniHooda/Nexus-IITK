Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Ayesha Nanda has received a poster prize
in the recently concluded 7th Annual Conference of the Division of Plasma Physics of
the Association of Asia Pacific Physics Society (AAPPS-DPP 2023), held from November
12-17, 2023 at Port Messe Nagoya, Japan. The poster's title is "Energy exchange in a
compact dipole plasma: thermodynamical investigations through measurements and
modeling" which is co-authored with Prof. Sudeep Bhattacharjee.
