6. Course Description: PG course

A) Objectives: The porous subsurface is crucial for various energy and environmental applications,
including groundwater resources and carbon sequestration. This proposed course aims to introduce
postgraduate and senior undergraduate students to the fundamental concepts of flow processes
involved in these applications. We will specifically focus on five key areas: carbon sequestration,
subsurface hydrogen  storage, geothermal energy  recovery, groundwater contamination, and
subsurface nuclear waste storage. In this course, students will learn how to use the concepts of porous
media flows for predicting flow behaviour in these contexts.
