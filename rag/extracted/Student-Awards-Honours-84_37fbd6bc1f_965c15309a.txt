Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Aditya Raj and Mr. Malik Faisal Nissar

Programme and Department: BT-MT/CE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. Chinmoy Kolay

Award/Honour: 1st Prize in INSDAG Civil AWARD 2018-2019

Host Organization: Institute For Steel Development & Growth (INSDAG)
Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Mr. Aditya Raj (5th-year BT-MT) and Mr.
Malik Faisal Nissar (4th-year BT-MT) from the Department of Civil Engineering have been
awarded the First Prize in the National Student Award Competition for Civil/Structural
Engineering Students for Best Innovative Structural Steel Design held during 14-15
February 2020 at the Institute for Steel Development and Growth (INSDAG), Kolkata.
The award includes a cash prize of Rs. 45,000 and a certificate.

Date: February 20, 2020
