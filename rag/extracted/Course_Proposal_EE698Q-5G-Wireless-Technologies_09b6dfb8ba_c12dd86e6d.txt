6. Course Description:

Objectives: The course will present an in-depth analysis of several key 5G wireless
technologies such as Massive MIMO, mmWave MIMO, Filter Bank Multi-Carrier
(FBMC), Non-Orthogonal Multiple Access (NOMA), Full-Duplex (FD) etc. It is also
intended to cover other advanced wireless technologies such as Cooperative
Communication, Cognitive Radio, Multi-User MIMO and more. Finally, students working
in groups of two are expected to prepare a term paper that will focus on an in-depth
study and analysis of any cutting edge 5G wireless technology of their choice.
