Brief Description of the Award/Honour Ms. Abhirami Ajith (21100263 Ph.D / HSS) has

tuition for any approved RBS course over the next two years (valid until December
2027). It will support her advanced training, particularly the course "Digital Codicology
and Book History" at the University of Pennsylvania, which aligns closely with her
doctoral research.
