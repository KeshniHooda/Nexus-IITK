Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name: Mr. Anandhakrishnan S.

Programme and Department: MTech/MSE
Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. N. P. Gurao and Prof. Rajdip Mukherjee

Award/Honour: Best Poster Award

Host Organization: BARC, Mumbai

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Anandhakrishnan S. has received the Best
Poster Award Second Prize at the International Workshop on High Entropy Materials
2023- IWHEM 23 ) held during April 23-25, 2023 at BARC, Mumbai, India.
He has received the award for his poster  titled “Crystal  plasticity simulation of
deformation behaviour of equiatomic FeCoNi medium entropy alloy in compression at
quasistatic and dynamic strain rate regime” coauthored with Anandhakrishnan S., Swati
Mahato, Shirish Chandrakar, P. Venkitanarayanan, Krishanu Biswas and N. P. Gurao.

Date: May 08, 2023
