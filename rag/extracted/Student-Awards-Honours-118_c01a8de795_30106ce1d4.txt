Award/Honour: D'source Corona Design Challenge award

Host Organization: IDC school of design IIT Bombay, MHRD

Brief Description of the Award/Honour Mr. Siddhant Shrivastava was selected as the
winner in D'Source Corona Design Challenge for the Product Design Category for the
product "Design of Modular Portable Isolation Facility for COVID-19". D'Source Corona
Design Challenge (DCDC) is an initiative for opportunities where design can make a
difference. It was a platform for seeking creative, innovative, out-of-the-box solutions
for the current scenario of COVID-19. It was an international call and entries from all
over the world. It was jointly organized by IDC school of design IIT Bombay, MHRD,
World Design organization, and other respective organizations.

Date: February 25, 2021
