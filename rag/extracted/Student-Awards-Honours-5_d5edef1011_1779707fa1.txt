Student Awards and Honours

Student’s Name(s): Mr. Debapratim Dhara & Ms. Gaurangi Gupta

Programme and Department: PhD/EE

Thesis Supervisor/Mentor: Prof. A.R. Harish

Award/Honour: 2018 IEEE Indian Conference on Antenna and Propagation(InCAP-2018)

Host Organization: 2018 IEEE Indian Conference held at Hyderabad

Brief Description of the Award/Honour: Our paper "A Wideband Polarization Diversity 3D
MIMO Antenna" authored by Debapratim Dhara, Gaurangi Gupta and A.R. Harish has been
awarded the "Dr Sudhakar Rao Best Student Paper Award" for Mr Debapratim Dhara and the "Dr
Sudhakar Rao Best Female Student Paper Award" for Ms Gaurangi Gupta at the 2018 IEEE Indian
Conference on Antenna and Propagation (InCAP-2018) held  at Hyderabad on 16th  -19th
December 2018. The awards included plaques for the authors and a cheque of Rs.10,000.

Date: December 28, 2018
