PG/ADMIS/9
Undertaking by D.IIT./M.Tech./Ph.D. Candidates

Academic Year:                        Semester:

I do hereby solemnly undertake to submit the following documents in the Academic
Section (PG Office) within 8 weeks from the date of registration on my admission to
D.IIT../M.Tech./Ph.D. programme to the Superintendent/Dealing Assistant.

I.      Attested copy of the High School/Secondary Examination indicating the date of
birth (being the only valid proof of age.

II.     Attested   copy    of   my     final    qualifying    examination    certificate:
B.Tech./M.Tech./M.Sc./M.A.  (in  case  of  diploma  not  already  received  a
provisional/transfer  certificate from  the  University/Institute  is  required  to be
submitted).

III.   GATE Score with Admit Card/CSIR/UGC fellowship examination held on

along with a copy. I also solemnly affirm that I have not used the score earlier for
admission any where else and got admission.

IV.    Medical certificate.

In case of my failure in submitting any of the above documents, I agree not to release my
scholarship till their submission.

I also know the seriousness and the implications of non-submission of these documents by
the stipulated date (i.e., within 8 weeks from the date of registration) may culminate even in
termination of my academic programme at I.I.T. Kanpur.
